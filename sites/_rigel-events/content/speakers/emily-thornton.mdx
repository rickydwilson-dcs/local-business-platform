Emily Thornton is an independent email marketing consultant who works primarily with service businesses — from plumbers and landscapers to consultants and freelancers. Her focus is on practical email strategy: how to build a list from scratch, how to segment it effectively, and how to write emails that people actually read and act on. She has worked with clients who started with zero subscribers and grown their lists into reliable, revenue-generating assets within months.

Emily is a strong advocate for email marketing in an era when many marketers have shifted their attention entirely to social media. Her argument is straightforward: email is the only marketing channel where you own the audience outright. Algorithms can suppress your social reach overnight, but your email list remains yours. She teaches businesses how to set up simple automation sequences, lead magnets that attract the right subscribers, and email cadences that nurture without annoying.

Saturday afternoon at Digital Marketing Weekend, Emily will lead an interactive workshop session in the Workshop Room. It will be practical, hands-on, and structured around the specific challenges that service businesses face: what to write about when you're not a content person, how to get people to sign up, and how to turn a list of email addresses into a steady stream of referrals and repeat business. Attendees will leave with templates and a 90-day email plan they can start using the following week.
