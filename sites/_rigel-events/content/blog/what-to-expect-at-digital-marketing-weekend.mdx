Digital Marketing Weekend is a free two-day event for small business owners, marketers, and freelancers held at the Winter Garden in Eastbourne on 17–18 October 2026. It is not a conference in the traditional sense: there are no keynote speakers selling software, no lead-generation booths, and no sessions that exist to pitch something. Every speaker is a practitioner sharing what actually works in their field, and every session is structured around practical takeaways you can use the week after the event.

Saturday opens with registration at 9:00am and a keynote from Ricky Wilson on the minimal marketing stack that delivers results for small businesses. The morning continues with Sarah Chen's local SEO session on the Main Stage, covering what has changed in local search and what still works. The afternoon splits between a social media session on the Main Stage and Emily Thornton's email marketing workshop in the Workshop Room — a hands-on session where you will leave with templates and a 90-day plan. The day closes with a panel discussion on marketing on a shoestring budget, followed by networking drinks on the terrace. Sunday brings Marcus Okafor's paid advertising session in the morning, James Hartley's AI tools demonstration before lunch, and afternoon workshops including a live session on building your 90-day marketing plan. The weekend closes with a networking session at 4:30pm.

What makes this event different is the format. Sessions are kept short and focused. Speakers are chosen for their ability to explain things clearly, not for their social media follower count. There are no ticket fees, no upsells, and no sales pitches from the stage. The goal is simple: you leave with more practical knowledge than you arrived with, and a clear sense of what to do next for your business.

Getting there is straightforward. The Winter Garden is a ten-minute walk from Eastbourne railway station along the seafront. There are several car parks nearby including the Wish Tower and Central car parks, both within five minutes of the venue. Doors open at 9:00am on Saturday. Bring a notebook, comfortable shoes, and an appetite for practical knowledge. We will see you there.
