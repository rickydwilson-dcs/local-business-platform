Australian rider Brayden Elliott is returning to the ZYN British Superbike Championship with NP Racing at round three, Knockhill, on June 19-21, 2026 — his first Superbike outing in nine months.

Elliott spent the intervening period racing in the Pirelli National Superstock class, where his form on Honda machinery earned him this opportunity to step back up to the premier category. He completed a test at Donington Park with the team ahead of the Knockhill round.

Speaking about the return, Elliott said he was "very excited to be back on a Superbike," while acknowledging the scale of the task ahead: he is "under no illusion as to how difficult this challenge is."

NP Racing team owner Neil Pearson echoed that note of realism, calling Knockhill "a tough first round" for the pairing while welcoming the team's return to the BSB grid.
