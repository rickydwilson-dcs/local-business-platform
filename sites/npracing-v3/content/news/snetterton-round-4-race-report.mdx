Round 4 of the ZYN British Superbike Championship at Snetterton was a tough weekend, but the team leaves with heads held high and real progress to build on for the rest of the season.

Friday brought a gearbox issue and a failed hose clip, cutting into track time — an engine strip rectified both faults ahead of Saturday. Saturday started well, with the bike settling in and time coming off each session. The team fought hard for a 15th-place finish in Race 1 — the first point of the 2026 season.

Overnight the crew found something in the setup, and Sunday warm-up saw the pace step on again. The sprint race started strong with a battle to hold 13th, before a fuelling issue forced a last-lap retirement. Race 3 then ended early with a cracked exhaust.

A good weekend showing real potential. We can't get annoyed about things out of our control — next up: Brands Hatch in a couple of weeks.
