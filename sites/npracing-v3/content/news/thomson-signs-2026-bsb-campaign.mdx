NP Racing has signed 23-year-old Connor Thomson, from Surrey, for a full-time debut in the 2026 ZYN British Superbike Championship.

Thomson previously raced in the Pirelli National Superstock Championship and made his first appearance with NP Racing as a wildcard entry at the 2024 Brands Hatch season finale — a run that put him on the team's radar for a full-season seat.

"Getting to be on the Superbike grid for my debut season in BSB is a moment I've worked towards for so many years," Thomson said of the signing.

Team owner Neil Pearson pointed to that 2024 wildcard as the deciding factor: "When Connor wildcarded for the team in 2024, he showed grit and determination on the mammoth task he undertook."
