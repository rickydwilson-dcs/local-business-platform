Brands Hatch Round 5 of the ZYN British Superbike Championship did not disappoint for the NP Racing crew.

Friday and Saturday saw the team try various setups, but Brayden Elliott was struggling to find the comfort to push. A big overnight change to the setup paid off, unlocking a strong sprint race.

Race 3 didn't go to plan — running P18, and with Brayden bettering his personal best at Brands Hatch, he tipped off at Stirlings and the weekend ended there.

Big thanks to all the marshals and medics who looked after Brayden — the team can't race without them — and to Tim Gibson of Tidan Ltd for his help this round.

Eyes down to keep improving at Oulton Park in a couple of weeks.
