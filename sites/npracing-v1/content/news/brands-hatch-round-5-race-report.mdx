Brands Hatch Round 5 of the ZYN British Superbike Championship did not disappoint for the NP Racing crew.

Friday and Saturday saw the team try various setups and ideas, but Brayden Elliott was struggling to find the comfort to really push. Overnight, a big call on set-up paid off — the team found something that let Brayden push hard, and it showed in a good sprint race.

Race 3 didn't go to plan. While running P18, and with Brayden bettering his personal best at Brands Hatch, he tipped off at Stirlings and the weekend ended there.

Big thanks to all the marshals and medics for looking after Brayden — we can't race without you. And a big thanks to Tim Gibson of Tidan Ltd for helping the team this round.

Eyes down to keep improving at Oulton Park in a couple of weeks.
