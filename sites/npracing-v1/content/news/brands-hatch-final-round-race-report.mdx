So that's a wrap on the 2026 British Superbike Championship.

There were plenty of challenges through the year, but we finished on a massive high at Brands Hatch.

We started the season with new faces in the team. Those faces are now a race family, and that's the thing we're proudest of.

On track, we hit our personal best targets at every round bar one. On a personal level, I'm happy to say it's been the best year I've ever had.

Thank you to everyone who has been part of it: the crew, the riders, our partners and sponsors, and everyone who has followed along and cheered us on.

Let's see what 2027 holds.
