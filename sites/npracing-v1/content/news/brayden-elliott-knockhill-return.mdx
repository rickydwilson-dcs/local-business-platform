Brayden Elliott is back on a Superbike grid. NP Racing has confirmed the rider will line up for round three of the ZYN British Superbike Championship at Knockhill (19–21 June 2026), returning to the premier class after a season spent racing in the Pirelli National Superstock championship. The move follows a test session with the team at Donington Park, where Elliott got his first extended run on the squad's machinery ahead of the step back up.

For Elliott, the return marks the achievement of a longer-term goal. "I'm very excited to be back on a Superbike," he said. "Competing in the premier category is always the goal and one that I've been working towards."

Team boss Neil Pearson welcomed the rider back into the NP Racing fold, pointing to the progress already made in testing. "We are excited to be returning to the BSB grid at Knockhill with Brayden," Pearson said. "He has already settled in to the team following our recent test."

Elliott's first weekend back in the Superbike class saw him cross the line P16 in Race 2 at Knockhill — a solid marker for a rider still finding his feet on the bike after a year away from the category (see the [full Knockhill race results on Crash.net](https://www.crash.net/bsb/results/1099209/1/2026-british-superbikes-knockhill-race-results-2)).
