NP Racing have confirmed that Australian Brayden Elliott will return to the team for the 2027 ZYN British Superbike Championship, with a change of manufacturer announcement to follow in due course.

NP Racing and Elliott has been a strong partnership this season and have continued to gain momentum, with points-scoring finishes and pushing to narrow the gap to their rivals to deliver the team's strongest season to date. Now the team and Elliott are targeting further steps forward in 2027 in the new era of the championship.

## Brayden Elliott

> "Firstly, I want to say a huge thank you to Neil and the entire NP Racing team. The opportunity they have given me throughout 2026, and now looking ahead to 2027, is something I'm incredibly grateful for. I feel very much at home with NP Racing, and I believe the team environment brings out the best in me as a rider.
>
> "It's great to have everything secured so early, allowing me to put all of my focus into preparing for next season. I'm incredibly excited about what is coming next with the team. I believe the package will give both myself and NP Racing the opportunity to take another step forward and continue building on the progress we've made."

## Neil Pearson, team owner

> "The 2026 BSB season turned out to be the best year that we have ever had as a team. After a late start with Brayden, we set out to build something; we set goals of bettering his PBs at each track and together we smashed that.
>
> "We have built something solid to move into the 2027 season and I'm delighted to continue with Brayden as I think we can aim for more again next year. With the new technical rules we will also be changing manufacturer with the addition of new partners for the season, and we look forward to sharing the details in due course."
