## Our story

NPRacing has been part of the British Superbike paddock since 2004, built
from the ground up as a privately run, Honda-mounted operation based in
Taunton, Somerset. What started as a smaller presence in the championship
grew steadily over more than a decade, and in 2020 the team took the step
up into full-time competition in BSB's premier Superbike class — the top
tier of British motorcycle road racing.

## From workshop to paddock

The team is led by Neil Pearson, founder of NP Motorcycles. Neil built NP
Motorcycles as a motorcycle servicing and workshop business, and over time
grew it into one of the more recognised independent names in British
Superbike racing. That workshop background still shows in how the team
operates today: technically grounded, hands-on, and focused on getting the
detail right on every bike that goes out on track.

## A family-style team that competes with the big names

NPRacing doesn't have the budget of the championship's factory-backed
squads — teams like Honda Racing UK, Ducati PBM, or McAMS Yamaha — but it
has built a reputation as one of BSB's respected independent outfits. That
reputation rests on a few consistent things: professionally prepared Honda
Fireblades, a close-knit and experienced technical crew, and a genuine
focus on developing riders rather than just fielding them. It's a
family-style operation in the paddock, and it's earned the team a name for
consistently punching above its weight against much larger, better-funded
rivals.

## 2026 season: Brayden Elliott signs on

For the 2026 season — raced as the ZYN British Superbike Championship —
NPRacing continued that tradition of developing talent. Brayden Elliott
joined the team and returned to the BSB grid with NPRacing from the
Knockhill round in June 2026, riding under race number 51 on the team's
Honda machinery.
