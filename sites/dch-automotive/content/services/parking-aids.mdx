  - question: What are flush-fit parking sensors?
    answer: >-
      Flush-fit sensors are recessed into the bumper so they sit level with
      the surrounding bodywork, rather than sitting proud like standard
      surface-mounted sensors — giving a factory-fitted look.
  - question: Can parking sensors be fitted to any vehicle?
    answer: >-
      Yes, we fit parking sensors to cars, vans and motorhomes. Larger
      vehicles like motorhomes benefit particularly from rear sensors given
      restricted visibility.

    answer: >-
      Yes — sensors can be colour-coded to match your vehicle's paintwork for

      front-end visibility is limited.
