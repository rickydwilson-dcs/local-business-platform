DCH Automotive fits vehicle trackers, immobilisers and physical security devices across cars, vans, motorhomes, plant machinery and motorbikes. All fitting is carried out by City & Guilds and IMI accredited installers, using Thatcham-approved products recognised by UK insurers.

## Vehicle Trackers

GPS-based tracking systems that locate your vehicle in the event of theft, with 24/7 monitoring and recovery support. We fit Smartrack trackers, chosen for their insurer recognition and reliable recovery network.

- Real-time location tracking via app
- Insurance-approved, Thatcham-recognised installations
- Fitting certificate provided for your insurer
- Suitable for cars, vans and motorhomes

## Plant Machinery Security

Excavators, dumpers, generators and other plant machinery are common theft targets on unattended sites. We fit rugged, weatherproof tracking and security devices built for the demands of construction and groundworks equipment.

- Weatherproof trackers designed for plant and yellow goods
- Site-to-site monitoring for hire fleets and contractors
- Reduces insurance premiums on plant cover

## Immobilisers

We fit the **Autowatch Ghost 2**, a CAN-bus immobiliser that disables the vehicle's ignition, starter or fuel system until a unique PIN code is entered on the vehicle's own buttons. Because it has no keyfob, LED or siren, there's nothing for a thief to detect, jam or clone.

- No keyfob to lose, clone or jam
- Disarmed via a PIN entered on existing buttons/switches
- Thatcham approved

## Bike Security

Motorbike-specific tracking and physical security, covering the same theft-prevention principles as our car and van security work.

- **BikeTrac** and **BikeTrac LITE** — motorbike-specific GPS trackers
- **SmarTrack Reco**, **SmarTrack Protector Pro Global** and **SmarTrack MotoTrak** — tracking and recovery systems for motorbikes
- **BikeTrac Grab Bag & Chain** and **BikeTrac Ground Anchor** — physical security to keep the bike where you left it
