DCH Automotive supplies and fits the full IROAD dash camera range, from a single-channel front camera through to 4K front-and-rear systems. Every model includes GPS, ADAS lane and collision warnings, and a parking mode with impact/motion detection.

## IROAD FX1 PRO — Single Channel

Front-only 1080p Full HD recording using a Sony STARVIS image sensor for strong low-light performance.

- 1080p Full HD @ 30fps, 140° viewing angle
- Sony STARVIS sensor with Real HDR
- Built-in GPS with speed camera alerts
- WiFi (2.4GHz), parking mode, ADAS
- From £185

## IROAD FX2 PRO — Front & Rear

The two-channel version of the FX1 PRO, adding a rear-facing camera for full front-and-rear coverage.

- 1080p Full HD front and rear @ 30fps
- Sony STARVIS front sensor, Real HDR
- Built-in GPS, WiFi, parking mode, ADAS
- From £215

## IROAD X10 PRO — 4K Front & Rear

Our top-tier system, stepping up to 4K UHD front resolution for the sharpest detail available — including number plates at distance — with a 1080p rear camera.

- 4K UHD front (Sony STARVIS 2, 8.4MP) @ 30fps, 1080p rear
- 160° front / 140° rear viewing angle
- Built-in GPS, 5GHz WiFi, parking mode, ADAS
- Expandable to 3-channel for taxis, trucks and RVs
- From £299.99

## IROAD FHD Rear Channel

A standalone rear-only 1080p camera, for adding rear coverage to an existing front camera or as a simple rear-facing solution.

- 1080p Full HD rear recording
- From £74.99

## What's Included on Every Model

- Built-in GPS with speed and red-light camera alerts

- ADAS lane departure and forward collision warnings
