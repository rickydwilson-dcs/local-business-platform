- Real-time location updates
- Instant movement/motion notifications

## DVS Progressive Safe System

For HGVs required to meet Direct Vision Standard (DVS) compliance, we fit a full progressive safe system:

- **Pictorial Warning Signage** — blind-spot warning stickers mounted at the rear of the vehicle or trailer
- **Blind Spot Information System (BSIS)** — audible and visual warnings of vulnerable road users on the nearside, differentiating stationary from moving objects below 30 km/h
- **Left Turn Warning Speaker** — automatically activated with the left indicator to alert cyclists and pedestrians to a left-turn manoeuvre
- **Nearside Camera System** — camera and in-cab display eliminating nearside blind spots
- **Moving Off Information System (MOIS)** — forward-facing radar detection (0–5 km/h, 180°) warning of vulnerable road users directly ahead
