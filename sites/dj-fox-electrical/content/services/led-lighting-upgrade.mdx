## Benefits of LED Lighting

**Energy Savings:**
- 80-90% less energy than incandescent bulbs
- 50-75% less than traditional halogen
- Dramatic electricity bill reduction
- Typically pay for upgrade within 1-2 years

**Longevity:**
- 25,000-50,000 hour lifespan
- Last 15-25 years typical use
- Rarely need replacement
- Minimal maintenance required

**Light Quality:**
- Excellent colour rendering
- No flickering or hum
- Instant full brightness (no warm-up)
- Available in warm and cool tones
- Better visibility throughout home

**Environmental:**
- Reduced carbon footprint
- Less landfill waste
- No mercury or hazardous materials
- Support net-zero home goals

## Colour Temperature Guide

**Warm White (2700K):**
- Cosy, traditional feel
- Perfect for living rooms and bedrooms
- Similar to incandescent lighting
- Most popular for homes

**Neutral White (4000K):**
- Balanced natural light
- Ideal for kitchens and home offices
- Reduces eye strain
- Good colour recognition

**Cool White (5000K+):**
- Bright, energising light
- Good for tasks requiring precision
- Less commonly used in homes
- Better for workshops
