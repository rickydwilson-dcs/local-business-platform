## Security Lighting Types

**Motion-Sensor Floodlights**
- Powerful 20-50W LED output
- Wide beam coverage of large areas
- PIR sensors with 5-10 minute timer
- Adjustable sensitivity settings
- Ideal for driveways, patios, large gardens

**Spotlight Security Lights**
- Focused beam on specific areas (entry doors, windows)
- 10-20W LED output
- Compact mounting options
- Motion and/or dusk-to-dawn control
- Great for entrances and corner areas

**Eave-Mounted Systems**
- Discrete mounting under eaves
- Multiple lights on single circuit
- Coordinated motion detection
- Protects entire perimeter
- Professional appearance

**Solar-Powered Options**
- No wiring required (easy retrofit)
- Automatic dusk-to-dawn operation
- Limited brightness compared to mains-powered
- Lower installation cost
- Good for secondary lighting areas

## PIR Sensor Settings

Security lighting effectiveness depends on proper sensor configuration:

- **Sensitivity adjustment** - Control how motion triggers lights (avoid false triggers)
- **Activation range** - Typically 5-10 metres, adjustable
- **Duration setting** - How long lights stay on after motion detected (usually 5-15 minutes)
- **Daylight override** - Prevents unnecessary daytime activation
- **Temperature compensation** - Better detection in all weather
