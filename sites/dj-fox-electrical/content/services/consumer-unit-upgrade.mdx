## Why Your Consumer Unit Needs Upgrading

**Old Rewirable Fuses:**
- Don't provide RCD protection (earth fault safety)
- Can be easily bypassed with incorrect fuses
- Don't handle modern electrical demands
- Risk of fires and electrical shocks

**No RCD Protection:**
- Leaves you vulnerable to electrocution
- Doesn't detect earth faults
- Legal requirement under Building Regulations
- Most common reason for upgrades

**Insufficient Circuits:**
- Old consumer units lack circuits for modern appliances
- Causes overloaded circuits
- Leads to repeated tripping
- Indicates need for upgrade

## Modern Consumer Unit Benefits

## Signs You Need a Consumer Unit Upgrade

- Home over 25 years old without recent upgrade
- Rewirable fuses instead of circuit breakers
- Missing RCD protection
- Frequent circuit tripping
- Burning smell from consumer unit
- Blown fuses regularly
- Planning renovations or new circuits
- Adding high-power appliances
