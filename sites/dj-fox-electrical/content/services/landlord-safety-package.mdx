## Legal Requirements for Rental Properties

**Electrical Safety Standards 2020 require:**
- EICR every 5 years maximum
- EICR at new tenancy start
- Remedial work completed on defects found
- Tenants provided copy within 30 days
- Records kept for 7 years

**Fire Safety Standards require:**
- Smoke alarms on each storey
- Carbon monoxide alarm near combustion appliances
- Regular testing of alarms
- Battery replacement annually

**Building Safety Act:**
- Complete safety responsibility of landlord
- Tenant safety paramount
- Non-compliance: serious fines and liability

Professional compliance services essential.

Complete electrical safety verification.
