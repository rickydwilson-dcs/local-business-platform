## IP Ratings Explained

IP ratings indicate weather protection:
- **IP44**: Splash protection from any angle
- **IP54**: Water spray protection, dust resistant
- **IP66**: Heavy water spray and submersion capable

We recommend the appropriate rating based on your location and exposure.

## Safety Standards for Outdoor Circuits

All outdoor installations include:
- Dedicated circuit from consumer unit
- 30mA RCD protection (essential for outdoor safety)
- Proper earthing for shock protection
- Weatherproof cable rated for external use
- Professional installation to Building Regulations
- Regular testing recommendations

## RCD Protection Essential

Outdoor circuits must have RCD protection due to wet environment risks. This automatically cuts power if earth faults detected, preventing electrocution. All our outdoor installations include this essential safety feature.
