## CCTV System Types

**IP Camera Systems (Network-Based)**

Modern IP (Internet Protocol) systems offer:

- High-definition resolution (2MP to 8MP+)
- Remote viewing from smartphone, tablet, or PC
- Cloud backup and storage options
- Flexible placement (wireless or cabled)
- Intelligent features (motion detection, person recognition)
- Scalable (add cameras as needed)

**Analogue DVR Systems (Traditional)**

Traditional systems offer:

- Lower initial cost
- Established, reliable technology
- Local recording storage
- Less bandwidth required
- Good for properties with stable camera placement

We recommend system type based on your property, budget, and monitoring requirements.

## CCTV Installation Process

1. **Site Survey** - We assess your property layout, lighting, power access, and security needs
2. **System Design** - Recommend camera types, placement, and recording capability
3. **Network Planning** - Design cabling routes for power and data
4. **Camera Installation** - Mount cameras with proper weatherproofing and positioning
5. **Cabling Installation** - Run Cat6 (or fiber optic) to cameras and recorder
6. **Power Supply** - Install dedicated circuits for recorder and PoE (Power over Ethernet) systems
7. **Recorder Setup** - Configure DVR/NVR, storage, and recording schedules
8. **Network Integration** - Set up remote viewing via WiFi or network
9. **Testing & Training** - Full system test and user training on operation
10. **Handover** - Provide manuals, passwords, and ongoing support details

## Legal Considerations

When installing CCTV, consider:

- **Privacy Laws** - Ensure cameras don't record neighbours' windows or public highways excessively
- **Data Protection** - GDPR compliance if recording employees or customers
- **Signage** - Visible "CCTV in Operation" signage required
- **Recording Retention** - Commercial systems typically retain 30-90 days footage

We advise on legal compliance during installation.
