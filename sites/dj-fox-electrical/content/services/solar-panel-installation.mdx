## Solar Panel System Components

**Photovoltaic Panels**
- Convert sunlight to DC electricity
- Modern panels highly efficient (20-22%)
- Various mounting options (roof, ground, hybrid)
- Typically 6-16 panels for residential systems
- Quality panels warranty 25-30 years

**Inverter**
- Converts DC power to usable AC electricity
- String inverter (single unit) or microinverters (per panel)
- Hybrid inverters enable battery storage
- Monitoring via app or web interface
- Warranty typically 10 years

**Generation Meter**
- Measures electricity generated
- Tracks renewable energy production
- Data feeds Smart Export Guarantee scheme
- Monitoring alerts for system issues

**Smart Export Guarantee Meter**
- Measures excess electricity exported to grid
- Integrates with energy supplier
- Tracks payment for exported electricity
- Monthly income from surplus generation
- Typical rates: 5-15p per kilowatt-hour

**Battery Storage (Optional)**
- Store excess daytime generation
- Provide evening/night-time power
- Increase self-consumption (60-80% typical)
- Backup power during grid outages
- Tesla Powerwall, LG, or similar systems
- Cost: £5,000-£15,000 depending on capacity

## Smart Export Guarantee Schemes

Export excess electricity and earn income:

**How It Works**
- Solar generates electricity during daytime
- Use power in home (self-consumption)
- Excess power exports to grid
- Energy supplier pays for exported electricity
- Payment credited to your account

**Supplier Options**
- Over 20 suppliers offer competitive rates
- Rates vary (currently 5-15p per kWh typical)
- Fixed rates available (good for planning)
- Variable rates track market
- Compare rates to maximize income

**Annual Income Example**
- 5kW system generating 4,500kWh annually
- 50% self-consumption (2,250kWh used, 2,250kWh exported)
- At 10p/kWh: £225 annual export income
- Battery storage increases self-consumption (higher savings)
