## Lighting for Office Productivity

Research shows proper lighting impacts:

- **Productivity** - correct lighting increases focus and reduces fatigue
- **Mood & Energy** - appropriate colour temperature affects employee wellbeing
- **Safety** - proper illumination prevents accidents
- **Energy Costs** - LED and controls reduce consumption by 40-60%

Our lighting design considers:

- **Task Lighting** - focused light at workstations (300-500 lux)
- **Ambient Lighting** - general office illumination (300-400 lux)
- **Accent Lighting** - highlighting features, wayfinding
- **Daylight Harvesting** - sensors adjust artificial light based on natural daylight

## Office Fit-Out Installation Process

1. **Design Phase** - Work with architects/designers to plan electrical infrastructure
2. **Specifications** - Detailed electrical design drawings and equipment list
3. **Building Regulations** - Submit designs for Building Control approval
4. **Procurement** - Order all electrical equipment and materials
5. **Site Preparation** - Prepare cable routes, equipment rooms, conduit systems
6. **First Fix** - Install all cables, conduits, backboxes, and infrastructure
7. **Building Work** - Coordinate with builders, plasterers, decorators
8. **Second Fix** - Install lights, switches, sockets, equipment
9. **System Integration** - Install control systems, networking, BMS
10. **Testing** - Full electrical testing and verification
11. **Commissioning** - Activate all systems and train occupants
12. **Handover** - Provide documentation, warranties, ongoing support
