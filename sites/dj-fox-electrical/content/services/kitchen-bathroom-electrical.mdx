## Safety Standards for Wet Areas

Kitchens and bathrooms have specific electrical safety requirements under Building Regulations and BS 7671:2018:

- **RCD Protection**: All sockets and circuits must have RCD (Earth Leakage) protection
- **Zone Classification**: Wet areas have restricted zones where sockets cannot be installed
- **IP Ratings**: Appliances and fittings must have appropriate splash protection
- **Cable Routing**: All cables must be protected from moisture and physical damage
- **Bonding**: Metalwork (pipes, radiators) must be bonded for safety
- **Ventilation**: Extraction systems required to prevent condensation and damp

We ensure all work complies fully with current safety standards.
