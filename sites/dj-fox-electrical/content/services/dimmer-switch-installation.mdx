## Dimmer Types

**Leading-Edge Dimmers** (Traditional)
- Suitable for incandescent and halogen bulbs
- Can cause flickering with some LED bulbs
- Inexpensive option
- Smooth dimming control

**Trailing-Edge Dimmers** (Modern)
- Specifically designed for LED bulbs
- No flickering or buzzing
- Works with halogen and incandescent too
- Recommended for LED lighting

**Smart Dimmers**
- WiFi or Bluetooth control
- App control from anywhere
- Voice assistant compatible (Alexa, Google Home)
- Scheduling and automation features
- Premium pricing but maximum flexibility

**Multi-Way Dimmers**
- Control single light from multiple locations
- 3-way or 4-way configurations
- Useful for stairs, hallways, large rooms
- More complex installation

## LED Dimmer Compatibility

Dimming LED bulbs requires the right dimmer:

**Key Considerations**
- LEDs consume less power than incandescent
- Old dimmers may flicker with LEDs
- Modern LED dimmers solve this problem
- Bulb and dimmer must be compatible

**LED Dimmer Requirements**
- Trailing-edge technology (PWM based)
- Minimum load compensation
- Works with wide voltage range
- Compatible bulbs specified

**Bulb Selection**
- Buy "dimmable" LED bulbs specifically
- Non-dimmable LEDs don't work with dimmers
- Check dimmer and bulb manufacturers match
- Quality bulbs reduce flickering

## Common Questions

**Will dimmers work with my LED bulbs?**
Use trailing-edge dimmers with dimmable LED bulbs. Standard dimmers cause flickering.

**Can I add a dimmer to any switch?**
Most switches can be replaced with dimmers. Some circuits have limitations due to load or wiring age.

**Do dimmers save electricity?**
Yes, dimming reduces energy consumption proportionally (30% brightness = 30% power consumption).

**Is dimmer installation DIY friendly?**
No. Improper installation creates fire hazards. Professional installation is essential.

**Do dimmers work with smart home systems?**
Smart dimmers integrate with Alexa, Google Home, and home automation platforms for voice control and scheduling.
