- Flickering lights when switch is used

## Switch Repair vs Replacement

**Repair Usually Possible For:**
- Loose plate tightening
- Contact cleaning and adjustment
- Mechanism lubrication and adjustment
- Loose wire reconnection
- Usually £40-70 for repair

**Replacement Recommended For:**
- Burnt or damaged internal components
- Cracked housing
- Corroded contacts
- Complete non-function
- Safety concerns
- Usually £60-100 per switch

## Safety Warning Signs

Never ignore these switch problems:
- Sparking when toggling switch
- Burning smell from switch
- Switch hot to touch
- Flickering lights indicating loose connection
- Electrical shock sensation
- Smoke or visible damage

These indicate serious electrical faults requiring immediate professional attention.
