## Types of Circuit Faults

**Short Circuit:**
- Live and neutral wires touching
- Causes immediate breaker trip
- Poses fire risk
- Requires cable replacement

**Earth Fault:**
- Live wire touching earth/ground
- Detected by RCD breaker
- Can cause electrocution risk
- Common in wet areas

**Overloaded Circuit:**
- Too much demand for circuit capacity
- Causes breaker tripping under load
- Solution: additional circuit installation
- Permanent fix recommended

**Damaged Cable:**
- Deteriorated insulation
- Physical damage from impact, water, rodents
- Dangerous if not repaired
- Often requires rewiring section

**Loose Connections:**
- Poor connections causing resistance
- Generate heat and can cause fires
- Found at breaker, outlets, appliances
- Need tightening or replacement
