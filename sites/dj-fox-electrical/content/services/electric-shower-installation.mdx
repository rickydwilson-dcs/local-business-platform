## Electrical Requirements

Electric showers require proper electrical infrastructure:

**Circuit Protection**
- Dedicated 40A or 50A circuit (depending on power)
- Double-pole switch for isolation
- RCD protection for user safety
- Proper cable sizing (6mm² or 10mm²)
- Surge protection recommended

**Consumer Unit Capacity**
- Check for spare capacity in main fuseboard
- MCB (Miniature Circuit Breaker) required
- Possible need for consumer unit upgrade
- Professional assessment essential
- Building Regulations compliance

**Wiring Standards**
- Proper cable selection for load
- Heat-resistant sheathing in bathroom
- RCD-protected circuit mandatory
- Isolating switch within reach of shower
- Installation to BS 7671 18th Edition

**Main Supply Adequacy**
- Check 100A supply sufficient
- High-demand showers can strain supply
- May need supply upgrade in rare cases
- Professional assessment determines requirements
- Usually existing supply adequate
