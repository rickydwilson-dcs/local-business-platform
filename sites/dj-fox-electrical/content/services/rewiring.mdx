## Why Rewiring Is Essential

**Old Properties Risk Factors:**
- Rubber or fabric cable insulation (degraded after 50+ years)
- Lack of earth continuity (electrical shock risk)
- No RCD protection (earth fault hazard)
- Insufficient circuits for modern appliances
- Overloaded circuits causing repeated tripping

**When Rewiring Becomes Necessary:**
- Property over 30 years with original wiring
- Planning major renovation or extension
- Adding significant electrical load (kitchen, bathroom, etc.)
- Evidence of problems (flickering lights, burning smells, tripping breakers)
- Buying or selling property with outdated wiring
- Rental property requiring certification

## Our Rewiring Process

1. **Survey and Assessment** - Inspect existing installation
2. **Design** - Plan new circuits based on current needs
3. **Planning Permission** - Ensure compliance with Building Regulations
4. **First Fix** - Install all cabling before plastering
5. **Inspection Point** - Building Control inspection if required
6. **Making Good** - Replaster walls and make good decoration
7. **Second Fix** - Install outlets, switches, consumer unit
8. **Testing** - Comprehensive electrical testing
9. **Certification** - Provide Building Regulation completion certificate
10. **Handover** - Complete explanation and circuit schedule

## Partial vs Full Rewiring

**Full Rewire:**
- Complete property electrical system replaced
- Typical for homes over 50 years old
- Usually during major renovation
- Cost 3,500-6,000 for 3-bed house

**Partial Rewire:**
- Specific rooms or areas upgraded
- Often done room-by-room during renovation
- Typically £500-1,500 per room
- Good option if budget is limited
