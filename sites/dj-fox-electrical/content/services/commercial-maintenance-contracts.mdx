## Types of Commercial Maintenance

**Preventative Maintenance Programs**

Reduce unexpected failures through scheduled inspections:

- **Quarterly Visits** - high-risk properties, mission-critical businesses
- **Bi-Annual Visits** - standard commercial properties, medium-risk environments
- **Annual Visits** - stable, low-risk operations, pre-arranged with seasonal focus

Each visit includes:

- Full electrical system walkthrough
- Testing of distribution boards and circuits
- Inspection of accessible cabling
- Assessment of environmental conditions
- Equipment and fixture condition review
- Detailed written report with recommendations
- Prioritized repair list if issues found

**Reactive Maintenance (Emergency Service)**

Fast response to unexpected electrical problems:

- **Priority Call-Out** - prompt guaranteed response for contract holders
- **Fault Diagnosis** - rapid identification of electrical issues
- **Emergency Repair** - immediate restoration of critical systems
- **Temporary Solutions** - temporary fixes to restore operations while permanent solution planned
- **Follow-Up** - same-day or next-day permanent repair scheduling

**Compliance & Testing Services**

Meet legal obligations for electrical safety:

- **EICR Testing** - Electrical Installation Condition Report (5-yearly requirement)
- **PAT Testing** - Portable Appliance Testing for workplace equipment
- **Fire Safety Testing** - Emergency lighting and fire alarm compliance
- **Gas Detection** - Where applicable (commercial kitchens, industrial facilities)
- **Documentation** - Full certification and compliance records

- **Customer impact** - damaged reputation, lost sales
