Battle is a historic market town with approximately 6,000 residents in the Rother District. Famous as the site of the Battle of Hastings (1066), the town centres around Battle Abbey and features extensive medieval heritage, including the Old Town with narrow streets, historic buildings, and period properties.

The town attracts history enthusiasts, tourists, and residents seeking a charming, heritage-rich community. Many properties are listed buildings or historic structures requiring specialist electrical work.
