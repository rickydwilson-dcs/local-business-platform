## Common Electrical Work in Eastbourne

### Victorian & Edwardian Properties

Many Eastbourne homes feature original Victorian and Edwardian architecture (built 1880s-1920s). These period properties often have outdated electrical installations with rubber insulation, limited earthing, and no RCD protection. We specialise in sympathetically rewiring these properties while maintaining period aesthetics.

### Modern Coastal Homes

Coastal properties need special attention due to salt air corrosion on metal components. We ensure enhanced protection with appropriate cable grades and sealed connections for durability.
