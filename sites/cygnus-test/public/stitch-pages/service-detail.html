Technical Specifications & FAQ

How long does a vehicle wrap last? expand_more
With proper care, our high-performance cast wraps typically last 5 to 7 years. Factors like exposure to intense sunlight and washing methods affect longevity. We use industry-leading 3M and Avery Dennison materials paired with matching UV laminates to maximize lifespan.
Can you wrap any vehicle? expand_more
Virtually any vehicle with a sound factory paint finish can be wrapped. This includes cars, vans, trucks, motorcycles, and even boats. If the paint is peeling or rusted, the vinyl may not adhere correctly, in which case we may recommend a spot-graphic approach instead.
What is the process from quote to completion? expand_more
1. **Discovery:** We discuss your goals and measure your vehicle. 2. **Design:** Our studio creates a 3D-mapped proof. 3. **Production:** Graphics are printed and precision-cut. 4. **Preparation:** The vehicle is decontaminated and cleaned. 5. **Application:** Our certified team applies the vinyl in a climate-controlled environment.
