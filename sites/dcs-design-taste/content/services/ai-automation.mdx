## Why automate now

The cost of running AI-assisted automations has dropped by roughly 90% in two years. Processes that required a full-time employee to manage are now handled by a workflow that costs a few pounds a month to run.

Most businesses we work with have two or three high-volume, low-judgement tasks that are genuinely better handled by a machine. Our job is to identify them and build reliable automations that handle them without breaking.

### What we build

**Lead and CRM workflows** — new enquiries captured from your website, email, or phone automatically create CRM records, send acknowledgement emails, and assign follow-up tasks. No manual data entry.

**Document and invoice automation** — proposals, contracts, and invoices generated from templates and routed for approval. Signed documents stored and tagged automatically.

**Content and scheduling pipelines** — AI-drafted social posts and blog summaries reviewed by your team in a single approval interface, then scheduled and published without touching each platform separately.

**Reporting and analytics delivery** — weekly or monthly reports compiled from GA4, Search Console, CRM data, and ad platforms, formatted, and emailed to stakeholders automatically.

### How we work

We start with a process audit — mapping what you currently do manually and identifying the highest-impact automation targets. From there we scope and price each workflow individually so you can prioritise based on ROI.

Everything is documented, tested, and handed over with training. You own the workflows.
