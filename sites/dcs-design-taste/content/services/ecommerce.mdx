## eCommerce that converts

Most eCommerce problems are not design problems. They're performance problems, checkout friction problems, and search visibility problems. We fix those first, then make the site look right.

### Performance by default

Every eCommerce site we build hits Core Web Vitals targets at launch. That means LCP under 2.5s, CLS under 0.1, and INP under 200ms on a mid-range Android device on a 4G connection. Not on a MacBook Pro on gigabit fibre.

We achieve this through image optimisation, aggressive caching, route prefetching, and keeping the JavaScript bundle small. We don't use bloated page builders or theme marketplaces.

### Search visibility

Product pages that don't rank don't sell. We configure:

- **Product schema** — name, price, availability, review aggregate, and SKU in structured data
- **Breadcrumb schema** — category hierarchy visible in search results
- **Canonical URLs** — correct handling of colour/size variants to avoid duplicate content
- **Sitemap** — dynamically generated, submitted to Search Console on deploy

### Checkout optimisation

We reduce checkout friction by minimising form fields, enabling address autocomplete, supporting Apple Pay and Google Pay, and testing the full purchase journey on real devices before launch.

Abandoned cart recovery workflows are configured during build, not added later as an afterthought.
