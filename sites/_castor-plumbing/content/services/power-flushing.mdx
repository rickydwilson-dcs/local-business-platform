  - question: "How do I know if my system needs a power flush?"
    answer: "Common signs include radiators cold at the bottom, slow warm-up times, noisy boiler or pump, discoloured water when bleeding radiators, or a significant drop in heating efficiency. If you are replacing a boiler, a power flush is always recommended."
  - question: "What is the difference between a power flush and a chemical flush?"
    answer: "A power flush uses high-velocity water flow to physically dislodge and remove sludge, rust, and scale from the system. A chemical flush relies on inhibitor chemicals to break down deposits more slowly. Power flushing is faster and more effective for heavily contaminated systems."
  - question: "How long does a power flush take?"
    answer: "A typical domestic system with 8–12 radiators takes 5–8 hours. Larger systems or those with heavy sludge may take a full day. We will advise on timing during the initial assessment."
  - question: "Should I fit a MagnaClean filter after a power flush?"
    answer: "Yes — we strongly recommend it. A MagnaClean magnetic filter traps iron oxide particles before they accumulate in the system, preventing future sludge build-up. It is a modest additional cost that significantly extends the life of your boiler and radiators."
  - question: "How often should a heating system be power flushed?"
    answer: "Every 5–7 years for a typical domestic system, or whenever symptoms of sludge accumulation appear. Systems with a MagnaClean filter installed may go longer between flushes as the filter removes particles continuously."
