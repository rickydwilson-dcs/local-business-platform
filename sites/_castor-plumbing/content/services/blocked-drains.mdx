  - question: "What is a CCTV drain survey?"
    answer: "A CCTV survey passes a small camera through the drain to inspect its condition from the inside. We can identify blockages, root intrusion, cracks, collapsed sections, and incorrect falls. You receive a recorded report and written diagnosis."
  - question: "How does high-pressure jetting clear a drain?"
    answer: "A high-pressure jetter pumps water at up to 4,000 psi through a flexible hose inserted into the drain. The force breaks up blockages and flushes debris to the nearest inspection chamber. It is safe for most drain materials including clay, concrete, and PVC."
  - question: "Will you need to dig up my garden to fix a drain?"
