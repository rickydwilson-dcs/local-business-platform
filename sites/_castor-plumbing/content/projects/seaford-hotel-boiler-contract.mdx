## The Client

A 22-bedroom seafront hotel in Seaford with a restaurant, bar, and function room required a reliable commercial heating and hot water maintenance partner. Their previous maintenance arrangement had resulted in a boiler failure in February that left the hotel without heating for 36 hours — resulting in guest compensation and reputational damage.

They approached DCS Plumbing for a comprehensive maintenance contract.

## System Overview

The hotel runs on a cascade system of two Ideal Commercial Evomax 80kW boilers, each capable of running the full building independently if the other requires servicing or repair. A 500-litre indirect calorifier provides domestic hot water. This redundancy is essential for a commercial hospitality property — a single-boiler system would mean any service or repair resulted in complete loss of heat.

## Contract Scope

The annual contract covers:

**Planned maintenance:**
- Two boiler services per year (April and October) for each boiler
- Annual calorifier inspection and legionella risk check
- Annual Gas Safe inspection with CP12 certificate
- Flue integrity check and combustion analysis

**Reactive maintenance:**
- Priority emergency callout response target of 4 hours
- Parts availability managed through a pre-agreed spare parts stock held at our Polegate workshop — including heat exchanger, pump, expansion vessel, and ignition components for both boiler models
- Direct out-of-hours phone number for duty manager contact

**Compliance documentation:**
- CP12 gas safety certificate
- Legionella risk assessment summary
- Maintenance records maintained in a compliance folder kept on site

## Year One Results

During the first year of the contract, both boilers were serviced on schedule. One reactive callout was required in November — a failed condensate pump on boiler number two. Boiler two was isolated, boiler one ran the building at full capacity while the repair was completed. Repair time was 3 hours 40 minutes from first contact to return to service.

The hotel reported zero unplanned guest-impacting boiler incidents during the contract year.
