## The Project

A Victorian terraced property in the Cliffe area of Lewes presented a complete bathroom restoration project. The owners had purchased the property in poor condition with a bathroom that had been poorly modernised in the 1980s — suspended ceiling tiles, plastic laminate panels, and undersized copper pipework that was struggling to deliver adequate flow pressure.

Their brief was clear: restore the room to something appropriate for the property's character while installing modern plumbing that would function reliably. They had sourced a reclaimed Victorian freestanding bath and wished to keep it.

## Survey and Planning

The survey identified several complications that shaped the project plan.

The original cast iron soil stack was intact but showed signs of cracking at one joint — it would need a replacement section before any further work. The floor joists, while structural, required sistering (doubling up) in the area below the bath location to carry the additional load of a cast iron bath filled with water. The existing pipework was 15mm copper throughout — undersized for a modern bathroom serving a walk-in shower and bath simultaneously.

Local planning advice confirmed that no consent was needed for the internal works, though the party wall situation with the adjoining property required a standard party wall notice.

## Installation

**Week 1:** Soil stack repair, joist sistering, and stripping the 1980s fit-out completely. The original encaustic floor tiles were uncovered intact beneath the lino and carefully lifted, labelled, and stored off-site.

**Week 2:** New 22mm hot and cold supply runs from the main rising main, concealed within the floor void. New drainage with properly graded runs to the repaired soil stack. First fix plumbing complete.

**Week 3:** New concealed-cistern WC with traditional-style high-level flush pipe. Freestanding bath waste and overflow fitted. Walk-in shower with thermostatic bar valve and fixed overhead rose.

**Week 4:** Floor tiles relaid by a specialist tile contractor (coordinated alongside the plumbing second fix). The encaustic tiles were cleaned, repointed, and sealed. Original skirting boards reinstated.

**Completion:** The freestanding re-enamelled bath was craned in through the first-floor window (the staircase was too narrow) and fitted to the bath feet and waste in a final session.

## Result

The finished bathroom combined period character with fully modern plumbing. The client reported that flow pressure at the shower was noticeably better than anywhere else in the house — a consequence of properly sized 22mm supply rather than the original 15mm.
