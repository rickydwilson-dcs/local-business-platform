## Socket Repair vs Replacement

**Repair Is Usually Possible For:**
- Loose connections tightening
- Minor contact cleaning
- Faceplate replacement
- Internal connection issues
- Usually £50-80 for repair

**Replacement Required For:**
- Burnt or damaged internal components
- Cracked housing
- Corroded contacts
- Complete non-function
- Safety concerns about integrity
- Usually £80-120 per socket

## Safety Concerns with Faulty Sockets

**Never Ignore:**
- Burning smells (indicates overheating)
- Scorch marks (electrical arcing)
- Excessive heat from socket
- Difficulty inserting plugs
- Power cuts to socket

These indicate serious faults requiring immediate professional attention.
