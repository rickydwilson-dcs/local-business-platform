This S3 arrived in poor condition, and the shell has been rebuilt from there almost entirely by
hand — new wheel tubs, quarter panels and a new valance, all fabricated in house, over 1,000 hours
of metalwork so far. Since then the body has been soda blasted, epoxy primed, levelled, and
painted underneath and in the cockpit. It now awaits reuniting with the freshly restored chassis,
before final prep and paint.

A different car from the finished S3 Continental elsewhere in this record — the two are shown here
before and during, side by side with the raw metalwork, at David's own request, so the depth of
work involved is plain to see.
