All new panels made by hand, in house — over 1,000 hours of metalwork so far. The chassis is being
rebuilt now, ahead of mating it back to the body for final prep and paint.

A different car from the finished S3 Continental elsewhere in this record — the two are shown here
before and during, side by side with the raw metalwork, at David's own request, so the depth of
work involved is plain to see.
