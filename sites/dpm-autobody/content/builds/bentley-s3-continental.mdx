A finished restoration, documented on film by a professional crew — the film is below. This is
chassis BC66 XA, restored in 2021.

The rebuild took the body back to bare metal, including hand-crafted lower-body panels — this
model was coachbuilt, and no replacement panels exist, so the new metal was shaped and fitted by
hand rather than bolted on from stock. The panels were blocked back through six grades before five
coats of lacquer went on, all to a consistent 4mm gap around the car. The chrome was redone, the
interior recolonised throughout, and the hood retrimmed by SM Trimming.

The finished car was showcased at the NEC in 2023.

A second Bentley S3, a different car and a different year, is in the workshop now: see its own
record elsewhere in this register, shown alongside the raw metalwork.
