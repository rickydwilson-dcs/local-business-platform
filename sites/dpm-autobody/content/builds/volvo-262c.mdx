A rare 262C, restored in-house for a repeat client. The original roof had suffered vinyl-roof
moisture-trap damage, so a donor vehicle was sourced from the USA to supply a replacement roof and
further parts.

The engine was rebuilt in-house, its components zinc-plated, with the fuel injection unit
refurbished off-site. Simon led the finessing work that brought it all together. The car is fully
rebuilt and now going through shakedown tests.
