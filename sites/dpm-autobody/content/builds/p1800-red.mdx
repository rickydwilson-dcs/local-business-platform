A full body restoration carried out by DPM — mechanical and trim work was done separately, by
another firm. Over 1,300 hours went into the body and paint alone, taking the shell back to bare
metal before the panels seen here went back on.

The car arrived at DPM as a stripped shell, in one of the poorest conditions the workshop has
taken on. Every exterior panel came off for blasting, and the inner structure — floors, chassis
rails, inner wings, scuttle and boot floor — was fabricated from scratch before paint. The
underside was finished in the factory's black stone-chip texture. Once DPM's own bodywork and
paint were complete, the mechanical rebuild and interior refit were carried out by another shop.
The whole restoration was documented on film by JPM Productions.
