Crashed at La Carrera Panamericana in 2022. DPM repaired the body, including a new door fabricated
in house, then finished the car in pink at the owner's request — to stand out at the next race. It
hasn't raced since, and is still due to.

The record here runs from the damage, through bare metal and masking, to the finished colour it
left DPM in. The crash photos show the car in its original livery, ahead of the new design it will
carry once it races again.

It went on to make the front page of *The Argus*, and a radio interview besides. The finished car
has also stood on DPM's own stand at the NEC.
