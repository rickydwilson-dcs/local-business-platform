## The Client

An Uckfield manufacturer of specialist industrial components had secured a stand at a national sector trade show — their first appearance at this event after two years of targeting it for lead generation. The stand allocation was a 3m × 2m shell scheme.

They had previously exhibited at regional events with basic equipment, but this national show carried more significance for the businesses they were trying to reach. They wanted a graphics package that looked as credible as the larger competitors they would be standing alongside.

## Brief and Timeline

The brief arrived with 10 working days to delivery — tight, but achievable for a digital print project.

The package required:
- A 3m × 2m curved pop-up display as the primary background
- Two pull-up retractable banners for the sides of the stand
- A tabletop display system for brochures and product samples
- 500 copies of a 4-page A4 capability brochure

The client supplied product photography — some of it recent high-quality studio shots, some of it older catalogue images at lower resolution. The low-resolution images required either replacement or careful treatment to be usable at large-format sizes.

## Design and Artwork

The primary pop-up display was centred on the strongest product photography — a studio shot of a component array that the client confirmed as current and high-resolution. This image ran as a full-bleed background at the correct print resolution.

The lower-resolution images were used on the pull-up banners at a smaller scale where their resolution was adequate. One product photograph was unusable at any significant size — the client was advised, agreed, and supplied a replacement within 24 hours.

The capability brochure design was a separate process — a 4-page A4 saddle-stitched booklet with product specifications, company overview, and contact information. Artwork was produced in InDesign and supplied as PDF/X-1a.

## Production

- Pop-up display: dye-sublimation fabric print onto the client's existing pop-up frame (confirmed as a compatible system)
- Pull-up banners: 1500mm × 2000mm digitally printed vinyl in retractable banner cassettes
- Tabletop display: foamex panel system with angled shelves
- Brochures: digital print, saddle-stitched, delivered to the client's Bellbrook unit

All items delivered 8 working days after brief, two days inside the required deadline.

## Result

The client attended the trade show and reported that the stand graphics prompted positive comment from several attendees on the quality of presentation. They returned three qualified leads that were in active commercial discussion at the time of writing.
