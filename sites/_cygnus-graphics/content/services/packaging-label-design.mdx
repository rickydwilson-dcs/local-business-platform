  - question: "What is a die-line?"
    answer: "A die-line is a flat, unfolded template that shows the exact cut, fold, and score lines for a piece of packaging before it is assembled. Designing to a die-line ensures your artwork fits the finished box or label precisely. We create or source the correct die-line for your packaging format before any artwork begins."
  - question: "Can you print small runs of labels?"
    answer: "Yes. Digital label printing supports short runs economically — from as few as 50 labels. This makes it practical to test a new product, run seasonal variants, or cater to small production batches without committing to offset quantities of thousands."
  - question: "What is the difference between waterproof and standard labels?"
    answer: "Standard paper labels are suitable for dry, indoor products. Waterproof labels use a polypropylene or polyester substrate with waterproof adhesive and are rated for moisture, condensation, and outdoor exposure. UV-rated versions also resist fading in direct sunlight — important for products displayed outdoors or with long shelf lives."
  - question: "Can you design packaging to match my existing brand?"
    answer: "Yes. Provide your brand guidelines or existing brand assets and we will design packaging that uses the correct colours, typography, and brand elements. Consistency across packaging and your other brand materials is a core part of what we deliver."
  - question: "What file formats do you supply for print-ready artwork?"
    answer: "We supply print-ready PDF files with correct bleed, trim marks, and colour profiles. Where a printer requires specific formats (EPS, AI, TIFF), we provide accordingly. We also supply layered working files on request."
