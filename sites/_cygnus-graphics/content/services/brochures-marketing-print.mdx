  - question: "What is the difference between saddle-stitch and perfect binding?"
    answer: "Saddle-stitching uses staples through the spine — suitable for brochures up to around 56 pages. Perfect binding glues pages into a flat spine — suitable for thicker documents of 48 pages and above. Perfect binding gives a more premium, book-like feel and a printable spine."
  - question: "What paper weight do you recommend for brochures?"
    answer: "Cover stock is typically 300–400gsm with a laminate finish. Text pages are usually 130–170gsm silk or satin. For a luxurious feel, 200gsm uncoated stocks work well. We recommend the appropriate specification based on your brochure's purpose and distribution context."
  - question: "Do you offer eco or recycled paper stocks?"
    answer: "Yes. We offer FSC-certified recycled stocks and can print with vegetable-based inks for clients with sustainability commitments. Eco stocks are available at a modest premium and are often preferred by professional services and environmentally focused businesses."
  - question: "Can you write the copy as well as design the brochure?"
    answer: "We specialise in design, not copywriting. However, we work with trusted copywriters and can coordinate the full project if required. If you provide copy, we will edit and structure it for layout as part of the design process."
  - question: "How many pages does a brochure typically have?"
    answer: "Saddle-stitched brochures must have page counts in multiples of 4. Common sizes are 8, 12, 16, 20, and 24 pages. Perfect-bound documents start at 48–52 pages. We can help structure your content to fit the most cost-effective page count."
