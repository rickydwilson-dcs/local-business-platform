  - question: "What is one-way vision film?"
    answer: "One-way vision film is a perforated vinyl applied to glazing. The perforations allow people inside to see out clearly while the outside presents a printed image or graphic. It is excellent for retail windows where you want privacy or full-colour graphics without blocking light entirely."
  - question: "Can window graphics be removed easily?"
    answer: "Yes — provided quality materials are used and the glass is clean on application. We use only premium removable vinyl on retail windows, ensuring clean removal without residue. Economy vinyls used by some suppliers can leave adhesive or cause glass damage on removal."
  - question: "How often should seasonal window graphics be changed?"
    answer: "Most retailers refresh window graphics 4–6 times per year to align with seasonal campaigns — January sale, spring, summer, back-to-school, Christmas, and so on. We can agree a programme of changes in advance with pre-designed artwork for each season."
  - question: "Can you do frosted or manifestation vinyl?"
    answer: "Yes. Frosted (etched effect) vinyl is frequently used for privacy on glass partitions, meeting room screens, and shop windows. Manifestation vinyl (safety markings at eye and knee height on large expanses of glass) is also available and may be required for compliance."
  - question: "Do you need to visit the site to take measurements?"
    answer: "For complex projects or large windows, a site visit is strongly recommended. For straightforward applications where you can provide accurate measurements and photographs, we can often quote and produce remotely."
