  - question: "What does print-ready artwork mean?"
    answer: "Print-ready artwork meets the printer's technical requirements: correct bleed and crop marks, embedded fonts, CMYK colour mode (not RGB), images at 300dpi minimum, and no overprint issues. Submitting incorrect files delays the job and can result in colour shifts or clipping."
  - question: "Do I need to provide a brief or can you work from rough ideas?"
    answer: "We can work from anything — a detailed brief, rough sketches, a verbal description, or existing brand materials you'd like developed further. The more context you can give us about your audience and the job's purpose, the more effectively we can design for it."
  - question: "Can you match specific Pantone colours?"
    answer: "Yes. We specify Pantone colours at the design stage and convert accurately to CMYK for digital offset print. For spot-colour print runs, we prepare artwork with correct separation and ink density. Pantone matching is included in our standard print design service."
  - question: "What if my artwork needs changes after proofing?"
    answer: "One round of revisions is included in all print design projects. Additional revision rounds are billed at our hourly rate. We always recommend reviewing a physical or digital proof carefully before giving final approval — changes after production begins incur reprinting costs."
  - question: "Can you redesign existing artwork that I have in low resolution?"
    answer: "Yes. We frequently recreate logos and artwork from low-resolution files — particularly from JPEG or PNG versions where the original vector files have been lost. We redraw everything in vector format so it scales correctly for any print size."
