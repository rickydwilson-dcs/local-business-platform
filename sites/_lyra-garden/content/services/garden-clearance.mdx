  - question: "What does a garden clearance include?"
    answer: "A garden clearance removes all vegetation overgrowth, garden waste, discarded items, and debris from the site. This typically includes cutting down overgrown grass and weeds, removing self-seeded shrubs and bramble, clearing fallen branches, and removing any rubbish or fly-tipped material. We leave the site in a condition suitable for the next stage — whether that is replanting, landscaping, or ongoing maintenance."
  - question: "Do you carry a Waste Carriers Licence?"
    answer: "Yes. We hold a current Environment Agency Waste Carriers Licence, allowing us to collect, transport, and dispose of garden waste legally. We can provide our licence number on request. Never use a clearance contractor who cannot produce a licence — fly-tipping of your garden waste could result in a fine against the property owner."
  - question: "Can you clear very overgrown gardens?"
    answer: "Yes — this is one of our specialisms. Gardens that have been neglected for years, with heavy bramble, self-seeded trees, and waist-high grass, require appropriate equipment and a methodical approach. We assess the site before quoting and advise on how many visits and what equipment are needed."
  - question: "Do you handle estate or probate clearances?"
    answer: "Yes. We work with solicitors, estate agents, and families managing probate on properties where the garden has been unmaintained. We understand these can be difficult circumstances and approach the work with appropriate discretion and sensitivity."
  - question: "What happens to the waste you remove?"
    answer: "Garden waste is taken to a licensed waste transfer station for composting and green waste processing. Non-organic waste (rubbish, fly-tipped items) is separated and handled according to waste category. We can provide waste transfer notes if required for your records."
