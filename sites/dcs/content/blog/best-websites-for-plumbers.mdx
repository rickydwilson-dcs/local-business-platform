# What Makes a Great Website for Plumbers?

Of all the trades, plumbing gets some of the most urgent search traffic. A burst pipe, a broken boiler, a blocked drain — people search for a plumber when something has gone wrong, and they need someone fast. That urgency means the business that appears at the top of Google when someone searches "plumber near me" at 7pm on a Tuesday is going to get the call.

A generic website won't cut it. This guide covers what a plumber's website specifically needs to rank locally, establish trust quickly, and turn visitors into customers.

## Gas Safe Registration: Display It Prominently

If you carry out gas work — boiler installations, gas hob fitting, gas safety checks — Gas Safe registration isn't optional. It's a legal requirement. Any plumber doing gas work without Gas Safe registration is operating illegally.

Customers increasingly know this. A homeowner who's been quoted by three plumbers and done a bit of research will check which ones are Gas Safe registered. Your registration needs to be visible on your website — homepage, footer, and any page referencing gas work. Include your registration number. Customers can use it to verify your registration directly on the Gas Safe website, and displaying it openly is a strong trust signal.

If you work with gas and your website doesn't prominently show your Gas Safe registration, you're losing customers to competitors who do.

## Emergency Plumber Positioning

Plumbing emergencies drive a large proportion of high-value enquiries. Burst pipes, boiler breakdowns, no heating in winter, severe leaks — these are situations where customers will pay immediately and don't shop around much. They search, they find someone credible, they call.

If you offer emergency or same-day call-outs, your website needs to say so clearly. This isn't a line in your about page — it's a primary message on your homepage and in your page titles. Things like:

- "Emergency plumber — same-day call-outs available"
- "24/7 emergency plumbing in [your area]"
- "Fast response for boiler breakdowns and burst pipes"

Your phone number should be click-to-call at the top of every page. On mobile, a sticky header with your number and a "Call now" button can make a meaningful difference to how many people actually call versus bouncing to a competitor.

If you don't offer emergency call-outs, that's fine — but make your typical availability clear. Customers searching in an emergency will self-select out; everyone else knows what to expect.

## Boiler Brand Specialisations

If you're particularly experienced with specific boiler brands, say so. Worcester Bosch and Vaillant are the most common mentions — both manufacturers run accreditation schemes (Worcester Bosch Accredited Installer, Advance Installer programmes) that carry their own trust signals and warranty implications.

Being a Worcester Bosch Accredited Installer or Vaillant Advance Installer isn't just a badge — it lets you offer extended warranties on installations, which is a genuine customer benefit. If you're accredited, that should be on your website and factored into how you describe your boiler installation service.

Even without formal accreditation, pages that mention brand expertise ("we install and service Worcester Bosch, Vaillant, and Ideal boilers") pick up brand-specific search traffic and signal expertise to customers who already have a brand preference.

## Landlord Gas Safety Certificates (CP12)

Landlords are legally required to have a Gas Safety Record (CP12) carried out by a Gas Safe engineer every 12 months for each gas appliance in a rented property. It's a recurring revenue stream for plumbers — the same landlord needs the same certificate every year.

A dedicated page for Landlord Gas Safety Certificates is worth having even if it represents a small proportion of your current work. Landlords search specifically for this service, often in advance of their renewal date. A clear page covering what's involved, how long it takes, and pricing (or at minimum a price range) will capture those searches and may bring in clients who then become regular annual customers.

## The Pages That Drive the Most Calls

Beyond gas safety, the service pages that generate the most enquiries for plumbers are:

**Emergency plumber.** A standalone page targeting emergency search terms in your local area. Different from your homepage — specifically written for people in an urgent situation.

**Boiler installation.** High-value, frequently searched. Cover replacement boilers, new installations, the process and timeline, and brands you install.

**Boiler service and repair.** Annual servicing is another recurring revenue stream. A page targeting "[town] boiler service" captures regular maintenance traffic.

**Blocked drains.** High search volume, straightforward job. A dedicated page for drain clearance and blockages will pick up significant traffic.

**Bathroom installation.** If you do bathroom fitting, this warrants its own page. It's a substantial project that customers research carefully.

**Central heating installation.** Particularly relevant for properties converting from electric or where the existing system needs replacing.

## Why Plumbers Get So Much from Local SEO

The intent behind plumbing searches is exceptionally high. Someone searching "plumber Chichester" or "emergency boiler repair Brighton" isn't browsing — they have a problem and they need it solved. The conversion rate from search visit to enquiry for plumbing is among the highest of any trade.

This is why local SEO investment pays off quickly for plumbers. Location pages targeting each town in your service area — "plumber in [town]", "boiler installation [town]" — capture high-intent searches from people who are already looking for exactly what you offer.

A plumber covering a five-town radius with a well-structured website can genuinely dominate local search in each of those areas. Most competitors either have no website or a poor one. The bar to clear is lower than it might seem.

## Photos That Actually Work

Good photography makes a real difference to whether customers trust you from a website visit. For plumbers, the content that works best:

**Before and after shots.** Particularly for larger jobs — bathroom installations, boiler replacements, new pipework. The transformation is visible and tangible.

**Boiler installations.** Clean, neat installations photographed in good light show workmanship. Customers comparing quotes will notice the difference between a tidy install and a messy one.

**Bathroom refits.** If you do these, portfolio photos are one of the most effective trust-builders on a plumbing website.

**Your van and equipment.** Sounds basic, but a photo of your branded van outside a job signals that you're a genuine, operating business.

You don't need a professional photographer — a modern smartphone in good light is enough for job photos. The key is taking them consistently on every job.
