## What Local SEO Actually Means

Local SEO is not magic. It's not a monthly retainer where someone does mysterious things to your website and sends you a report full of graphs. For most tradespeople, it comes down to a few straightforward things done properly.

When someone searches "boiler service in Lewes" or "roofer near me", Google tries to show the most relevant, trustworthy local businesses. It looks at your website, your Google Business Profile, and how many people have reviewed you. The sites that show up have usually done three things right: their website has the right content, it's technically sound, and they've accumulated some reviews.

We take care of the website side. The reviews part is down to you — but it's not hard.

## What We Build Into Every DCS Website

### Service Pages

A single page that says "we do plumbing" doesn't give Google much to work with. We create a dedicated page for each of your main services — boiler installation, central heating repairs, emergency call-outs — each written clearly, targeting the search terms your customers actually use. More specific pages mean more chances to appear in search results.

### Location Pages

If you serve five towns, you need five location pages. Each one is specific to that area — mentioning the town, nearby areas, and the services you offer there. This is how you show up when someone in Bexhill searches for a tradesperson, not just someone in your home town.

### Schema Markup

We add structured data to your site that tells Google you're a real local business — your name, address, phone number, the services you offer, and your coverage area. Google uses this to populate local search panels and sometimes to show star ratings directly in search results.

### Google Business Profile Guidance

Your Google Business Profile (the listing that appears on Google Maps and in the local results panel) is separate from your website, but the two work together. We walk you through setting it up correctly or improving it if you already have one — the right categories, service areas, photos, and how to get that first batch of reviews.

### Sitemap Submission

Once your site is live, we submit a sitemap to Google Search Console so Google finds and indexes your pages quickly. We also connect your site to Search Console so we can spot any crawl issues early.
