## Smart Home Wiring Components

A comprehensive smart home installation includes:

**Network Infrastructure**
- Cat6 cabling runs to key areas (living room, kitchen, bedrooms)
- Strategic WiFi access point placement
- Network switch installation
- Redundant pathways for future expansion

**Automation Circuits**
- Dedicated circuits for smart lighting systems
- Smart thermostat power and data lines
- Security system wiring and control points
- Smart speaker placement and power

**Physical Infrastructure**
- Conduit runs for future cable additions
- Proper earthing and surge protection
- Distribution points in loft and under floors
- Cable trays and management systems

## Design Considerations

**WiFi Coverage**
- Structural assessment for WiFi dead zones
- Access point placement for optimal coverage
- Optional: wired backhaul for performance
- Considerations for external coverage (garden)

**Network Performance**
- Cat6 wiring ensures future gigabit capability
- Proper grounding prevents interference
- Surge protection for valuable equipment
- Network switch sizing for device count

**Future Expansion**
- Spare conduits for additional cables
- Extra outlets in key locations
- Over-sized consumer unit for growing demand
- Labelled cable runs for easy identification
