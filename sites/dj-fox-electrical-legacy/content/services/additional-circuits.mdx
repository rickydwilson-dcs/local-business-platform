## Common Issues We Solve

When do you need additional circuits? Common scenarios include:

- **New high-power appliances**: Electric cookers, ovens, showers, or heat pumps require dedicated circuits
- **Overloaded circuits**: Lights flickering, circuit breakers tripping repeatedly, or sockets feeling warm to touch
- **Home office setup**: Running multiple computers, servers, and monitors safely
- **Kitchen renovations**: Modern kitchens demand more power than older wiring can provide
- **EV charger installation**: Electric vehicle charging points need their own dedicated circuit
- **Hot tubs or pool equipment**: Outdoor electrical features require proper circuit design
- **Industrial equipment**: Workshops and commercial spaces need multiple circuits for machinery

## The Circuit Installation Process

1. **Initial Survey** - We assess your existing installation, consumer unit capacity, and power requirements
2. **Circuit Design** - We calculate appropriate circuit breaker ratings and cable sizes for your appliances
3. **Installation** - New cable is run from the consumer unit to the desired location, with proper protection and routing
4. **Testing** - All circuits are tested with specialist equipment to ensure safe operation
5. **Certification** - You receive a Minor Electrical Installation Works Certificate for compliance and insurance purposes

## Building Regulations & Compliance

All additional circuits are installed to comply with:

- **Part P Building Regulations** (notifiable electrical work in England)
- **BS 7671:2018** (18th Edition Wiring Regulations)
- **IET Guidance Notes** on safe design and installation
- **Electrical Safety Standards 2016** (as amended)

We handle all certification requirements, so you can be confident your installation is legally compliant and insurable.
