- Lights flickering or dimming
- Circuit breakers repeatedly tripping
- Partial power loss to rooms or areas

## Our Fault-Finding Process

1. **Initial Consultation** - Understand symptoms and when they occur
2. **Visual Inspection** - Check visible wiring, outlets, switches, consumer unit
3. **Circuit Testing** - Test individual circuits with professional equipment
4. **Appliance Testing** - Identify if the fault comes from an appliance
5. **Earth Continuity Testing** - Check earthing and continuity
6. **Insulation Testing** - Test for faults in cable insulation
7. **Load Testing** - Test circuits under load to identify intermittent faults
8. **Diagnosis Report** - Explain findings and recommend solutions
