## Built to sell

Our ecommerce solutions are built on the same platform as our business websites — with the addition of product management, Stripe payment processing, and order workflows.

No platform lock-in, no per-transaction fees beyond Stripe's standard rates, and no compromises on performance or SEO.

## Core capabilities

- Product catalogue with variants, pricing, and stock
- Stripe payment processing (cards, Apple Pay, Google Pay)
- Order confirmation emails and fulfilment tracking
- VAT and tax configuration
- Inventory management
- Customer account management (optional)

## Performance and SEO

Every product page is statically generated at build time. This means fast load times and full search engine indexability — critical for organic traffic.

We implement structured product schema so your listings can appear in Google Shopping results.

## Integration

If you use accounting software, inventory management tools, or logistics providers, we can build integration pipelines to reduce manual data transfer and keep your systems in sync.
