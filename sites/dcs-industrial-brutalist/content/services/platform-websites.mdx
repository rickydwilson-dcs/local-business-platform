## The platform difference

Most agencies build websites as bespoke one-off projects. We build on a platform. Every site shares the same foundation — the same performance optimisations, the same structured content architecture, the same deployment pipeline.

The result: faster builds, lower costs, and a site that improves as the platform improves.

## What is included

Every platform website includes:

- Custom visual design aligned to your brand
- Full set of service and location pages
- Schema.org structured data for local SEO
- Automatic sitemap generation
- Mobile-first responsive layouts
- Managed hosting and SSL
- Uptime monitoring

## Who is this for

Platform websites are built for UK service businesses, tradespeople, professional services, and local businesses that need a production-grade online presence without the cost and complexity of custom development.

If you need a website that works — that loads fast, ranks in search, and converts visitors — this is the right starting point.
