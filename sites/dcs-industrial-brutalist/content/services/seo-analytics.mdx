## SEO that lasts

Most SEO advice focuses on tactics that degrade over time. We focus on the fundamentals: technical health, structured data, content quality, and site authority. These compound in value rather than decay.

## Technical SEO

All platform websites include technical SEO as standard:

- Schema.org structured data for local business, services, FAQs, and breadcrumbs
- Automatic XML sitemap generation
- Clean URL structure
- Mobile-first responsive design
- Fast page load times via static generation

## Analytics

We configure GA4 to track what matters to your business: enquiry form submissions, phone number clicks, specific page engagement, and conversion paths.

Out-of-the-box GA4 tracking tracks almost nothing useful by default. Proper configuration is required.

## Reporting

We provide monthly reports in plain language: ranking movements, traffic trends, conversion rates, and recommended actions. No vanity metrics.
