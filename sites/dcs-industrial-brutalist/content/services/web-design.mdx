## Design with intent

Good design is not decoration. It is the structured communication of value and function. We approach design as a system problem: how does every visual element reinforce what the business does and who it serves?

## What we deliver

- **Logo and brand mark** — Vector, in all required formats
- **Colour palette** — Accessible, purposeful, documented
- **Typography system** — Heading and body scales with usage rules
- **Component design** — Buttons, cards, forms, navigation, and page sections
- **Page layouts** — Desktop, tablet, and mobile breakpoints
- **Design system documentation** — Usage guidelines for consistent application

## Our approach

We do not design in isolation. Every design decision is made with the implementation context in mind. Designs that cannot be built faithfully are not useful designs.

Where design work leads to a platform website build, the design system translates directly into the site's theme configuration — no translation or interpretation required.
