## Infrastructure that doesn't keep you up at night

A website is not a one-time project. It is live infrastructure that requires ongoing attention: security patches, platform updates, performance monitoring, and content changes.

Our maintenance service handles this so you don't have to think about it.

## What we monitor

- **Uptime** — Continuous monitoring with immediate alerts on outages
- **Security** — Vulnerability scanning and patch management
- **Performance** — Page speed and Core Web Vitals tracking
- **Certificates** — SSL and domain expiry monitoring

## What we maintain

- Platform and dependency updates
- Content management updates
- Configuration and settings management
- Backup verification

## Support access

All maintenance clients have direct access to our support team. We respond in plain language, without jargon, and we tell you what the problem is and what we are doing to fix it.

No ticket queues. No automated responses for the first three emails.
