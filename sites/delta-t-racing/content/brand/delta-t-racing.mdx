## Who we are

Delta T Racing is a UK-based motorcycle racing team built by people from the
refrigeration industry who share a passion for speed, precision and the thrill
of track competition. It was founded by a small group of dedicated riders and
motorsport enthusiasts, and it's focused on three things: developing talent,
improving performance, and enjoying the spirit of racing.

In refrigeration, ΔT — delta T — is the temperature difference an engineer
works to. On track, it's the gap on the stopwatch. Both come down to the same
thing: measure it, understand it, make it better.

## Where we race

For the 2026 season the team races primarily with Bemsee, the British
Motorcycle Racing Club (BMCRC): nine rounds at six track layouts across five
iconic UK circuits. Our four riders compete across three class championships —
MRO Supertwins, MRO Minitwins and BMCRC Thunderbike 500.

## How we work

The team is just getting started, and every race weekend is an opportunity to
learn, push limits and move forward. Our riders bring together riding skill,
engineering know-how and a shared drive to improve with every lap. Behind the
scenes the team works continuously on bike setup, data and preparation to get
the best possible performance on race day.

## More than competition

Delta T Racing is about the community around motorcycle racing as much as the
racing itself. From supporters and sponsors to friends and fellow racers, we're
proud to be part of the UK club racing scene — and as the team grows, we'll be
sharing race reports, updates and behind-the-scenes moments right here.
