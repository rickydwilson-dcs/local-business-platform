Oulton Park is done. The weekend started perfectly, with ideal weather for
test day — but the race weekend itself was challenging. Conditions were bad
enough that the whole weekend was declared a wet race, which caused track
issues and left some riders unable to finish.

Here's how it went for each of our four riders, in their own words.

## Gene Goodrum — #679

"First time at Oulton Park and what an eye-opener this was — the track is a
serious place to learn. I got on well with it, though, and learning another,
newer SV certainly helped with lap times. The weather was a mixed bag, which
made consistency hard to achieve. Three solid race finishes, and I've climbed
one place in the points to run 6th overall."

## Jared Oakley — #96

"My first meeting of the season, at the Oulton Park circuit in Cheshire. The
practice day went well and I found a good rhythm on my 650cc Kawasaki ER6
Supertwin.

"The first race day was tricky, with wet weather and the realisation that my
little 'Kwaker' was seriously outgunned by the 100hp Aprilias that made up most
of the field. Regardless, I had a good day — despite finishing last! — and felt
positive for the final.

"Thankfully the final brought dry, mild conditions and I was comfortable on the
bike. Starting races knowing I'd finish at or near the back of the grid was
okay — I was having fun! Some banzai braking into the first corner got me past
multiple riders, which felt great, even if the hard work was undone on the
longer straights. Roll on Donington Park!"

## Lance Jordan — #88

"A challenging start to the weekend, with an incident during practice at
Knickerbrook. We rebuilt the bike but missed qualifying, so I started from the
back of the grid. Great day racing on Friday — then on Saturday the weather
turned for the worse, and I had two DNFs with another off into Hislop's."

## Dylan Jordan — #25

"Another new circuit and a lot of learning done. I managed my quickest lap on
test day, which was in amazing conditions. Sadly I had my first off during
qualifying, making it two over the weekend when I made a rookie mistake at
Druids — luckily both crashes were low-consequence. I also finished 11th in
race 1, 4th in class, which is a great achievement for me."
