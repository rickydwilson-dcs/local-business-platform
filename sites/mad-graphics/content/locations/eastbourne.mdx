Eastbourne is the largest town we serve, and one of the busiest for enquiries. From sole traders working the Arndale and town centre to hospitality businesses along the seafront and marina operators at Sovereign Harbour, the variety of work we handle here reflects the breadth of the local economy.

Mad Graphics has been producing and fitting vehicle graphics, signs, and print from our Polegate workshop since 2004. Polegate sits just a few minutes from Eastbourne, which means quick turnarounds and easy drop-offs for local customers.

The trades sector in Eastbourne is strong — builders, plumbers, electricians, and landscapers make up a significant share of our vehicle graphics work. A well-lettered van doing rounds through Meads, Old Town, and Langney is a consistent source of new enquiries for those businesses.

Retail and hospitality clients typically come to us for shop fascia signs, window graphics, banners for seasonal promotions, and marketing print. We also produce workwear and branded merchandise for businesses that want a consistent look across their team as well as their vehicles.

Whatever your sector, the process is the same: we quote clearly, produce everything in-house, and apply to a high finish. No subcontractors, no surprises.
