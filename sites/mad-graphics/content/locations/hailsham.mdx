Hailsham is one of the closest towns to our Polegate workshop — a fact that makes the relationship straightforward for customers. Drop-off is easy, turnarounds are quick, and the journey is short enough that we are a genuinely local option rather than a trip into town.

Hailsham's commercial character is rooted in its market town history. These businesses need vehicle graphics that work on country roads, in farmyards, and on motorways alike: clear, durable, and professionally applied.

The Hailsham Retail Park adds a more conventional retail dimension, and we produce sign and banner work for businesses there. Town centre shops and the market itself represent another strand of demand: banners for market days, window graphics for seasonal promotions, and exterior signs for newly opened businesses.

The trades sector — builders, electricians, heating engineers, landscapers — is well represented in and around Hailsham, and van graphics remain a core product for this group. A van covering the Hailsham and Wealden area is effectively a moving advertisement for every hour it is on the road. We design graphics with that in mind: readable, clear contact details, and a layout that registers in a glance.
