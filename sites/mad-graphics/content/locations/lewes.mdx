## Vehicle Graphics & Signs in Lewes

Lewes is the county town of East Sussex, and its business community reflects that status. The High Street is home to a strong concentration of independent retailers and boutique shops. The professional services sector — legal, financial, and property — is well represented. And as a town with a significant number of historic and listed buildings, there is a particular awareness here of sign sensitivities that does not apply everywhere.

Mad Graphics works with all of these business types. For retailers, we produce window graphics, fascia signs, and display banners that suit the individual character of each premises. For professional services firms, we handle the kind of restrained, brand-consistent vehicle graphics and stationery print that projects the right image to clients.

We produce everything in-house at our Polegate workshop, which is about 8 miles from Lewes town centre. Design, print, cut, and application are all handled by the same team, which means a single point of contact and no handoff errors between suppliers.

The trades and services sector operating in and around Lewes — builders, electricians, plumbers, landscapers — forms another significant part of our work. A well-lettered van working the Lewes area is a reliable source of local enquiries, and we know how to design vehicle graphics that get read at the speeds people actually drive.
