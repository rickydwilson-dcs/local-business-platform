## Vehicle Graphics & Signs in Bexhill-on-Sea

Bexhill-on-Sea sits to the west of Hastings on the East Sussex coast, and its business community has a distinctive character. The De La Warr Pavilion — one of Britain's finest examples of Modernist architecture — anchors a cultural and arts scene that extends to nearby businesses. Seafront hospitality ranges from traditional cafes to contemporary restaurants. The town centre supports a mix of independent and national retail, and there is a light industrial base on the outskirts handling trades and services for the surrounding area.

Mad Graphics works with all of these sectors. For hospitality and retail, we produce exterior signs, window graphics, and promotional banners designed to attract footfall and hold up in coastal conditions. For trades and industrial businesses, we handle vehicle graphics and fleet branding efficiently — a clear process, quick turnaround, and results that keep vehicles looking sharp on the road.

Our Polegate workshop is a short drive from Bexhill, making drop-offs for vehicle work straightforward. All production — design, print, lamination, and application — happens in-house, so there is no outsourcing and no unnecessary delays.

We have been operating from the same workshop since 2004, which means we have a clear understanding of what works in East Sussex's coastal environment — materials that resist salt air, colours that hold against UV exposure, and sign finishes that remain professional through winter weather.
