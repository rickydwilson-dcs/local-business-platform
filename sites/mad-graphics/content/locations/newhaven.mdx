## Vehicle Graphics & Signs in Newhaven

Newhaven's economy is shaped by its port. The cross-Channel ferry terminal brings significant logistics and transport activity, with associated companies operating fleets of vehicles that need consistent, professional branding. The Newhaven Industrial Estate adds a further layer of commercial activity — engineering firms, trade suppliers, warehousing and distribution operations, and marine-adjacent businesses.

This makes Newhaven one of our more fleet-heavy service areas. Transport and logistics companies need vehicle graphics that project a professional image, comply with any operator branding requirements, and hold up to sustained road use including motorway and cross-continental exposure. We use commercial-grade vinyl rated for heavy outdoor use, and laminate all printed work before application as standard.

Beyond fleet graphics, industrial estate businesses come to us for site signs, facility identification, safety signage, and product labels. These are often less visible than vehicle graphics but no less important — a well-signed industrial premises is easier to navigate, easier to find, and presents a more professional image to suppliers and clients arriving on site.

The town centre and local retail also represent a consistent stream of work: shop signs, window graphics, banners, and print for businesses serving Newhaven's residential community. Our Polegate workshop is around 7 miles from Newhaven and straightforward to reach via the A26 and A27.
