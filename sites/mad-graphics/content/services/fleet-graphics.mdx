## Fleet Graphics & Branding

A branded fleet of vehicles is one of the most visible assets a growing East Sussex business can have. When your vans, trucks, and cars carry the same logo, colours, and contact details, every vehicle reinforces the same message — to every person who sees it on the road, on a job site, or parked outside a customer's property.

The challenge with fleets is consistency. Different vehicle types have different panel dimensions and curves. New vehicles get added over time. Old vehicles need re-lettering or removal when they are returned at end of lease. Managing this without a single supplier who holds your artwork and understands your brand leads to visible inconsistency — slightly different shades, different text sizes, different layouts across a fleet that is supposed to look uniform.

Mad Graphics manages fleet branding from our Polegate workshop. We produce a master artwork template for your brand, adapt it for each vehicle type in your fleet, and hold your files for the lifetime of the account. When a new vehicle arrives, matching graphics can be produced and fitted quickly from the existing template without a full redesign.

We work with fleets of any size across East Sussex — from two-vehicle sole traders who want a consistent look to regional companies with dozens of vehicles. Fleet pricing reflects the total job and improves with volume.

All graphics are produced and applied in-house. We schedule work in batches to keep vehicle downtime to a minimum and provide a clear timeline before any vehicles are booked in.

We regularly serve fleet customers from Eastbourne, Hastings, Lewes, Uckfield, Bexhill-on-Sea, Hailsham, Seaford, and across the wider East Sussex area.

## How It Works

### 1. Fleet Audit & Brief
Tell us the vehicles in your fleet — makes, models, colours — and share your brand guidelines or existing artwork. We assess the scope and produce a fleet quote.

### 2. Master Artwork & Approval
We create a master template adapted for each vehicle type and send visuals for sign-off. All vehicles are confirmed before production begins.

### 3. Phased Production & Application
Vehicles are scheduled in batches to limit downtime. Each vehicle is graphics-fitted at our Polegate workshop and ready for return within the agreed turnaround.

### 4. Ongoing Artwork Management
We retain your files. Future additions to the fleet are matched from the existing template without extra design costs.
