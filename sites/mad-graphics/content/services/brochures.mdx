faqs:
  - question: "What's the difference between saddle stitched and perfect bound brochures?"
    answer: "Saddle stitched brochures are folded and stapled through the spine, ideal for smaller page counts (typically 8-64 pages). Perfect bound brochures have a square spine with glued pages, better for thicker publications (typically 32+ pages) and give a more professional book-like appearance."
  - question: "What sizes can you print brochures in?"
    answer: "We offer standard sizes like A4, A5, and DL, plus custom sizes to your exact specifications. Our wide-format printing capabilities allow for unique dimensions that help your brochures stand out from standard formats."
  - question: "Do you provide design services for brochures?"
    answer: "Yes, we have in-house design and artwork services with over 20 years of experience. We can create your brochure from concept to completion, or work with your existing artwork and brand guidelines to produce professional results."
  - question: "How much do brochure printing services cost?"
    answer: "Brochure printing typically ranges from £200-£5,000 depending on quantity, size, page count, and finishing options. We provide detailed quotes based on your specific requirements including paper quality, binding method, and any design work needed."
  - question: "What's the typical turnaround time for brochure printing?"
    answer: "Standard turnaround is 5-10 working days from artwork approval, depending on complexity and quantity. Rush jobs can often be accommodated for urgent deadlines. Design work may add 3-5 days to the overall timeline."
  - question: "What paper options are available for brochures?"
    answer: "We offer various paper weights and finishes including matt, gloss, and silk options. Typical weights range from 130gsm to 350gsm for covers, with text pages usually on 115-170gsm stock. We'll recommend the best option for your budget and intended use."
  - question: "Can you print brochures in full colour?"
    answer: "Yes, all our brochures are printed in full colour using professional digital and litho printing equipment. We ensure consistent colour reproduction and can match specific Pantone colours when required for brand consistency."
  - question: "Do you offer finishing options for brochures?"
    answer: "Yes, we provide various finishing options including lamination for durability, spot UV for highlighting specific areas, and different binding methods. These finishing touches enhance the professional appearance and longevity of your brochures."
  - question: "What file formats do you accept for brochure artwork?"
    answer: "We accept PDF, AI, EPS, and high-resolution JPEG files. PDFs are preferred with all fonts embedded and images at 300dpi minimum. Our design team can also work with your rough concepts if you need complete design services."
  - question: "Can you help with brochure content and copywriting?"
    answer: "While our primary expertise is in design and printing, we can provide guidance on layout and visual presentation. For copywriting services, we can recommend trusted local professionals who work well with our design process."
  - question: "Do you have public liability insurance for commercial printing work?"
    answer: "Yes, we maintain full public liability insurance coverage for all our commercial printing services. This provides peace of mind when working on business-critical projects and ensures professional standards are maintained throughout."
  - question: "How do I get a quote for my brochure printing project?"
    answer: "Contact us with details of your requirements including quantity, size, page count, and any design needs. We'll provide a detailed quote within 24 hours, and can often give indicative pricing over the phone for standard specifications."
