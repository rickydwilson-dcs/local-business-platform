## Brochures & Booklets

A well-produced brochure is one of the most enduring sales tools a business can invest in. In a world of digital communication, a tangible, high-quality printed brochure carries a weight and credibility that an email or web page simply cannot replicate. It gets left on a desk, passed between colleagues, and referred to when a buying decision is being made.

Mad Graphics produces brochures and booklets for businesses and organisations across East Sussex — Eastbourne, Hastings, Lewes, Bexhill-on-Sea, Uckfield, Crowborough, and the surrounding areas. We have been delivering professional print since 2004, and brochure production is one of the services we are proudest of.

We produce two primary binding styles. Saddle-stitched brochures — stapled through the spine — are the standard choice for booklets from 8 to around 60 pages. They lie flat when open, are economical to produce, and suit product catalogues, event programmes, service guides, and company capability documents. Perfect-bound booklets have a glued square spine that gives a book-like appearance — well suited to annual reports, thick product catalogues, and any situation where a more premium format is appropriate.

Cover options include gloss and matt laminate, soft-touch laminate for a tactile premium feel, and spot UV varnish for selective high-gloss highlights. Text pages are available in a range of weights to match the intended use and feel.

Short runs are available for businesses that need a professional brochure without ordering hundreds of copies. We produce runs from 25 copies upward using digital printing, which makes short-run brochures commercially viable for proposal packs, tender submissions, and premium client-facing documents.
