## Folders & Presentation Packs

A presentation folder is the physical container for your most important business communications. When you are submitting a tender, presenting a proposal, welcoming a new client, or attending a pitch meeting, the folder holding your documents is part of the impression you make. A well-designed, properly produced folder says that you take detail seriously — before the contents are even read.

At Mad Graphics, we produce presentation folders for businesses across East Sussex. Our clients include property developers, construction firms, professional services companies, hospitality groups, educational establishments, and commercial businesses in Eastbourne, Hastings, Lewes, Bexhill-on-Sea, Uckfield, and across the region.

Our standard presentation folder is A4 portrait format, printed on 350–400gsm silk board with two internal pockets and at least one business card spine pocket. This format holds standard A4 insert sheets, leaflets, brochures, and price lists securely while presenting them neatly when the folder is opened. The spine pocket keeps a business card visible and accessible, which is important when the folder is passed between contacts within a client organisation.

Cover finish options include gloss laminate, matt laminate, and soft-touch laminate. Soft-touch (velvet) laminate is a popular premium choice for high-value client folders — the tactile quality distinguishes it from standard gloss and signals a higher level of investment in the relationship. Spot UV varnish can be applied over a matt laminate cover to highlight the logo or a specific design element with a contrasting high-gloss finish.

We can design your folder from a brief and brand guidelines, or print from supplied print-ready artwork. All orders include a digital proof before production.
