faqs:
  - question: "What file formats do you accept for artwork preparation?"
    answer: "We work with all major file formats including PDF, AI, EPS, PSD, TIFF, and JPEG. Our team can convert between formats and optimise files for your specific printing requirements."
  - question: "How much does artwork and pre-press service cost?"
    answer: "Our artwork services start from £50 for basic file checking and preparation. Complex projects requiring extensive colour correction or format conversion typically range from £100-£300 depending on the scope of work required."
  - question: "How long does the artwork preparation process take?"
    answer: "Simple file checking and basic corrections usually take 24-48 hours. More complex projects involving extensive colour work or multiple format conversions may take 3-5 working days. We'll provide a clear timeline when you submit your files."
  - question: "Do you provide proofs before final printing?"
    answer: "Yes, we provide digital proofs for all projects and can arrange physical colour-matched proofs when required. This ensures you're completely satisfied with colours and layout before we proceed with the final print run."
  - question: "Can you fix low-resolution images in my artwork?"
    answer: "We can enhance image quality to some extent, but there are limitations with very low-resolution files. We'll assess your images and advise on the best approach, which may include sourcing higher resolution alternatives or professional image enhancement techniques."
  - question: "What if my colours don't match what I see on screen?"
    answer: "Screen colours often differ from print colours due to different colour systems. Our colour correction service ensures accurate reproduction using professional colour management systems and Pantone matching where required."
  - question: "Do you offer colour matching for existing brand materials?"
    answer: "Absolutely. We can colour match to existing printed materials, Pantone references, or brand guidelines. Our 20+ years of experience ensures accurate colour reproduction across all your marketing materials."
  - question: "Can you prepare artwork for different types of printing?"
    answer: "Yes, we optimise files for various printing methods including digital print, litho, wide-format, and UV printing. Each process has specific requirements, and we ensure your files are perfectly prepared for the chosen method."
  - question: "What happens if there are issues with my original artwork files?"
    answer: "We'll identify any technical issues during our file checking process and provide detailed feedback. We can often resolve problems in-house or recommend solutions to ensure your artwork prints successfully."
  - question: "Do you work with files created by other designers?"
    answer: "Yes, we regularly work with artwork created by external designers and agencies. We can prepare and optimise any professional design files, regardless of who created them originally."
  - question: "Is there a minimum order for artwork services?"
    answer: "No minimum order applies to our artwork and pre-press services. We're happy to prepare files for single items or large commercial print runs, with pricing adjusted accordingly for the scope of work required."
  - question: "Can you help if I'm not sure what print specifications I need?"
    answer: "Certainly. Our experienced team can advise on the best print specifications for your intended use, whether it's business cards, large format displays, or vehicle graphics. We'll ensure your files are optimised for the most suitable printing method."
