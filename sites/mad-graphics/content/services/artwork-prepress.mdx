## Artwork & Pre-Press Services

Good print production starts with good artwork. A file that is supplied correctly — at the right resolution, in the right colour mode, with correct bleed and safe zone settings — goes to production without delays or unexpected results. A file that isn't checked before production can produce problems that only become apparent once the job is printed, cut, or applied.

Mad Graphics carries out artwork checking and pre-press preparation as a standard part of every production job. We also offer pre-press services independently — reviewing and preparing artwork supplied by external designers, marketing agencies, and clients who need to supply files to us or to another print provider.

Pre-press preparation covers the full range of checks and corrections needed to take a design file to a production-ready state. Resolution assessment ensures images will reproduce clearly at the intended print size. Colour mode conversion addresses the difference between how colour is handled on screen (RGB) and in print (CMYK or spot colour). Bleed and margin checks confirm that edge elements are set up correctly for trimming. Font handling ensures text is preserved correctly regardless of which software opens the file. File packaging consolidates all linked assets.

For cut vinyl and screen printing production, we also handle colour separation — identifying the individual colour layers from composite artwork and confirming they are correctly specified for production.

If you are supplying artwork to us for production, we provide artwork specification guides in advance. If files need corrections, we identify the issues clearly and either correct them in-house or return the file with instructions so your designer can make the changes.

We serve artwork and pre-press clients across Eastbourne, Hastings, Lewes, Bexhill-on-Sea, Seaford, Hailsham, and across the wider East Sussex area.

## How It Works

### 1. File Receipt & Initial Check
Supply your files and tell us the intended application and production method. We review the files against the requirements for that job — resolution, colour mode, dimensions, bleed, and font handling.

### 2. Issue Report or Clearance
If files are production-ready, we confirm and proceed. If issues are identified, we report them clearly with recommended actions. Minor corrections may be made in-house; significant changes are flagged for your designer to address.

### 3. Pre-Press Preparation
Once files are confirmed correct, we complete any pre-press steps required — colour conversion, separation, profile assignment, and file packaging for production.

### 4. Production Sign-Off
For larger or colour-critical jobs, a proof is produced for your approval before full production begins. Once signed off, the job moves to production.
