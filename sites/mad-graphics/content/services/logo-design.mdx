    answer: "Logo design typically ranges from £150-£500 depending on complexity and revisions required. This includes multiple concepts, vector formats, and brand guidelines. We provide detailed quotes after understanding your specific requirements."

    answer: "Most logo projects are completed within 7-14 days from brief approval. Initial concepts are usually ready within 3-5 days, with additional time for revisions and finalising brand guidelines and file formats."
  - question: "What file formats do you provide with the logo?"
    answer: "You receive comprehensive file packages including vector formats (AI, EPS, PDF), high-resolution raster files (PNG, JPEG), and web-optimised versions. All files are provided in colour, black and white, and single colour variations."

    answer: "We typically present 3-4 initial logo concepts based on your brief. This gives you variety whilst maintaining focus on solutions that meet your specific requirements and brand objectives."

    answer: "Absolutely. With 20+ years in graphics and signage, we design logos specifically considering their application across vehicle graphics, building signs, and marketing materials. Your logo will be optimised for all future branding needs."

    answer: "No, once final payment is made, you own full copyright to your logo design. We provide a copyright transfer document and you're free to use the logo across all applications without any ongoing fees or restrictions."
