## Logo Design

A logo is the foundation of a business's visual identity. It appears on everything — the van, the sign, the website, the business card, the workwear, the packaging. When it is well-designed, every application reinforces the same professional impression. When it is poorly designed, or designed only for one medium, the inconsistencies become apparent as soon as it is applied in the real world.

Mad Graphics has designed logos for East Sussex businesses from our Polegate studio since 2004. Our background in print and signage production gives our design team a practical understanding of how logos perform across all applications — not just on screen.

We work with new businesses starting from scratch, established businesses that have outgrown their original logo, and businesses undergoing a formal rebrand. The starting point is a detailed brief: your business type, your target customers, the tone you want to project, any visual references you respond to, and the applications where the logo will appear.

From that brief our designers produce initial concepts — typically two or three distinct directions. Each concept is presented with a rationale explaining the design decisions. Once you've chosen a direction, we refine it through revision rounds until the design is right.

The final delivery includes a complete set of file formats covering all print and digital applications: vector files for production use, high-resolution rasterised files for web and digital, versions on light and dark backgrounds, and a single-colour monochrome version for applications where full-colour reproduction isn't possible.

We serve logo design clients across Eastbourne, Hastings, Lewes, Bexhill-on-Sea, Seaford, Hailsham, Newhaven, and across East Sussex.

## How It Works

### 1. Brief & Discovery
We discuss your business, your audience, your competitors, and your visual preferences. A written brief is agreed before design work begins.

### 2. Concept Presentation
We present initial concepts with a design rationale. You review, give feedback, and choose the direction you want to develop.

### 3. Refinement
The chosen concept is refined through revision rounds. We refine typography, proportions, colour, and detail until the design meets your brief.

### 4. Final Delivery
Approved artwork is packaged as a complete file set in all required formats. We advise on colour references and usage guidelines.
