## Vehicle Graphics for East Sussex Businesses

Mad Graphics has been producing and applying vehicle graphics from our Polegate workshop since 2004. We specialise in cut vinyl lettering, digitally printed panels, and hand-applied signwriting for vans, cars, and commercial fleets of any size.

Every set of graphics we produce is designed to be read at speed — clear contact details, bold branding, and clean layouts that make your vehicles work as moving billboards. We handle everything in-house: artwork, print, lamination, and application.

Whether you need a single van lettered up for a sole trader or a dozen vehicles branded consistently for a growing fleet, the process is the same: we quote quickly, produce accurately, and apply to a high finish.

Our service covers the full East Sussex area, with customers regularly coming to us from Eastbourne, Hastings, Lewes, Bexhill-on-Sea, Uckfield, Hailsham, Seaford, Newhaven, and surrounding towns.

## Why Choose Mad Graphics?

Mad Graphics has operated from the same Polegate workshop for over 20 years. That continuity means we have built real knowledge of what works on the road in East Sussex — what colours hold up in coastal salt air, which layouts get read at roundabout speed, and how to apply graphics to the panel curves found on the most common commercial vehicles.

We use commercial-grade vinyl from established suppliers, and every printed graphic is laminated before application to resist UV fading and road wash. There are no subcontracted application teams — the people who quote your job also produce and fit it.

Our pricing is straightforward. We quote per job with no hidden setup fees, and we aim to turn most single-vehicle projects around within a few working days.
