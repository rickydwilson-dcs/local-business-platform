## Vehicle Livery Design

A vehicle livery is more than a logo on a door. It is a considered layout that makes the best use of every panel on your vehicle — positioning your brand identity, contact details, and service information so they communicate clearly to pedestrians, passing drivers, and people looking at a parked van from ten metres away.

Getting this right before anything is cut or printed saves time, money, and the disappointment of graphics that looked fine on a flat screen but read poorly on an actual vehicle. That is what the livery design stage is for.

Mad Graphics has an in-house studio at our Polegate workshop. Our designers produce vehicle livery layouts to scale — working from real vehicle templates that accurately represent the panel dimensions, curves, wheel arches, and handle positions of your specific make and model. What you approve is what gets produced.

The design process starts with a brief. We want to know how the vehicle is used, who sees it and from what distance, what message is most important (trade, brand, contact), and what brand assets you already have. From that brief we produce an initial layout that optimises readability and visual impact within the constraints of the vehicle's bodywork.

Typography, colour specification, and graphic hierarchy are all considered at this stage. If a colour combination will not meet basic contrast requirements in real-world lighting, we will flag it. If a typeface will not be legible at speed, we will recommend an alternative.

Design is followed by production and application — all handled in-house. For customers who want livery design as a standalone service, we can deliver print-ready artwork files for use with another supplier.

We work with businesses across Eastbourne, Hastings, Lewes, Bexhill-on-Sea, Seaford, Hailsham, and across East Sussex.

## How It Works

### 1. Design Brief
We take a brief covering your business, vehicles, brand assets, and objectives. Existing artwork or brand guidelines are supplied at this stage.

### 2. Initial Layout Proof
Our studio produces a scaled livery layout on your vehicle template and presents it for review. Typography, colour, and panel treatment are all shown as they will appear on the vehicle.

### 3. Revisions & Sign-Off
You provide feedback and we refine the layout. Production does not begin until you have given written sign-off on the final design.

### 4. Production & Application
Approved artwork goes to our production team. Graphics are cut or printed, laminated, and applied by our own fitters at the Polegate workshop.
