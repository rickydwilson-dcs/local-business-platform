faqs:
  - question: "What sizes can you print canvas prints in?"
    answer: "We offer custom canvas printing in virtually any size, from small 8x10 inch personal prints up to large commercial displays over 60 inches wide. Our wide-format equipment handles standard sizes like A4, A3, A2, A1, and A0, plus bespoke dimensions to fit your specific wall space perfectly."
  - question: "How much do canvas prints cost?"
    answer: "Canvas print pricing starts from £50 for smaller personal prints, with larger custom pieces ranging up to £500 depending on size and complexity. Commercial projects typically range from £200-£5,000. We provide free quotes based on your specific requirements including size, quantity, and finishing options."
  - question: "How long does it take to produce canvas prints?"
    answer: "Most canvas prints are completed within 3-5 working days from artwork approval. Rush orders can often be accommodated within 24-48 hours for an additional fee. Complex custom sizing or large commercial orders may require 5-7 working days. We'll confirm exact timescales when you place your order."
  - question: "What file formats do you accept for canvas printing?"
    answer: "We accept high-resolution JPEG, PNG, TIFF, and PDF files. For best results, images should be at least 300 DPI at the final print size. Our in-house design team can advise on file preparation and can enhance or adjust images if needed to ensure optimal print quality."
  - question: "Do you offer gallery wrap or just flat mounting?"
    answer: "We specialise in gallery wrap canvas prints where the image extends around the edges of the frame, creating a professional museum-quality finish. This eliminates the need for additional framing and provides a clean, contemporary look. We can also accommodate specific edge treatments if required."
  - question: "Are your canvas prints suitable for outdoor use?"
    answer: "Our canvas prints use UV-rated materials designed primarily for indoor display. Whilst they offer excellent fade resistance indoors, we don't recommend extended outdoor exposure. For outdoor applications, we can suggest alternative weather-resistant materials from our large-format printing range."
  - question: "Can you help with image editing or design work?"
    answer: "Yes, our in-house design team with over 20 years' experience can enhance images, adjust colours, remove backgrounds, or create custom artwork. This service is included for minor adjustments, with more extensive design work quoted separately. We ensure your canvas print achieves the best possible visual impact."
  - question: "Do canvas prints come ready to hang?"
    answer: "All our canvas prints come fully stretched on wooden frames and are wall-ready with hanging hardware fitted. No additional framing is required. We include appropriate fixings and can advise on wall mounting for heavier pieces. Installation guidance is provided with every order."
  - question: "What's the difference between your canvas prints and photo prints?"
    answer: "Canvas prints are stretched over wooden frames creating a textured, artistic finish similar to traditional paintings. They're thicker, more substantial, and don't require framing. Photo prints are flat prints on photographic paper requiring separate framing. Canvas offers a more premium, gallery-style presentation."
  - question: "Can you print from old or damaged photographs?"
    answer: "Yes, our experienced team can work with old, faded, or damaged photographs. We can scan physical photos, enhance digital quality, restore colours, and remove scratches or tears digitally before printing. This restoration work is quoted separately but allows precious memories to be preserved beautifully on canvas."
  - question: "Do you have public liability insurance for commercial installations?"
    answer: "Yes, Mad Graphics carries full public liability insurance covering all our services including canvas print production and any installation advice we provide. We've been established since 2004 and maintain comprehensive coverage for commercial and domestic projects throughout East Sussex."
  - question: "How do I get a quote for my canvas print project?"
    answer: "Contact us with your requirements including desired size, quantity, and any special finishing needs. We can provide instant quotes for standard sizes, or detailed estimates for custom projects within 24 hours. Email your images for a comprehensive quote including any design work needed."
