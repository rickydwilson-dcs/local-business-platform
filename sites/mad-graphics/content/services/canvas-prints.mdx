## Canvas Prints

Canvas prints bring a level of visual warmth and quality to a space that flat-printed boards and posters cannot quite replicate. The texture of the canvas material combined with the depth of the stretcher frame gives each print a gallery-quality appearance that suits offices, hotels, restaurants, health clinics, and any commercial interior where the environment matters.

At Mad Graphics, we produce canvas prints to custom sizes in-house at our Polegate workshop. We have been providing print solutions to businesses across East Sussex since 2004, and canvas print is one of the most satisfying products we produce — because a well-executed large canvas print genuinely transforms a space.

We print on quality artist-grade canvas using pigment-based inks that are designed for colour longevity. The canvas is stretched by hand over a robust timber sub-frame and stapled on the reverse, leaving the print face and wrapped edges free of hardware. Hanging fixings are fitted before despatch so the print is ready to go up immediately.

Commercial clients are our primary focus for canvas print. Hotels and guest houses in Eastbourne, Hastings, Bexhill-on-Sea, and the wider East Sussex area use canvas prints to add character and local identity to rooms and public spaces — coastal photography, local landmarks, abstract art. Offices and corporate spaces use them to display company values, brand imagery, or professional photography of their work and team.

Restaurants and hospitality venues use canvas prints as feature wall graphics, menu art, and atmospheric imagery that contributes to the customer experience.

If you have your own photography or artwork at sufficient resolution, the process is straightforward. If you need sourcing support for appropriate imagery, our design team can assist with licensed photography or custom artwork creation.

## How It Works

### 1. Image & Size Discussion
Share your image file and intended dimensions. We check resolution at the target size and advise on any adjustments needed.

### 2. Proof
A digital colour proof is provided for approval, especially for colour-critical or large-format work.

### 3. Print & Stretch
Printed on quality canvas, stretched over a timber sub-frame, and fitted with hanging hardware.

### 4. Ready to Collect or Deliver
Collect from our Polegate workshop or arrange delivery anywhere in East Sussex.

## Why Choose Mad Graphics?

Canvas stretching and mounting requires skill. A poorly stretched canvas shows waves, slack corners, or inconsistent tension across the face. Our team takes care with every print to ensure the finished piece is taut, square, and properly finished on the reverse.

We print with pigment inks for colour permanence — important for any display that will be in place for months or years.
