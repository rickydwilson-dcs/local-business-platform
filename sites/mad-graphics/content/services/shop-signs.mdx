## Shop Signs & Fascia Signs

Your shop front is your first point of contact with passing trade. A well-executed fascia sign tells people immediately what you do, makes your business look established and trustworthy, and earns its cost back in enquiries that would otherwise have walked past.

Mad Graphics produces shop signs and fascia signage for independent retailers, hospitality businesses, salons, offices, and service premises across East Sussex. We work with businesses on Eastbourne's high streets and shopping districts, in Lewes town centre, along Hastings' retail areas, and throughout the smaller towns and villages across the county.

Our fascia sign production covers printed flat boards, applied vinyl lettering on existing boards, flat cut acrylic or foam letters fixed proud of the fascia for a three-dimensional finish, and projecting blade signs for visibility from along the street.

We produce signs on substrates suited to exterior use — aluminium composite, dibond, and UV-stable PVC foam board in appropriate thicknesses for the application. All print is UV-laminated and all materials are selected to withstand the East Sussex coastal and inland climate without fading or delaminating.

If you have existing brand artwork, we work from it directly. If you are starting a new business or rebranding, our in-house studio can develop a shop front identity from a brief — testing layouts at scale before anything goes to production.

We can handle installation for most fascia applications in East Sussex. This includes standard fascia replacements and projecting sign brackets. Where planning consent is required — conservation areas, listed buildings, or non-standard sizes — we can advise on the application process based on our experience with Eastbourne Borough Council and Lewes District Council.

## How It Works

### 1. Site Assessment & Quote
We take measurements or you supply them. If needed, we visit the premises to assess fixing options, fascia condition, and any planning considerations. A quote follows promptly.

### 2. Design & Approval
Our studio produces a visual of your sign in context — showing how it will look on your specific fascia. Amendments are included before sign-off.

### 3. Production
Signs are produced in-house to the approved design. Standard turnaround is 3 to 5 working days from sign-off.

### 4. Installation
We install the finished sign at your premises. For flat fascia panels and projecting signs we carry out the full installation. We leave the site clean and the sign ready to trade.
