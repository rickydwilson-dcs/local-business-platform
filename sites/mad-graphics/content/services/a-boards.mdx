## A-Boards & Pavement Signs

An A-board at your entrance does one job — it catches the eye of someone walking past who did not already know they were going to stop. For cafes, restaurants, independent shops, salons, and service businesses in East Sussex town centres and high streets, that foot traffic is a significant source of new customers.

Mad Graphics designs and produces A-boards and pavement signs for businesses across East Sussex. We supply complete sign systems — frame and panel together — and replacement panels for existing frames. Printed panels, chalkboard surfaces, and interchangeable poster pocket systems are all available.

Printed A-board panels produced in our Polegate workshop are laminated for outdoor durability. They can carry your branding, a daily special, a promotion, or directional information. For businesses that change their message regularly, we also produce poster pocket A-board systems where a printed A1 or A0 insert slides in and out of the frame — cost-effective for frequent updates since only the insert needs replacing, not the whole panel.

Chalkboard panels suit businesses that write their own daily content — menus, specials, events — without needing to return to a print supplier. We supply chalkboard-surfaced panels to fit most standard A-board frames.

For events, we produce A-boards and larger freestanding signs for temporary use — festivals, markets, exhibitions, and corporate events across East Sussex. Printed correx panels on reusable frames are the most practical and cost-effective option for one-off applications.

We serve businesses in Eastbourne, Hastings, Lewes, Bexhill-on-Sea, Seaford, Hailsham, Uckfield, Newhaven, Polegate, Peacehaven, and across East Sussex.

## How It Works

### 1. Brief & Frame Specification
Tell us your location, how often you want to change the message, and what you want the sign to carry. We recommend the most appropriate frame and panel type.

### 2. Design & Proof
Our studio designs the panel layout and presents a proof for your approval. For replacement panels, we match your existing brand or content.

### 3. Production
Panels are printed and laminated in-house. Lead time is typically 2 to 3 working days from sign-off. Frames are supplied alongside or to order.

### 4. Collection
Complete A-board systems and replacement panels are available to collect from our Polegate workshop. Most are ready to use straight away.
