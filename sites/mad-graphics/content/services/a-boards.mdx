faqs:
  - question: "How much do A-boards cost?"
    answer: "Our A-boards typically range from £50-£200 depending on size and specification. This includes the aluminium frame, snap-frame mechanism, and initial printed insert. Additional poster inserts cost from £15 each."
  - question: "What sizes of A-boards do you offer?"
    answer: "We supply A-boards in A2, A1, and A0 sizes. A2 is popular for cafes and small shops, whilst A1 and A0 provide maximum impact for larger businesses and busy locations."
  - question: "How quickly can you deliver A-boards?"
    answer: "Standard A-boards with printed inserts are typically ready within 3-5 working days. Rush orders can be accommodated within 24-48 hours for an additional charge, subject to availability."
  - question: "Are your A-boards weatherproof?"
    answer: "Yes, our A-boards feature powder-coated aluminium frames and UV-rated printed inserts that resist fading and weather damage. The snap-frame design also includes rubber seals to protect posters from moisture."
  - question: "Can I change the posters myself?"
    answer: "Absolutely. Our snap-frame system allows you to change posters in seconds without tools. Simply lift the frame edges, replace the poster, and snap closed. We provide instructions with every A-board."
  - question: "Do I need planning permission for pavement signs?"
    answer: "Most councils allow A-boards under permitted development rights, but regulations vary. We advise checking with your local East Sussex council. Signs must typically be removed outside business hours and not obstruct pedestrian access."
  - question: "Can you design the posters for my A-board?"
    answer: "Yes, our in-house design team can create professional poster designs that maximise impact and readability. Design services are included in our full-service packages or available separately from £25."
  - question: "What areas do you cover for A-board delivery?"
    answer: "We deliver throughout East Sussex from our Polegate base. Local delivery within 10 miles is typically free, with modest charges for longer distances. Installation service is also available if required."
  - question: "How long do A-board posters last outdoors?"
    answer: "Our UV-rated poster materials typically maintain excellent colour and clarity for 12-18 months outdoors. However, high-traffic locations or extreme weather may require more frequent replacement."
  - question: "Can A-boards be weighted for windy conditions?"
    answer: "Yes, we supply water-fillable bases or sandbag attachments for exposed locations. Our frames are designed to be stable in normal conditions, but additional weighting is recommended for seafront or hilltop locations."
  - question: "Do you offer bulk discounts for multiple A-boards?"
    answer: "Yes, we provide competitive pricing for multiple units. Businesses requiring 3 or more A-boards typically receive a 10-15% discount. Contact us for a tailored quote based on your requirements."
  - question: "What's included in your A-board service?"
    answer: "Our service includes the aluminium snap-frame, initial poster design and print, delivery within East Sussex, and ongoing support. We also provide guidance on positioning and local regulations to ensure compliance."
