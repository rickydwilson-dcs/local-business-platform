faqs:
  - question: "What sizes can you print for large format work?"
    answer: "We can print virtually any size from A3 up to 1600mm wide with unlimited length. Common sizes include A0, A1, A2 posters, banners up to several metres, and custom dimensions to suit your specific requirements."
  - question: "How much does large format printing cost?"
    answer: "Pricing starts from £50 for smaller prints, with most domestic jobs ranging £50-£500 and commercial projects £200-£5,000. Final cost depends on size, material, quantity, and finishing requirements. We provide free quotes for all projects."
  - question: "What materials can you print on?"
    answer: "We offer multiple substrates including photo paper, canvas, vinyl, banner material, foam board, aluminium composite, correx, and rigid plastics. All outdoor materials are UV-rated for weather resistance and longevity."
  - question: "How quickly can you complete large format printing?"
    answer: "Most standard prints are completed within 24-48 hours. Rush jobs can often be turned around same day or next day depending on size and complexity. We'll confirm exact timescales when you place your order."
  - question: "Do you provide design services for large format prints?"
    answer: "Yes, we offer in-house design and artwork services. Our experienced team can create designs from scratch, adapt existing artwork, or prepare your files for optimal print quality at large formats."
  - question: "Can you print technical drawings and architectural plans?"
    answer: "Absolutely. We specialise in printing technical drawings, architectural plans, engineering diagrams, and CAD files with precise accuracy and clarity, maintaining all detail even at large scales."
  - question: "Are your large format prints suitable for outdoor use?"
    answer: "Yes, we use UV-rated inks and materials specifically designed for outdoor applications. Our outdoor prints resist fading, weather damage, and maintain colour vibrancy for extended periods in all weather conditions."
  - question: "Do you offer finishing services for large format prints?"
    answer: "We provide various finishing options including lamination for protection, mounting on rigid boards, hemming and eyelets for banners, and trimming to exact specifications. All finishing is completed in-house for quality control."
  - question: "What file formats do you accept for large format printing?"
    answer: "We accept PDF, AI, EPS, TIFF, and high-resolution JPEG files. For best results, supply vector files or high-resolution raster images at 150-300 DPI. We can advise on file preparation if needed."
  - question: "Can you handle large quantity orders?"
    answer: "Yes, we regularly handle both single prints and large quantity runs. Our commercial pricing structure offers better rates for larger orders, and we can schedule production to meet your delivery requirements."
  - question: "Do you deliver large format prints?"
    answer: "We offer local delivery throughout East Sussex and can arrange courier services for larger orders. Collection from our Polegate facility is also available, and we'll carefully package all prints to prevent damage during transport."
  - question: "What's included in your large format printing service?"
    answer: "Our service includes file checking and preparation, professional printing using quality materials, basic finishing, quality control inspection, and careful packaging. Design services and advanced finishing options are available as extras."
