## Large Format Printing

Large format printing covers everything from a two-metre exhibition backdrop to a floor-to-ceiling window graphic, from a hoarding-mounted promotional board to a photographic canvas installation. If you need something bigger than a standard desktop printer can produce, Mad Graphics can help — and we do it all in-house at our Polegate workshop.

We have been producing large format print for businesses, events, and organisations across East Sussex since 2004. Our equipment handles a wide range of substrates and widths, giving us the flexibility to print on paper, self-adhesive vinyl, PVC, mesh, canvas, foam board, correx, and specialist display films. The right substrate for your project depends on how it will be displayed, how long it needs to last, and whether it needs to be applied to a surface, hung, or mounted.

Retail clients use large format print for window displays, internal wall graphics, promotional boards, and seasonal decorations that transform the look of a shop without permanent alteration. Hospitality venues use backdrop graphics for event photography, large feature wall prints for ambience, and menu boards that command attention in a busy room.

Event organisers and exhibitors rely on large format print for stage backdrops, entrance graphics, directional signage, and branded display walls. Construction companies and developers use it for hoarding graphics and site boards.

Whatever the application, the process starts with your artwork — or a brief for our design team. We check that files are at an appropriate resolution for the intended print size, advise on the right substrate, and provide a proof before production. Because everything is done in-house, we can turn jobs around quickly and address any last-minute changes without involving third-party suppliers.
