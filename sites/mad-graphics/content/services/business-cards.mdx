## Business Cards

A business card is often the first physical piece of branded material a new contact receives from you. Its quality, finish, and design say something about your business before a word is spoken. At Mad Graphics, we produce business cards that make the right impression — from clean and professional to premium and distinctive.

We serve businesses across Eastbourne, Hastings, Lewes, Bexhill-on-Sea, Seaford, Hailsham, Polegate, and the whole of East Sussex. Clients range from sole traders and freelancers to established companies, professional services firms, and hospitality businesses.

Our standard business cards are printed on 400gsm coated board — thick and rigid, with none of the flimsy feel of cheap desktop-printed cards. Finish options include gloss laminate for vibrant, high-contrast colour; matt laminate for a refined, understated appearance; and soft-touch laminate for a premium velvet feel that consistently draws compliments from people who handle it.

Double-sided printing is available and, in many cases, recommended. The reverse side of a business card is a significant amount of print space that is often left blank. Using it for a list of services, a QR code linking to your website, a map, or your social media handles increases the usefulness of the card and the likelihood that it is kept rather than discarded.

We also offer folded business cards — a 85×110mm card that folds down to standard 85×55mm size — for clients who have more information to communicate than a standard card accommodates.

If you have existing brand guidelines and print-ready artwork, send us your files and we will have a proof to you quickly. If you are starting from scratch or need a refresh, our design team creates business card designs as part of a full design and print package.
