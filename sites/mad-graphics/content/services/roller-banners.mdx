## Roller Banners & Pull-Up Banners

Roller banners — also known as pull-up banners — are the go-to display solution for exhibitions, conferences, trade events, shop floors, and reception areas. They are fast to set up, easy to transport, and make an immediate visual impact wherever they are placed. At Mad Graphics, we print and supply roller banners to businesses across East Sussex from our Polegate workshop.

The mechanism is simple: the printed graphic is housed in a compact base cassette. When needed, you pull the graphic upward, clip in the support pole, and your display is ready in under a minute. When the event is over, the graphic retracts back into the base for protection during transit. The carry bag supplied with each unit makes moving between venues straightforward.

We offer a range of standard widths — 800mm, 850mm, and 1000mm are the most popular — as well as wider formats for situations where you need a larger presence. Height is typically around 2 metres, giving you a tall, upright display that reads well across a busy exhibition hall or event space.

Print quality is key with roller banners, because viewers often stand close to the graphic. We print on high-quality display media with vibrant, colour-accurate inks that reproduce photography, brand colours, and fine text cleanly. If you have a specific colour system (Pantone references or a brand guide), we will work to match it as closely as possible.

Our in-house design team can create your roller banner artwork from a brief or existing assets. We supply a digital proof before production and will not print until you are satisfied with the layout.

Roller banners are popular with solicitors, estate agents, letting agents, retail shops, restaurants, healthcare providers, and any business that exhibits or attends events across Eastbourne, Hastings, Lewes, Bexhill-on-Sea, and the surrounding areas.

## How It Works

### 1. Size & Brief
Choose your width and tell us your message and branding. We prepare artwork or check your supplied file.

### 2. Proof & Approval
A digital proof is sent for your review. We adjust until you are satisfied, then proceed to print.

### 3. Print & Assemble
The graphic is printed, trimmed, and fitted into the cassette and hardware. Packaged with a carry bag.

### 4. Ready to Collect or Deliver
Pick up from Polegate or have the banner delivered to your address across East Sussex.
