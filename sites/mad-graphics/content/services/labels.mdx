## Labels & Product Labels

A well-designed label communicates quality before a customer has opened the product. For local producers, food businesses, and craft goods makers across East Sussex, the label is often the first impression — and it needs to be consistent, durable, and clearly printed.

Mad Graphics has supplied product labels to East Sussex businesses from our Polegate studio since 2004. We produce short runs and long runs, on paper and vinyl substrates, for a wide range of product types.

We work with small food and drink producers, cosmetics makers, craft beer and cider breweries, honey and jam producers, candle makers, and many other independent businesses across the region. The common requirement is professional labelling without the minimum order quantities imposed by large commercial print houses. Our digital print process is well-suited to short runs — the unit costs are reasonable and we can turn orders around quickly.

Paper labels are appropriate for products stored in dry conditions — boxed goods, dry food products, and shelf-stable items. Waterproof vinyl labels are the right choice for bottles, jars, refrigerated goods, and anything exposed to moisture during use or storage. We can laminate labels to add further protection against abrasion and handling wear.

All label artwork is checked and colour-managed before printing. If you supply your own files we'll verify them against production requirements. If you need design work, our studio will produce label artwork to your specification and provide a proof before production begins.

We serve label customers across Eastbourne, Hastings, Lewes, Bexhill-on-Sea, Seaford, Uckfield, Hailsham, and the wider East Sussex area.

## How It Works

### 1. Brief & Quote
Tell us your product type, label dimensions, substrate preference, and quantity. We'll provide a written quote and advise on material options.

### 2. Artwork & Proof
Supply your artwork or commission our design team. We check files for print readiness and send a digital proof — or a physical sample for colour-critical runs — before production.

### 3. Print & Finish
Labels are digitally printed and finished in-house. Lamination and die-cutting are applied as required. Typical turnaround is two to four working days from sign-off.

### 4. Collection or Delivery
Collect from our Polegate workshop or arrange delivery. For ongoing repeat orders we can hold your approved artwork for fast reordering.
