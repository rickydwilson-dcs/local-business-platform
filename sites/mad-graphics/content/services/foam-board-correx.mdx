faqs:
  - question: "What's the difference between foam board and correx?"
    answer: "Foam board has a lightweight foam core sandwiched between smooth surfaces, ideal for indoor displays and short-term outdoor use. Correx is corrugated plastic sheeting that's more flexible and weather-resistant, perfect for longer-term outdoor applications like estate agent boards."
  - question: "What sizes can you print foam board and correx?"
    answer: "We can produce custom sizes up to 1.5m wide with virtually unlimited length. Common sizes include A4, A3, A2, A1, and bespoke dimensions to fit your specific requirements. Our team will advise on the most cost-effective sizing options."
  - question: "How much does foam board and correx printing cost?"
    answer: "Pricing typically ranges from £50-£500 per job depending on size, quantity, and complexity. A standard A2 full-colour foam board starts from around £15, whilst larger custom correx boards range from £25-£75 each. Contact us for a detailed quote."
  - question: "How long does production take?"
    answer: "Standard orders are completed within 3-5 working days from artwork approval. Rush jobs can often be accommodated within 24-48 hours for an additional charge. We'll confirm exact timescales when you place your order."
  - question: "Are the materials suitable for outdoor use?"
    answer: "Yes, we use UV-rated inks and materials designed for outdoor applications. Correx boards are particularly weather-resistant and suitable for long-term outdoor use. Foam boards work well for shorter outdoor campaigns or permanent indoor displays."
  - question: "Do you provide design services?"
    answer: "Absolutely. Our in-house design team can create professional artwork from your brief, or work with existing logos and content. Design services are included in most projects, ensuring your boards look professional and effective."
  - question: "Can you deliver or do I need to collect?"
    answer: "We offer both collection from our Polegate workshop and delivery throughout East Sussex. Delivery charges vary by location and order size. Large orders can often qualify for free local delivery within our coverage area."
  - question: "What file formats do you accept for artwork?"
    answer: "We accept PDF, AI, EPS, and high-resolution JPEG or PNG files. For best results, provide vector files where possible. Our design team can also work with lower-resolution images and create print-ready artwork for you."
  - question: "Are your materials environmentally friendly?"
    answer: "We use recyclable materials where possible. Correx boards are fully recyclable, and we can advise on the most environmentally responsible options for your project. We also offer eco-friendly ink options for environmentally conscious clients."
  - question: "Do you offer mounting or finishing options?"
    answer: "Yes, we can provide various finishing options including mounting holes, corner rounding, and lamination for extra durability. We can also recommend the best mounting methods for your specific application and provide guidance on installation."
  - question: "What's your minimum order quantity?"
    answer: "There's no minimum order quantity - we're happy to print single boards or large quantities. However, unit costs decrease with larger orders, so we'll always advise on the most cost-effective approach for your requirements."
  - question: "Are you covered by public liability insurance?"
    answer: "Yes, we maintain full public liability insurance coverage. As an established business operating since 2004, we ensure all necessary insurances and certifications are in place to protect both our clients and our operations."
