## Foam Board & Correx Printing

Rigid printed boards are the workhorses of both indoor display and outdoor site signage. Foam board and correx are two of the most widely used materials in this category, and Mad Graphics produces both at our Polegate workshop for businesses and organisations across East Sussex.

Foam board — also marketed as foamex or PVC foam board — is a smooth-faced expanded PVC sheet that takes direct print beautifully. It is rigid, lightweight, and easy to mount. The smooth surface gives excellent colour reproduction, making it a popular choice for in-store displays, menu boards, point-of-sale graphics, framed prints, exhibition inserts, and any indoor environment where you need a flat, stiff display graphic that looks finished without a frame. We stock foam board in 3mm and 5mm thicknesses to suit different display requirements.

Correx is a different animal. The twin-wall, fluted polypropylene construction makes it inherently weatherproof, and its light weight means it can be fixed quickly with cable ties, staples, or dedicated posts. Estate agents across Eastbourne, Hastings, Lewes, Bexhill-on-Sea, Seaford, Hailsham, and the wider East Sussex area use correx boards for their standard for-sale, to-let, and sold boards. Construction companies use correx for temporary site signage, health and safety notices, and directional boards. Event organisers use it for entrance signage, roadside directions, and sponsor boards.

We print directly onto both materials using UV-resistant inks that maintain colour quality in outdoor conditions. All boards are cut to your exact size requirements, and custom shapes are available. Double-sided printing on correx is a standard option for estate agent boards and roadside signs that need to be read from both directions.
