## Flyers & Leaflets

Flyers and leaflets remain one of the most effective forms of local marketing for businesses in East Sussex. Whether you are promoting a new service, advertising an event, announcing a seasonal offer, or increasing brand awareness in your local area, a well-designed, professionally printed flyer placed in the right hands delivers results.

At Mad Graphics, we have been printing flyers and leaflets for East Sussex businesses since 2004. We serve clients in Eastbourne, Hastings, Lewes, Bexhill-on-Sea, Seaford, Hailsham, Uckfield, Polegate, and across the region — from independent shops and tradespeople to hospitality venues, healthcare providers, schools, and community organisations.

We print on quality gloss or matt coated paper stock, with lamination options available for a more premium finish. Standard sizes include A5, A6, and DL — all common formats that fit standard display stands, letterboxes, and carrier bags. Custom sizes are available for briefs that require something different.

Short print runs are welcome. If you are a local business testing a new promotion, we can print as few as 50–100 copies. If you are planning a town-wide distribution or a mail-drop campaign, we scale to tens of thousands without compromising on quality or turnaround time.

Turnaround is typically 2–3 working days from artwork approval for standard jobs. If you are working to a specific deadline — an event, a launch date, a seasonal campaign — tell us when you order and we will plan accordingly.

Our design team can create your flyer artwork from a brief if you do not have print-ready files. We will walk you through what information works best on a flyer, how to structure a clear call to action, and how to make your brand stand out on a crowded notice board or pile of leaflets.
