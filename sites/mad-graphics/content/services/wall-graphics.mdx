## Wall Graphics & Wall Art

A well-executed wall graphic transforms a blank wall into a brand statement, a wayfinding system, or a piece of large-format art that defines the character of a space. For East Sussex offices, retail units, reception areas, restaurants, and hospitality venues, wall graphics are a highly effective and relatively low-cost way to create a professional interior environment.

Mad Graphics has produced commercial wall graphics from our Polegate studio since 2004. We work with businesses of all sizes — from sole traders decorating a single consultation room to multi-site organisations rolling out branded wall schemes across offices and retail outlets.

We produce both printed and cut vinyl wall graphics. Printed graphics allow full-colour photography, complex illustrations, gradient backgrounds, and brand imagery to be reproduced at large scale. Cut vinyl is suited to text, logos, icons, and geometric shapes in solid colours — producing a crisp, flat finish with no substrate visible around the lettering.

All wall vinyl we use is printable and cuttable to tight tolerances. For printed wall graphics, we use materials specifically designed for interior wall application — they conform slightly to surface texture and resist peeling at edges in low-humidity environments. Lamination protects the print against scuffing and cleaning.

We serve customers across Eastbourne, Hastings, Lewes, Bexhill-on-Sea, Seaford, Hailsham, Newhaven, and across East Sussex. For large commercial fit-out projects, we can work to programme timescales alongside your contractor.

## How It Works

### 1. Site Survey or Brief
For large installations we recommend a site visit to check wall dimensions and surface condition. Alternatively, supply accurate measurements and wall type details and we'll prepare artwork to fit.

### 2. Design & Proof
Our studio produces scaled artwork showing how the graphic will appear on your wall. A digital proof is provided for approval before production. Large multi-panel installations are planned at this stage.

### 3. Production
Graphics are printed and laminated in-house. Cut vinyl elements are produced on our digital cutters. Lead time is typically two to four working days from sign-off.

### 4. Installation
Supply with fitting instructions for self-application, or book our installation team for a professional fit. We advise on which approach suits your project scale and complexity.
