faqs:
  - question: "How much does brand identity design cost?"
    answer: "Brand identity packages typically range from £200-£2,000 depending on complexity. Basic logo and colour palette starts around £200, whilst comprehensive brand identity with full guidelines ranges £500-£2,000. We provide detailed quotes after understanding your specific requirements."
  - question: "What's included in a complete brand identity package?"
    answer: "Our complete packages include logo design, colour palette, typography selection, brand guidelines document, and various logo formats for different applications. We also provide guidance on how to apply your brand consistently across signage, print materials, and digital platforms."
  - question: "How long does brand identity design take?"
    answer: "Typical timelines are 2-4 weeks depending on project scope. Logo design usually takes 1-2 weeks, whilst complete brand identity with guidelines takes 3-4 weeks. We work efficiently whilst ensuring thorough consultation and revision stages."
  - question: "Do you provide brand guidelines documentation?"
    answer: "Yes, comprehensive brand guidelines are included with full identity packages. These cover logo usage, colour specifications, typography, spacing requirements, and application examples. This ensures consistent brand presentation across all your materials and signage."
  - question: "Can you redesign our existing brand or just create new ones?"
    answer: "We handle both new brand creation and rebranding projects. Our 20+ years experience includes refreshing outdated brands, complete rebrands, and creating entirely new identities. We assess your current position and recommend the best approach for your goals."
  - question: "Will the brand work for both digital and print applications?"
    answer: "Absolutely. We design brands that work effectively across all media - from business cards and signage to websites and social media. All logo formats and colour specifications are provided to ensure consistent reproduction across different platforms and materials."
  - question: "Do you help implement the brand on signage and marketing materials?"
    answer: "Yes, as a specialist print graphics company, we can implement your new brand identity across signage, vehicle graphics, exhibition materials, and printed marketing. This ensures perfect brand consistency from design through to final application."
  - question: "What happens if we need changes after the brand is completed?"
    answer: "We include revision rounds during the design process to ensure satisfaction. Post-completion changes can be accommodated for additional fees. We maintain all original design files and can provide ongoing brand support as your business evolves."
  - question: "Can you work with our existing marketing team or agencies?"
    answer: "Certainly. We regularly collaborate with marketing teams, web designers, and other agencies. We provide all necessary brand files and specifications to ensure seamless integration with your existing marketing efforts and maintain brand consistency."
  - question: "Do you provide trademark advice for new logos?"
    answer: "Whilst we're not trademark solicitors, we conduct basic searches to avoid obvious conflicts and can recommend trademark professionals if needed. We design distinctive logos that are original and suitable for trademark registration where appropriate."
  - question: "How do we get started with brand identity design?"
    answer: "Contact us for an initial consultation where we discuss your business, target audience, and brand goals. We then provide a detailed quote and timeline. Once approved, we begin with research and concept development, keeping you involved throughout the creative process."
  - question: "Are you covered by public liability insurance for design work?"
    answer: "Yes, we maintain comprehensive public liability insurance covering all our design and print services. This provides protection for both parties during the brand development process and any subsequent implementation of brand materials."
