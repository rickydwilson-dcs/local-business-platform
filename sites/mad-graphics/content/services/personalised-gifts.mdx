## Personalised Gifts

A personalised gift carries a message that a standard product cannot. Whether it marks an achievement, celebrates a milestone, thanks a valued client, or recognises years of service, a custom-made item shows a level of thought and care that generic gifts simply don't convey.

Mad Graphics produces personalised gifts and custom-printed items from our Polegate studio. We work with individuals looking for a unique personal gift and with businesses that need personalised items for clients, staff, and promotional use.

Our production capability covers a range of gift formats. Photo prints on canvas, board, and acrylic allow personal or brand photography to be reproduced as framed or mounted artwork. Keyrings and lanyards can be produced with printed inserts, custom artwork, and branded graphics. Awards and plaques can be printed or engraved with recipient names, dates, and achievement details. Desk items, custom frames, and branded gift sets round out a broad range of options.

For businesses, personalised gifts serve a practical purpose. A framed print presented to a long-serving employee. A branded award for a customer loyalty programme. A custom plaque commemorating a project completion. A printed canvas for a client's office. These are items that the recipient keeps and that continue to represent your business for years after the gift is given.

We keep production mostly in-house, which allows us to produce single items economically and control the quality of every piece. We don't require large minimum orders for most gift items.

We serve gift customers across Eastbourne, Hastings, Lewes, Bexhill-on-Sea, Seaford, Hailsham, Newhaven, Polegate, and the wider East Sussex area.

## How It Works

### 1. Brief & Quote
Tell us what you're looking for — the occasion, the recipient, the message, and any artwork or photography you want to use. We'll propose suitable options and provide a quote.

### 2. Artwork & Personalisation
We prepare the artwork including any personalised text, names, dates, or logos. A proof is provided for your review before production begins.

### 3. Production
Items are produced in-house to your approved proof. Most personalised gift items are ready within three to five working days. We'll confirm the lead time for your specific order.

### 4. Collection or Delivery
Collect from our Polegate workshop or arrange delivery. Packaging options are available for gifting presentation.
