## Personalised Merchandise & Promotional Items

Branded merchandise puts your business name and logo into the hands of existing customers and potential new ones. A mug used daily in a customer's kitchen. A pen that travels through an office. A tote bag carried on the high street. Each of these items is a low-cost, long-running advertisement for your business — one that the recipient actively chooses to use.

Mad Graphics supplies personalised merchandise and promotional items to East Sussex businesses, event organisers, and charities from our Polegate base. We source products from established trade suppliers and manage the branding process from artwork to delivery.

The range covers the full breadth of common promotional items: ceramic and travel mugs, branded pens and pencils, notebooks and pads, tote bags and reusable shopping bags, lanyards, keyrings, USB drives, umbrellas, and much more. If you have a specific product in mind, we can generally source it through our supplier network.

We work with businesses across every sector in East Sussex — professional services firms ordering desk items for client meetings, hospitality businesses creating branded merchandise for retail, trade associations equipping exhibition stands, and community events producing branded giveaways on a limited budget.

Our in-house design team can prepare your artwork for any product format, adapting logo files to the print area specifications of each item and providing proofs for your approval before production begins.

We serve merchandise customers across Eastbourne, Hastings, Lewes, Bexhill-on-Sea, Seaford, Hailsham, Newhaven, Polegate, and the wider East Sussex area.

## How It Works

### 1. Product Selection & Quote
Tell us the items you're interested in, approximate quantities, and your artwork or brief. We'll confirm availability, minimum quantities, and provide a written quote.

### 2. Artwork Preparation & Proof
We prepare your artwork to the product's print area specifications. A digital proof is provided for your approval. For colour-critical orders, physical samples can be requested prior to full production.

### 3. Production & Delivery
Orders are placed with our trade suppliers once artwork is approved. We manage the production and quality check process. Most orders arrive within ten to fifteen working days. We'll give you a confirmed lead time at the quote stage.
