## Directional & Wayfinding Signs

Good wayfinding signage is invisible in the best possible way — it answers the question a visitor is about to ask before they have to ask it. Poor wayfinding does the opposite: it creates confusion, generates wasted staff time, and leaves visitors with a negative first impression before they have even reached their destination.

Mad Graphics produces directional and wayfinding sign systems for business parks, industrial estates, healthcare premises, office buildings, car parks, hospitality venues, and any multi-unit or multi-area premises across East Sussex. We design complete systems from scratch and produce individual signs to match and extend existing installations.

A wayfinding project typically involves three categories of sign working together. Arrival signs establish that the visitor is in the right place and provide an orientation overview. Directional signs along routes confirm the path with arrows and distance indicators. Destination signs — room numbers, unit names, department labels, door plates — confirm that the visitor has arrived.

All three categories need to be visually consistent for a wayfinding system to work. That means consistent typography, consistent colour use, consistent arrow conventions, and consistent mounting heights and positions. We design wayfinding systems as a coherent whole, not as a collection of individual signs.

External wayfinding — car park directions, estate road signs, entrance identifiers — is produced on aluminium composite and aluminium post systems rated for outdoor exposure. Internal signs use substrates suited to wall mounting: acrylic, brushed aluminium-faced panels, and rigid PVC, depending on the environment and finish requirement.

## How It Works

### 1. Site Walk & Navigation Brief
We assess the site or receive your site plan. We map the visitor journey — from arrival to destination — and identify the sign positions needed to guide navigation at each decision point.

### 2. System Design & Schedule
Our studio designs the sign types, sizes, and graphic format for the system and produces a sign schedule with quantities and positions. A visual proof of each sign type is presented for approval.

### 3. Production
Signs are produced in-house to the approved design. Projects are scheduled in sequence to allow logical installation and minimise disruption to the premises.

### 4. Installation
Our team installs signs at the agreed positions. External post-mounted signs and internal wall plates are fitted to a clean, level standard. We check the complete navigation flow on completion.
