faqs:
  - question: "What types of directional signs do you create?"
    answer: "We design and manufacture a full range of directional signage including wall-mounted wayfinding signs, post-mounted exterior directional signs, hanging directory boards, floor graphics, and digital-ready directional displays. All signs can be customised to match your brand and architectural requirements."
  - question: "How much do directional signs typically cost?"
    answer: "Directional signage projects typically range from £200-£5,000 depending on the number of signs, materials, and complexity. Simple interior directional signs start around £50-£150 each, whilst comprehensive wayfinding systems for larger facilities require detailed quotation based on your specific requirements."
  - question: "What materials do you use for directional signs?"
    answer: "We use UV-rated materials including aluminium composite panels, dibond, acrylic, and weather-resistant vinyl graphics. For exterior applications, we select materials specifically rated for long-term outdoor exposure. Interior signs can utilise a wider range of finishes including brushed metals and premium acrylics."
  - question: "How long does it take to design and install directional signs?"
    answer: "Simple directional signs can be completed within 5-7 working days. Comprehensive wayfinding systems typically require 2-3 weeks including design consultation, artwork approval, and installation. We'll provide a detailed timeline during your initial consultation based on project scope."
  - question: "Do you provide design services for directional signage?"
    answer: "Yes, we offer complete in-house design and artwork services. Our experienced designers will work with you to create directional signage that matches your brand guidelines, meets accessibility requirements, and provides clear, intuitive wayfinding for your specific location and user needs."
  - question: "Can you install directional signs on listed buildings?"
    answer: "We have extensive experience working with heritage and listed buildings throughout East Sussex. We'll advise on appropriate fixing methods and materials that comply with conservation requirements whilst providing effective directional signage. Planning permission guidance can be provided where necessary."
  - question: "Are your directional signs compliant with accessibility requirements?"
    answer: "Yes, we design directional signage to meet current accessibility guidelines including appropriate contrast ratios, font sizes, and positioning heights. We can incorporate tactile elements, Braille, and pictographic symbols where required to ensure your wayfinding system is accessible to all users."
  - question: "What's included in your directional signage service?"
    answer: "Our complete service includes site survey, design consultation, artwork creation, material specification, manufacturing, and professional installation. We also provide maintenance advice and can offer ongoing support for larger wayfinding systems including updates and additions as your facility evolves."
  - question: "Do you offer temporary directional signage for events?"
    answer: "Yes, we create temporary directional signage for events, construction projects, and short-term requirements. Options include portable A-frame signs, banner displays, and temporary post-mounted directional signs. These can be hired or purchased depending on your event frequency and requirements."
  - question: "How do you ensure directional signs will withstand weather conditions?"
    answer: "We specify UV-rated materials and weatherproof fixings for all exterior directional signage. Our 20+ years experience in East Sussex coastal conditions means we understand local weather challenges and select appropriate materials and installation methods to ensure long-lasting performance."
  - question: "Can you update existing directional signage systems?"
    answer: "Absolutely. We can assess your current directional signage and provide updates, additions, or complete system overhauls. Whether you need individual sign replacements or a comprehensive wayfinding system upgrade, we'll work with existing infrastructure where possible to minimise costs and disruption."
  - question: "Do you provide public liability insurance for installation work?"
    answer: "Yes, we carry full public liability insurance for all installation work. This covers our team and your property during the installation process. We can provide insurance certificates upon request and ensure all work is completed to professional trade standards with appropriate safety measures."
