  - question: "How much do shop signs typically cost?"
    answer: "Shop signs typically range from £200-£1,500 depending on size, materials and complexity. Simple acrylic signs start around £200, whilst illuminated fascia signs can cost £800-£1,500. We provide free quotes with no obligation."
  - question: "Do you need planning permission for shop signs?"
    answer: "Most shop signs fall under permitted development rights, but larger signs or those in conservation areas may need planning permission. We can advise on local East Sussex planning requirements and help with applications if needed."

    answer: "Simple signs like A-boards take 3-5 working days, shop signs typically take 1-2 weeks, and larger site boards or hoardings take 2-3 weeks. Installation is usually completed within 1-2 days depending on complexity."
