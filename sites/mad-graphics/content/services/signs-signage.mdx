## Signs & Signage for East Sussex Businesses

Mad Graphics produces the full range of business signage from our Polegate workshop — from a single A-board to a complete site hoarding installation. Everything is designed and printed in-house, which means faster turnaround, more consistent quality, and a direct line to the team if anything needs adjusting.

We work with businesses of all sizes across East Sussex. Independent retailers need shop fascias that attract passing trade. Construction companies need site boards that meet planning requirements and project a professional image. Hospitality businesses need menus, directional signs, and window graphics that reflect their brand without requiring a full refit.

Our team can take a brief from scratch or work from your existing brand guidelines. If you have artwork ready, we can go straight to production. If you need design work first, our in-house studio handles that too.

We serve customers across Eastbourne, Hastings, Lewes, Bexhill-on-Sea, Seaford, Hailsham, Uckfield, Newhaven, Polegate, Peacehaven, and the wider East Sussex area.
