## Fabric Banners & Flags

Fabric print has become the display material of choice for premium indoor events, hospitality venues, and brand-conscious businesses that want something a little more refined than standard PVC. At Mad Graphics, we produce fabric banners for indoor use and feather flags for outdoor display, serving clients across Eastbourne, Hastings, Lewes, Bexhill-on-Sea, Seaford, and the whole of East Sussex.

Fabric banners are dye-sublimation printed, which means the ink is bonded into the fibres of the material rather than sitting on the surface. The result is vivid, saturated colour with a soft, textile finish that looks excellent in conference rooms, hotel lobbies, retail environments, and branded event spaces. Fabric banners are also significantly lighter than PVC, which makes them easier to hang, store, and transport between events.

Feather flags — also known as flutter flags or swooper flags — are the outdoor counterpart. Their distinctive teardrop or feather shape means they catch the eye on a busy forecourt, at a roadside location, or along an event perimeter. The flexible pole allows the flag to move with the wind rather than resisting it, which protects both the print and the mounting hardware. We supply flags with ground spike, water base, and car-boot mount options to suit your specific installation.

Both products are reusable and designed to last. Fabric banners can be carefully washed and stored flat or rolled for the next event. Feather flags pack down into a compact carry bag with the pole sections disassembled.

Popular uses include car dealership forecourts, garden centres, sports clubs, leisure centres, outdoor markets, and any business or organisation that needs branded outdoor presence during events or seasonal promotions across East Sussex.
