faqs:
  - question: "What is the typical cost for fabric banners?"
    answer: "Fabric banner pricing typically ranges from £50-£500 depending on size, complexity, and quantity. We provide detailed quotes based on your specific requirements including dimensions, design complexity, and finishing options."
  - question: "How long does it take to produce fabric banners?"
    answer: "Standard production time is 3-5 working days from artwork approval. Rush orders can often be accommodated within 24-48 hours for an additional charge, depending on current workload and banner specifications."
  - question: "What sizes are available for fabric banners?"
    answer: "We can produce fabric banners in virtually any size up to 5 metres wide. Popular sizes include 2m x 1m, 3m x 2m, and custom exhibition booth sizes. We'll advise on optimal dimensions for your specific application."
  - question: "Are fabric banners suitable for outdoor use?"
    answer: "Our fabric banners are specifically designed for indoor use and exhibition environments. For outdoor applications, we recommend our PVC banners or other weather-resistant materials that can withstand wind, rain, and UV exposure."
  - question: "Do you provide design services for fabric banners?"
    answer: "Yes, we offer comprehensive in-house design and artwork services. Our experienced team can create professional designs from scratch or work with your existing brand guidelines to produce exhibition-quality graphics."
  - question: "What finishing options are available?"
    answer: "We offer various finishing options including hemmed edges, eyelets, pole pockets, and Velcro strips. The choice depends on your display method and mounting requirements. We'll recommend the best finishing for your specific application."
  - question: "Can you print from my existing artwork?"
    answer: "Yes, we can work with your existing artwork provided it meets our technical specifications. We require high-resolution files (minimum 150dpi at full size) in formats such as PDF, AI, or high-quality JPEG. We'll check artwork quality before printing."
  - question: "How do I care for and clean fabric banners?"
    answer: "Fabric banners can be gently cleaned with a damp cloth and mild detergent. Avoid harsh chemicals or abrasive cleaning. Store rolled or folded carefully to prevent creasing. With proper care, they'll maintain their quality through multiple uses."
  - question: "Do you offer installation services?"
    answer: "We can arrange installation for larger projects or complex displays. For standard fabric banners, installation is typically straightforward and can be handled by your team. We provide clear instructions and mounting hardware where required."
  - question: "What's the minimum order quantity?"
    answer: "We can produce single fabric banners with no minimum order quantity. However, larger quantities often benefit from economies of scale, reducing the per-unit cost for multiple banners with similar specifications."
  - question: "Are you insured for commercial projects?"
    answer: "Yes, we carry full public liability insurance and have been established since 2004. We're experienced in working with commercial clients and understand the importance of professional standards and reliable delivery timescales."
  - question: "Can fabric banners be reprinted or updated?"
    answer: "Yes, we keep digital files of all artwork, making it easy to reprint banners or create updated versions with new information. This is particularly useful for seasonal campaigns or events with changing details while maintaining consistent branding."
