## Van Graphics & Van Signwriting

A well-lettered van is one of the most cost-effective forms of advertising available to a local business. It puts your name, trade, and contact details in front of thousands of potential customers every day — in every street, car park, and job site your vehicle visits.

Mad Graphics has been producing van graphics from our Polegate workshop since 2004. We work with sole traders, small businesses, and growing East Sussex companies that need one van or a dozen branded consistently.

We specialise in cut vinyl lettering and digitally printed panel graphics. Cut vinyl is the right choice when you need clean, sharp text and logos — it sits flat on the panel, resists fading, and comes off cleanly when you change vehicles. Printed graphics allow photographic imagery, graduated colours, and more complex designs to be applied across doors, sides, and rear panels.

All vinyl we use is rated for exterior use and UV exposure. Printed graphics are laminated before application to resist road wash and East Sussex coastal salt air. Application is carried out in-house by the same team that produces the graphics — there is no handoff to a third-party fitter.

A typical job includes your business name, trade or service description, phone number, and website address. We can match your existing brand colours and reproduce your logo at the correct sizes for each panel. If you need design work from scratch, our in-house studio will produce a layout for your approval before anything goes to print.

Most single-van jobs are completed within one to two days of artwork sign-off. We serve customers regularly from Eastbourne, Hastings, Lewes, Bexhill-on-Sea, Seaford, Hailsham, and across the wider East Sussex area.

## How It Works

### 1. Quote & Brief
Tell us your van make and model, what information you want displayed, and whether you have existing artwork or need design from scratch. We will provide a written quote, usually the same day.

### 2. Design & Approval
Our studio produces a scaled layout showing exactly how your graphics will sit on each panel. We send this for your approval before production begins. Amendments are included at this stage.

### 3. Production
Once approved, graphics are cut or printed and laminated in-house. Lead time from sign-off to ready-to-fit is typically one working day for cut vinyl and two days for printed graphics.

### 4. Application
Bring your van to our Polegate workshop. Application is carried out by our own team and typically takes two to four hours depending on the size and complexity of the job. Most customers collect the same day.
