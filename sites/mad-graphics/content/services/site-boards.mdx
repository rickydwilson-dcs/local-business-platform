## Site Boards

Site boards serve a practical function — identifying a property, marking a development, meeting planning conditions, or advertising a build to passing trade — and they do it in all weathers, often for months at a time. The quality of the substrate and print process determines whether a board still looks professional on week twelve or has faded, warped, and peeled by week four.

Mad Graphics produces site boards for construction companies, housebuilders, developers, and estate agents across East Sussex from our Polegate workshop. We have supplied site boards for residential developments, commercial builds, renovation projects, and estate agency instructions in Eastbourne, Hastings, Lewes, Bexhill-on-Sea, Uckfield, Hailsham, Pevensey, Ringmer, and across the county.

We produce on correx and foamex substrates, both with UV-laminated print. Correx is the standard choice for estate agent boards and short-term site identification — it is lightweight, post-mounted easily, and cost-effective for high-volume or frequently changed instructions. Foamex is the better choice for longer-term hoardings, development branding, and any application where the board needs to hold its shape over an extended period on a fixed frame.

Boards can carry your company branding, planning notice information, contact details, property particulars, or a combination. We design from your brief or produce to your existing template.

Turnaround is typically two to three working days from artwork sign-off, which suits most site board requirements. For urgent requirements — planning condition compliance, fast-moving sales instructions — we can discuss accelerated production at the quoting stage.
