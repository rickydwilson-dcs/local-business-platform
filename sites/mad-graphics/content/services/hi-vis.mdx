## Hi-Vis & Safety Workwear

Branded hi-vis workwear serves two purposes simultaneously. It keeps your team safe and visible on site, and it puts your business name in front of everyone else on that site — site managers, other contractors, clients, and passers-by. A crew in consistently branded hi-vis looks organised and professional in a way that unmarked safety gear never does.

Mad Graphics has supplied branded hi-vis and safety workwear to East Sussex tradespeople and contractors from our Polegate studio since 2004. We work with sole traders needing a handful of branded vests, small contracting firms equipping a team, and larger construction and utilities businesses ordering across multiple garment types.

We produce print and embroidery on hi-vis vests, jackets, polos, and bib-and-brace garments. The key consideration with certified hi-vis garments is placement — branding must not cover the retro-reflective tape or reduce the fluorescent panel area required by EN ISO 20471 certification. We understand these requirements and design placements accordingly, so your garments remain compliant after branding.

For hi-vis print work we commonly use vinyl transfer printing, which produces clean, durable results on the high-visibility fabric without the issues that can arise with direct digital printing on fluorescent materials. Embroidery is available for a premium finish on heavier garments such as jackets and fleeces.

We can supply certified hi-vis garments through our trade accounts and brand them in-house, or brand garments you supply. We serve trades customers across Eastbourne, Hastings, Lewes, Bexhill-on-Sea, Seaford, Hailsham, and across East Sussex.

## How It Works

### 1. Brief & Garment Selection
Tell us the garment types, EN class requirement, sizes, quantities, and your artwork. We'll recommend garment options and print or embroidery method, and quote accordingly.

### 2. Artwork & Placement Proof
We prepare your artwork and show you exactly where logos will be placed on the garment, confirming that placement meets certification requirements. Proof provided for your approval.

### 3. Production
Garments are branded in-house using the agreed method. Standard lead time is five to seven working days from sign-off, subject to garment availability.

### 4. Quality Check & Collection
Finished garments are checked before packing. Collect from our Polegate workshop or arrange delivery.
