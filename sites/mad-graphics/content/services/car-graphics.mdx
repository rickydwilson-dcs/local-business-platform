## Car Graphics & Lettering

Cars are increasingly used as branded business vehicles — by estate agents, letting agencies, mortgage advisers, therapists, cleaning companies, and tradespeople who use a car rather than a van. A set of well-applied door graphics or a rear window print turns a company car into a working advert in every car park and street it visits.

Mad Graphics has been producing car graphics from our Polegate workshop since 2004. We specialise in cut vinyl lettering and digitally printed graphics — not full wraps. For most business purposes, targeted, well-designed graphics on doors, tailgates, and rear windows deliver exactly the visibility a branded car needs, without the cost or complication of a complete colour change.

Cut vinyl is well suited to cars. It sits flat on curved door panels, produces crisp edges on text and logos, and can be removed cleanly at the end of a lease or when a vehicle changes hands. Printed graphics allow more complex designs with graduated colour, photography, and larger logo reproductions, typically on rear windows or full door panels.

We work from your existing brand guidelines or design from scratch if you need it. Artwork is proofed before production and every job is fitted by our own team at the workshop.

Common applications include door lettering with business name and phone number, rear window graphics with a website or tagline, and bonnet or roof graphics for additional visibility in car parks and from elevated positions on site.

We serve customers from Eastbourne, Hastings, Lewes, Bexhill-on-Sea, Seaford, Hailsham, Uckfield, and across East Sussex.

## How It Works

### 1. Quote & Artwork Brief
Tell us your car make, model, and colour, and share your logo or brand details. We'll quote the job and confirm what design work is needed.

### 2. Design Approval
We produce a scaled visual of how graphics will look on your specific vehicle and send it for sign-off. No production begins without your approval.

### 3. Production
Graphics are cut or printed and laminated in-house. Most car graphics are ready to fit within one to two working days of artwork approval.

### 4. Application
Drop your car at our Polegate workshop. Most jobs are completed within half a day to a full working day. You will be contacted when it is ready to collect.

## Why Choose Mad Graphics?

Car graphics require a lighter touch than van work — panels are smaller, curves are tighter, and the tolerances on a well-maintained company car are tighter than on a commercial vehicle. We have been applying graphics to cars across East Sussex since 2004 and understand the preparation, tool techniques, and material weights that produce clean results on automotive paintwork.

There are no subcontracted fitters. The people who design and print your graphics are the same people who apply them, which means any detail that matters at the design stage carries through to the finished vehicle.
