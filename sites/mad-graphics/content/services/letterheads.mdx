## Letterheads & Compliment Slips

Branded stationery communicates something important: that your business takes presentation seriously. A professionally printed letterhead tells the recipient, before they read a word of the letter, that they are dealing with an established and organised business. It is one of the simplest ways to project credibility and brand consistency across every piece of formal correspondence you send.

At Mad Graphics, we produce letterheads and compliment slips for businesses of all sizes across East Sussex. Whether you are a sole trader needing a small quantity of stationery for client proposals, or an established company needing a regular supply of letterhead for ongoing correspondence, we can supply what you need from our Polegate workshop.

Letterheads are printed on quality uncoated bond paper — 100gsm is the standard weight for correspondence, with heavier weights available for clients who want a more substantial feel. The uncoated surface is important: it accepts handwriting and overprinting from laser and inkjet printers without issue, which is essential for stationery that will be used with in-house office printers.

Compliment slips follow the same brand design as the letterhead but are formatted to DL size — one-third of A4. They are used to accompany invoices, samples, brochures, or any small parcel or package where a brief, branded note is appropriate. They carry more presence than a handwritten sticky note and keep brand consistency across every customer touchpoint.

If you have an existing brand identity, we work from your logo and brand guidelines. If you are a new business or are refreshing your brand, our design team creates stationery designs that are clean, professional, and consistent across your full stationery suite.

Quantities from 100 to several thousand are available, with competitive pricing at larger volumes.
