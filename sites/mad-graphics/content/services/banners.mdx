## Banners & Displays for Events and Promotions

Mad Graphics prints banners, roller banners, mesh banners, and fabric flags for businesses, event organisers, and community groups across East Sussex. All printing is done in-house at our Polegate studio, which means we can turn around urgent orders quickly and maintain consistent quality across repeat orders.

Banners are one of the most cost-effective ways to get a business noticed — at a trade show, outside a shop, at a community event, or on a construction hoarding. We print in vibrant colour on materials suited to the intended use: heavy-duty PVC for outdoor longevity, mesh for windy locations, fabric for indoor events and exhibition stands, and cassette-loaded vinyl for roller banner systems.

Whether you need a single pull-up banner for a meeting or fifty PVC banners for a national promotion, we can quote and produce to your schedule. Our design team can work from your brief or adapt supplied artwork to the correct dimensions.

We serve customers across Eastbourne, Hastings, Lewes, Bexhill-on-Sea, Seaford, Hailsham, Uckfield, Crowborough, Newhaven, Polegate, Peacehaven, and surrounding areas of East Sussex.

## Why Choose Mad Graphics?

We've been printing for East Sussex businesses for over 20 years, and banners are one of our most requested products. We know what works outdoors on the East Sussex coast — where UV exposure and sea air degrade cheaper materials faster than inland — and we spec materials accordingly.

All our banner printing uses UV-stable inks and we apply additional reinforcement to eyelet points as standard, because that's where most banners fail in windy conditions. Roller banner mechanisms are sourced from reputable hardware suppliers with replacement cassette compatibility.

You deal directly with the production team, not an intermediary. Queries about artwork, sizing, or turnaround get answered by the people doing the work.
