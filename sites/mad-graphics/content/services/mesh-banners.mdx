## Mesh Banners

When a banner needs to span a scaffold frame, cover a building hoarding, or hang in an exposed coastal or hilltop location, solid PVC is often not the right choice. Wind pressure builds against solid sheet material and can cause catastrophic failure — putting stress on fixings, scaffold clamps, and the banner itself. Mesh banners solve this problem by allowing air to pass through the material while still delivering a bold, readable printed graphic.

At Mad Graphics, we produce mesh banners for construction companies, developers, local authorities, and businesses carrying out refurbishment works across East Sussex. Our clients include contractors in Eastbourne, Hastings, Lewes, Bexhill-on-Sea, Uckfield, Crowborough, and across the region.

The material is a perforated woven mesh, typically printed at around 50% coverage. At the viewing distances typical of scaffolding — pavement to first or second storey — the perforations are effectively invisible and the design reads as a solid image. This means you get strong visual impact alongside the structural safety benefits of reduced wind resistance.

We print mesh banners in full colour with UV-resistant, weatherproof inks. The material is designed for long-term outdoor exposure and will not degrade quickly under normal British weather conditions. Eyelets are welded at regular intervals so you can fix securely with cable ties, bungee cords, or scaffold-specific fittings.

Common uses include contractor branding on building sites, developer project promotion on hoarding fences, temporary advertising around venue refurbishments, and event perimeter signage at exposed coastal or open-ground locations across East Sussex.

We produce mesh banners to any custom size — from a few square metres to large multi-storey installations. Provide us with your measurements and we will prepare a quote and artwork proof.
