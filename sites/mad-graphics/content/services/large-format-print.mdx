## Large Format Printing in East Sussex

Mad Graphics operates large format print production from our Polegate workshop, producing everything from A1 posters to exhibition backdrops, canvas prints, foam board displays, and correx boards. All printing is done on our in-house wide-format equipment using pigment inks for colour accuracy and UV stability.

Large format printing covers a wide range of end uses — retail point-of-sale, exhibition and conference displays, photographic prints for interiors, construction site information boards, and short-run poster campaigns. We work with individual customers, businesses, event organisers, and trade clients who need reliable output on tight schedules.

The advantage of in-house production is speed and quality control. We check every file before it goes to print, proof colour-critical work, and inspect output before it leaves the workshop. If something isn't right, we can reprint and still make your deadline.

We serve customers across Eastbourne, Hastings, Lewes, Bexhill-on-Sea, Seaford, Hailsham, Uckfield, Crowborough, Newhaven, Polegate, and the wider East Sussex area.

We've been producing large format print for East Sussex clients since 2004. In that time we've calibrated our equipment and workflows around the kinds of jobs local businesses actually need — fast-turnaround retail posters, exhibition graphics that need to pack flat, and correx site boards that hold up in Sussex weather.
