    answer: "Contact us with your requirements including size, material preference, quantity, and deadline. We'll provide a free, detailed quote typically within 24 hours. You can visit our Polegate workshop or send files electronically for assessment."
  - question: "What's the difference between foam board and correx for displays?"
    answer: "Foam board is lightweight and ideal for indoor displays with a smooth, professional finish. Correx is waterproof plastic sheeting perfect for outdoor use and temporary signage. We'll recommend the best material based on your intended application and environment."
