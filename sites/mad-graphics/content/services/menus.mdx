## Menus & Price Lists

The menu is one of the most important pieces of print a hospitality business produces. It is handled by every customer, consulted multiple times per visit, and read carefully in a way that few other print items are. A well-designed, professionally printed menu enhances the dining experience and reflects the quality of the food and service it represents.

At Mad Graphics, we have produced menus, price lists, and hospitality print for cafés, restaurants, pubs, hotels, and takeaways across East Sussex for over twenty years. We work with venues in Eastbourne, Hastings, Bexhill-on-Sea, Lewes, Seaford, Hailsham, Polegate, Battle, St Leonards-on-Sea, and across the region — from independent local restaurants to multi-site hospitality operators.

Menu formats we produce include single-sheet A4 and A5 menus, folded bifold and trifold formats, wiro-bound and tabbed menu collections for larger restaurants, and large-format printed boards for wall mounting. For café and counter environments, we produce A4 and A3 laminated price lists that sit in frames, clip into holders, or are fixed to counters and boards.

Lamination is strongly recommended for menus in food service environments. A double-sided laminated menu is fully encapsulated, wipe-clean, and significantly more durable than an unlaminated sheet. This matters enormously for venues that go through multiple covers per service and need menus that look presentable for weeks rather than days.

We also produce specials boards, daily menu inserts, seasonal menu updates, and event menu cards for private dining and functions. Because food and pricing changes regularly in the hospitality sector, we make the reprint process as fast and straightforward as possible for our regular venue clients.

Whether you need ten copies of a fine dining menu or five hundred laminated price lists for a busy café, we produce them with the same attention to quality.
