faqs:
  - question: "How much does menu printing cost?"
    answer: "Menu printing typically costs between £50-£500 depending on quantity, size, and finish. Laminated A4 menus start from around £3-£5 each, whilst board menus and custom sizes vary based on specifications. We provide detailed quotes for all projects."
  - question: "How quickly can you print new menus?"
    answer: "Standard menu printing takes 3-5 working days from artwork approval. We offer fast reprint services for existing designs, often completing within 24-48 hours for urgent requirements. Rush jobs may incur additional charges."
  - question: "What menu formats do you offer?"
    answer: "We print laminated menus, rigid board menus, and custom-sized options. Popular formats include A4, A3, and bespoke sizes. We can accommodate single-page menus, folded designs, and multi-page booklet styles depending on your requirements."
  - question: "Do you provide menu design services?"
    answer: "Yes, we offer in-house design and artwork services. Our experienced team can create professional menu layouts, incorporate your branding, and optimise food photography. Design services are included in many packages or available separately."
  - question: "Are the menus suitable for restaurant use?"
    answer: "Absolutely. Our laminated menus are designed for heavy commercial use, easy to wipe clean, and resistant to spills and daily handling. We use UV-rated materials that won't fade under restaurant lighting conditions."
  - question: "Can you match our existing branding?"
    answer: "Yes, we can match your existing colour schemes, fonts, and branding elements. Provide us with your brand guidelines, logos, or existing materials, and our design team will ensure consistency across all menu materials."
  - question: "What's the minimum order quantity?"
    answer: "We accommodate orders from single menus up to large commercial quantities. There's no strict minimum order, though smaller quantities may have higher per-unit costs. Most restaurants order 20-50 menus for optimal cost efficiency."
  - question: "How do I place a menu printing order?"
    answer: "Contact us with your requirements including quantity, size, and any existing artwork. We'll provide a detailed quote and timeline. Once approved, we'll handle design (if needed), send proofs for approval, then proceed to printing."
  - question: "Can you print menus in different languages?"
    answer: "Yes, we can print menus in various languages and accommodate special characters or fonts. Our design team has experience with multilingual menus for diverse customer bases. Just provide the translated text during the design phase."
  - question: "Do you offer menu reprints for seasonal changes?"
    answer: "Absolutely. We keep your menu designs on file for easy reprints and updates. Seasonal menu changes, price updates, or new dish additions can be quickly implemented. Our fast reprint service is perfect for regular menu updates."
  - question: "What file formats do you accept for menu artwork?"
    answer: "We accept PDF, AI, EPS, and high-resolution JPG files. If you don't have print-ready artwork, our design team can work with Word documents, images, or even handwritten notes to create professional menu layouts."
  - question: "Are you covered by public liability insurance?"
    answer: "Yes, Mad Graphics carries full public liability insurance for all our printing services. We've been established since 2004 and maintain comprehensive coverage for commercial projects. Insurance certificates are available upon request for larger contracts."
