## Window Stickers & Decals

A shop or office window is one of the most valuable display spaces a business has. Window stickers and decals make that space work harder — communicating opening hours, seasonal promotions, brand identity, privacy requirements, and legal information to anyone walking or driving past.

Mad Graphics has supplied window graphics to East Sussex retailers, offices, restaurants, salons, and service businesses from our Polegate studio since 2004. We produce cut vinyl, digitally printed, frosted, and static cling window graphics in any size and shape.

Cut vinyl window stickers are the right choice for logos, text, and graphic shapes in solid colours. They produce a clean, flat finish directly on glass and are available in a range of colours. For photographic imagery, graduated backgrounds, or full-colour printed designs, we print onto a clear or white vinyl that adheres to the glass and reproduces colour artwork accurately.

Static cling vinyl is ideal for promotional messages and seasonal campaigns — it adheres to smooth glass without adhesive and can be repositioned and reused. For longer-term applications such as opening hours, privacy screening, and permanent brand graphics, adhesive vinyl provides a more durable solution.

We also produce frosted and etched-effect window vinyl for office privacy screening, meeting room glazing, and decorative glass treatment. These are produced on a translucent frosted vinyl and cut to any shape, including custom shapes, patterns, and branded graphics.

We serve window sticker customers across Eastbourne, Hastings, Lewes, Bexhill-on-Sea, Seaford, Hailsham, Newhaven, and across the wider East Sussex area.

## How It Works

### 1. Quote & Specification
Tell us your window dimensions, the information or design you need to display, and whether you need a temporary or permanent solution. We'll advise on the right material and provide a quote.

### 2. Design & Proof
We produce scaled artwork showing how the sticker or decal will appear on your window. For inside-face application, the artwork is mirrored at this stage. Proof sent for approval before production.

### 3. Production
Graphics are printed or cut in-house and prepared for application. Standard orders are ready within one to three working days from artwork sign-off.

### 4. Application
We supply full application instructions with each order. For larger or complex window installations, we can arrange fitting by our team at your premises.
