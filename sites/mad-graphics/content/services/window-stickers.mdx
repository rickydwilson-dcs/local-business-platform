faqs:
  - question: "What's the difference between static cling and adhesive window stickers?"
    answer: "Static cling stickers use static electricity to stick to glass without adhesive, making them completely removable and repositionable. Adhesive vinyl stickers use permanent or removable adhesive for stronger hold but may leave slight residue on removal."
  - question: "How much do custom window stickers cost?"
    answer: "Window stickers typically range from £50-£500 depending on size, quantity, and complexity. Small business hour signs start around £50, whilst large promotional displays can reach £500. We provide free quotes for all projects."
  - question: "How long do window stickers last outdoors?"
    answer: "Our UV-rated materials are designed for 3-5 years outdoor performance without significant fading or deterioration. Indoor applications can last much longer as they're protected from weather and UV exposure."
  - question: "Can you create custom shapes and sizes?"
    answer: "Yes, we can cut window stickers to any custom shape or size using our precision cutting equipment. Whether you need circular logos, complex die-cut shapes, or standard rectangles, we can accommodate your requirements."
  - question: "Do you provide design services for window stickers?"
    answer: "Absolutely. Our in-house design team has 20+ years experience creating effective window graphics. We can work from your existing artwork or create completely new designs that suit your brand and objectives."
  - question: "How quickly can window stickers be ready?"
    answer: "Standard turnaround is 3-5 working days from artwork approval. Rush jobs can often be completed within 24-48 hours for an additional fee, depending on complexity and our current workload."
  - question: "Will static cling stickers work on textured glass?"
    answer: "Static cling works best on smooth, clean glass surfaces. For textured or frosted glass, we recommend adhesive vinyl options which provide better contact and adhesion to irregular surfaces."
  - question: "Can window stickers be applied to vehicle windows?"
    answer: "Yes, we create window stickers suitable for vehicle applications. We ensure compliance with legal requirements for visibility and can advise on optimal placement for maximum impact whilst maintaining safety."
  - question: "Do you install window stickers or is it DIY?"
    answer: "We can provide professional installation for larger or complex projects, particularly commercial applications. Smaller stickers come with application instructions, and our team can advise on best practices for bubble-free installation."
  - question: "Are your window stickers suitable for double-glazed windows?"
    answer: "Yes, our window stickers are perfectly suitable for modern double-glazed windows. We recommend external application for maximum visibility, though internal application is possible depending on your requirements."
  - question: "What file formats do you accept for artwork?"
    answer: "We accept most common formats including PDF, AI, EPS, and high-resolution JPG or PNG files. Vector formats are preferred for best quality, but our design team can work with whatever you have available."
  - question: "Can window stickers be removed and reused?"
    answer: "Static cling stickers can be carefully removed and repositioned multiple times if handled properly. Adhesive vinyl stickers are generally single-use, though removable adhesive options allow clean removal without reuse capability."
