## Custom Stickers

Custom stickers are one of the most versatile and cost-effective branded items a business can produce. They appear on product packaging, equipment cases, promotional materials, vehicle windows, shop counters, and event giveaways. When the quality is right, they reinforce brand recognition every time someone sees them.

Mad Graphics has produced custom stickers from our Polegate studio since 2004. We cut and print in-house, which means faster turnaround and consistent quality from order to order.

We offer three main formats. Die-cut stickers are cut exactly to the outline of your artwork — the result is a clean, professional shape with no white border, ideal for brand logos and product stickers. Kiss-cut stickers are cut to shape but remain on a full backing sheet, making them easy to peel and apply individually — a popular choice for event handouts and promotional packs. Sheet stickers group multiple designs on a single sheet, useful when you need several different stickers in smaller quantities.

All stickers are produced on outdoor-grade waterproof vinyl. Printed stickers are laminated to protect against abrasion, moisture, and UV fading. This matters for product labels, vehicle window stickers, and anything that will be handled regularly or exposed to the elements.

We work with sole traders, small businesses, market traders, craft producers, and event organisers across East Sussex. Whether you need 50 stickers for a product launch or 5,000 for a regional campaign, we can scale the job accordingly and advise on the most cost-effective production method.

If you have existing artwork we can prepare it for production. If you need a design created, our in-house studio can produce it and show you a proof before anything goes to print.

## How It Works

### 1. Brief & Quote
Tell us the shape, size, quantity, and format you need. Supply your artwork or let us know if you need design. We'll provide a written quote promptly.

### 2. Artwork Preparation
We check your supplied files or produce artwork in-house. A digital proof is sent for your approval before production begins. Any amendments at this stage are included.

### 3. Print & Cut
Once approved, stickers are digitally printed, laminated, and cut using our in-house equipment. Most orders are turned around within two to three working days of sign-off.

### 4. Collection or Delivery
Collect from our Polegate workshop or arrange courier delivery. We'll confirm the most practical option when quoting.
