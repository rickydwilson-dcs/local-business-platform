## Embroidered Uniforms & Polos

Embroidered workwear carries a quality signal that printed alternatives cannot replicate. The raised, textured finish of a well-executed logo on a polo shirt or jacket communicates professionalism — whether your team is meeting clients, working front-of-house, or representing your business on site.

Mad Graphics has supplied embroidered uniforms to East Sussex businesses from our Polegate studio since 2004. We work with hospitality venues, care providers, professional services firms, estate agents, retailers, and tradespeople who want their team to look consistent and smart.

Embroidery begins with digitising — converting your logo artwork into a stitch program that tells the embroidery machine the exact sequence, direction, and density of stitches. We handle digitising in-house and create the file once; it is then used for every subsequent order at the same size, making reorders straightforward and fast.

We embroider polo shirts, T-shirts, sweatshirts, hoodies, fleeces, softshell jackets, work jackets, and caps. Garment supply is available through our trade accounts with leading workwear and corporate clothing brands. If you have garments you want embroidered, we can work on supplied stock subject to a suitability check.

Chest logos are the most common placement — typically left chest, 80–100mm wide. We also embroider sleeve logos, back yoke logos, and collar branding. Multiple placements on the same garment are common for corporate uniforms.

We serve embroidery customers across Eastbourne, Hastings, Lewes, Bexhill-on-Sea, Seaford, Hailsham, and across the wider East Sussex area.

## How It Works

### 1. Brief & Garment Selection
Tell us the garment types, sizes, quantities, and placement positions. Supply your logo artwork. We'll recommend garments and provide a quote including digitising if required.

### 2. Digitising & Proof
Your artwork is converted to a stitch file. We send a stitch-out proof for your approval before full production begins. Amendments to the stitch file are included at this stage.

### 3. Embroidery
Once approved, garments are embroidered in-house. Lead time is typically five to seven working days for standard orders. Rush options are available — ask when enquiring.

### 4. Quality Check & Collection
Finished garments are checked before packing. Collect from our Polegate workshop or arrange delivery.

## Why Choose Mad Graphics?

Embroidery quality depends on digitising. A poorly digitised file produces uneven stitching, puckering, and designs that look unprofessional on the garment. We digitise in-house and review stitch-outs before approving any file for production — so problems are caught before your order is on the machine.

We've been doing this for over 20 years. That experience shows in the quality and consistency of the finished product.
