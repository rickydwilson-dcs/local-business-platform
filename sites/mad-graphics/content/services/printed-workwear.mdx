faqs:
  - question: "What printing methods do you offer for workwear?"
    answer: "We offer screen printing for larger quantities and heat transfer for smaller runs or complex designs. Screen printing provides excellent durability for workwear that gets regular use and washing, whilst heat transfer is ideal for detailed logos or small batches."
  - question: "How much does printed workwear cost?"
    answer: "Domestic jobs typically range from £50-£500, whilst commercial projects are £200-£5,000 depending on quantity and complexity. Screen printing becomes more cost-effective for larger quantities, typically 20+ garments. We provide free quotes for all requirements."
  - question: "What types of workwear can you print on?"
    answer: "We print on t-shirts, hoodies, polo shirts, hi-vis vests, work shirts, fleeces, and most cotton or polyester workwear. Our experience includes safety workwear, corporate uniforms, and promotional clothing for various industries across East Sussex."
  - question: "How long does printed workwear take to complete?"
    answer: "Standard turnaround is 5-7 working days from artwork approval. Rush jobs can often be accommodated in 2-3 days for an additional charge. Complex multi-colour designs or large quantities may require additional time, which we'll discuss during your quote."
  - question: "Do you design artwork or do I need to provide it?"
    answer: "We offer in-house design and artwork services as part of our complete service. You can provide your logo or design, or our experienced team can create artwork from scratch. We'll ensure your design works well with the chosen printing method and garment."
  - question: "Will the prints withstand washing and workplace conditions?"
    answer: "Yes, we use UV-rated materials and professional-grade inks designed for workwear durability. Our prints are tested to withstand regular industrial washing, outdoor exposure, and typical workplace wear. Screen printed items are particularly robust for heavy-duty use."
  - question: "Can you print on hi-vis safety workwear?"
    answer: "Absolutely. We have specialist experience printing on hi-vis garments whilst maintaining their safety compliance. We understand the requirements for reflective tape placement and ensure prints don't compromise the garment's safety certification."
  - question: "What's the minimum order quantity for printed workwear?"
    answer: "There's no strict minimum, but screen printing becomes cost-effective from around 20 pieces. For smaller quantities, heat transfer is often more economical. We'll recommend the best printing method based on your quantity and budget requirements."
  - question: "Do you offer delivery across East Sussex?"
    answer: "Yes, we provide delivery throughout East Sussex from our Polegate base. Local delivery is often included in larger orders, and we can arrange collection for smaller jobs. We've been serving the local area since 2004 with reliable service."
  - question: "Can you match specific brand colours for workwear printing?"
    answer: "Yes, we can match Pantone colours and specific brand requirements. Our in-house expertise ensures colour consistency across your workwear range. We'll provide samples if needed to ensure the colours meet your brand standards before proceeding with the full order."
  - question: "Are you insured for commercial workwear printing?"
    answer: "Yes, we carry full public liability insurance for all our printing services. This covers our work on your premises if required and provides peace of mind for commercial clients. We've been established since 2004 with a solid reputation for reliable service."
  - question: "How do I get a quote for printed workwear?"
