## Printed Workwear

Branded workwear does two jobs at once. It makes your team look professional and consistent on site, in the shop, or on customer premises. And it puts your business name in front of every person your team interacts with throughout the working day — a quiet but effective form of marketing.

Mad Graphics has supplied printed workwear to East Sussex businesses from our Polegate studio since 2004. We work with sole traders fitting one branded hoodie, small trade teams needing ten printed T-shirts, and growing businesses ordering polo shirts and jackets across multiple job roles.

We offer both screen printing and direct-to-garment digital printing. Screen printing is the traditional method for workwear — ink is applied through colour-separated screens directly to the fabric, producing vibrant, durable results that hold up to frequent washing and heavy use. It is the most cost-effective method for larger quantities with designs that use a limited number of solid colours.

Digital printing allows full-colour and photographic designs to be printed directly onto fabric using an inkjet process. It suits complex designs with gradients or multiple colours, and is practical for short runs where screen setup costs would make screen printing expensive per unit.

We can supply garments through our trade accounts — T-shirts, polo shirts, hoodies, sweatshirts, and fleeces from leading workwear brands — and print them in-house. Or we can print on garments you supply. We'll advise at the quote stage on garment options and print method to suit your requirements.

We serve workwear customers across Eastbourne, Hastings, Lewes, Bexhill-on-Sea, Seaford, Hailsham, Newhaven, and across East Sussex.

## How It Works

### 1. Brief & Garment Selection
Tell us the garment types, sizes, quantities, and your artwork or brief. We'll recommend garment options and print method, and provide a written quote.

### 2. Artwork Preparation
We prepare your artwork for the chosen print method. A proof is provided for your approval before production begins.

### 3. Printing
Garments are printed in-house using screen or digital methods as agreed. Typical lead time is five to seven working days from sign-off for most orders.

### 4. Collection or Delivery
Collect from our Polegate workshop or arrange delivery. We check quality before packing.
