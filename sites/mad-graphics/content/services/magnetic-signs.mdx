faqs:
  - question: "How much do magnetic signs cost?"
    answer: "Our magnetic signs start from £50 per pair for standard sizes. Pricing depends on size, quantity, and design complexity. We provide free quotes with no obligation - contact us with your requirements for accurate pricing."
  - question: "What sizes of magnetic signs can you produce?"
    answer: "We create custom magnetic signs in any size up to 1200mm wide. Popular sizes include 300mm x 600mm for car doors and 400mm x 800mm for van sides. We'll recommend the optimal size for your vehicle and visibility needs."
  - question: "How long do magnetic signs last?"
    answer: "With proper care, our UV-rated magnetic signs typically last 3-5 years. The high-quality materials resist fading, cracking, and weather damage. Regular cleaning and proper storage when not in use will maximise their lifespan."
  - question: "Will magnetic signs damage my vehicle?"
    answer: "No, properly applied magnetic signs won't damage your vehicle's paintwork. They're designed to be completely removable without leaving residue or marks. This makes them ideal for lease vehicles or when you want to preserve resale value."
  - question: "How quickly can you produce magnetic signs?"
    answer: "Standard magnetic signs are typically ready within 3-5 working days from artwork approval. Rush orders can often be accommodated for an additional charge. We'll confirm exact timescales when you place your order."
  - question: "Do you provide design services?"
    answer: "Yes, we offer complete in-house design and artwork services. Our experienced team can create professional designs that work effectively on magnetic signs, ensuring your branding looks sharp and readable at driving speeds."
  - question: "Can magnetic signs be used at motorway speeds?"
    answer: "Our magnetic signs are designed for normal driving conditions including dual carriageways. For regular motorway use, we recommend additional safety measures or consider our permanent vehicle graphics options for maximum security."
  - question: "How do I apply and remove magnetic signs?"
    answer: "Simply clean the vehicle surface, position the sign, and smooth out any air bubbles. To remove, gently peel from one corner. Clean both the sign and vehicle surface regularly to maintain strong adhesion and prevent dirt buildup."
  - question: "What file formats do you need for artwork?"
    answer: "We accept most common formats including AI, EPS, PDF, and high-resolution JPG or PNG files. If you don't have suitable artwork, our design team can create professional graphics from your logo and requirements."

    answer: "Yes, we provide delivery throughout East Sussex and surrounding areas. Local delivery is often free for orders over £100. We can also arrange collection from our Polegate workshop if preferred."
  - question: "Can I get matching permanent vehicle graphics later?"
    answer: "Absolutely. Many customers start with magnetic signs to test their design, then order matching permanent vehicle graphics. We keep your artwork on file making it easy to maintain consistent branding across your fleet."
  - question: "Are your magnetic signs suitable for curved surfaces?"
    answer: "Our magnetic signs work best on flat or gently curved metal surfaces. For vehicles with complex curves or non-magnetic panels, we can recommend alternative solutions like our custom vehicle graphics that conform to any surface shape."
