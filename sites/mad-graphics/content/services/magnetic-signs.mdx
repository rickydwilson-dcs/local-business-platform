## Magnetic Vehicle Signs

Magnetic vehicle signs are the practical choice for businesses that use personal vehicles for work, operate part-time, or want the flexibility to remove branding when a vehicle is used privately. They attach to any steel-bodied car or van in seconds, display exactly the same professional branding as permanent graphics, and come off just as quickly when the working day ends.

Mad Graphics produces magnetic signs in-house at our Polegate workshop. We print onto commercial-grade magnetic sheet with a UV-laminated face, which gives the same print quality and weather resistance as permanent vinyl graphics while remaining fully removable.

A typical magnetic sign for a van door includes a business name, trade or service description, phone number, and logo. We produce pairs as standard — one for each front door, or one for each rear door depending on your preference. Larger panels for the sides of vans are also available.

Magnetic signs are a good starting point for businesses that are not yet ready to commit to permanent vehicle graphics. They are also popular as a cost-effective way to brand a second or personal vehicle without permanently lettering it.

Correct use matters. We advise every customer on how to store and clean their signs to prevent moisture build-up between the magnet and the vehicle surface, which is the most common cause of minor paint marking.

We produce magnetic signs for customers across Eastbourne, Hastings, Lewes, Bexhill-on-Sea, Seaford, Hailsham, Uckfield, Newhaven, Polegate, and the wider East Sussex area.

## How It Works

### 1. Quote & Design
Tell us your vehicle type and panel dimensions — or bring it in and we will measure. Share your artwork or brief us on your brand and we will produce a design.

### 2. Artwork Approval
We send a scaled visual of the sign for your approval before production. No changes to artwork can be made after production begins.

### 3. Production
Signs are printed and laminated in-house onto magnetic sheet. Lead time is typically two to three working days from artwork sign-off.

### 4. Collection & Fitting Advice
Collect your signs from our Polegate workshop. We will show you how to attach, remove, and store them correctly to maximise paint protection and sign lifespan.
