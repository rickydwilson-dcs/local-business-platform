faqs:
  - question: "How much do floor graphics cost?"
    answer: "Floor graphics typically cost from £50 for basic designs up to £500 for complex commercial installations. Pricing depends on size, quantity, design complexity, and whether you need custom shapes. We provide free quotes with no obligation."
  - question: "Are floor graphics safe to walk on?"
    answer: "Yes, our floor graphics feature non-slip laminate surfaces specifically designed for pedestrian traffic. The anti-slip coating maintains grip even when wet, meeting health and safety requirements for commercial environments."
  - question: "How long do floor graphics last?"
    answer: "Indoor floor graphics typically last 6-12 months with normal footfall, whilst outdoor applications last 3-6 months depending on weather exposure. We use UV-rated materials to prevent fading and ensure maximum durability."
  - question: "Can you create custom shapes and sizes?"
    answer: "Absolutely. We specialise in custom floor graphics cut to any shape or size you require. Our in-house design team can create bespoke graphics to fit your exact space and brand requirements, from simple circles to complex branded shapes."

    answer: "Standard floor graphics are typically ready within 3-5 working days from artwork approval. Rush jobs can often be accommodated within 24-48 hours for an additional fee, depending on size and complexity."

    answer: "We can arrange professional installation for larger commercial projects. For smaller domestic jobs, floor graphics come with detailed application instructions and are designed for straightforward self-installation on clean, dry surfaces."
  - question: "What surfaces can floor graphics be applied to?"
    answer: "Floor graphics work on most smooth, clean surfaces including concrete, tarmac, tiles, vinyl flooring, and sealed wood. The surface must be dry and free from dust, grease, or loose material for proper adhesion."

    answer: "Yes, our floor graphics are designed for clean removal when heated gently. Temporary graphics for events or promotions can be removed without leaving residue on most surfaces, though some cleaning may be required."

  - question: "What's the difference between indoor and outdoor floor graphics?"
    answer: "Outdoor floor graphics use more aggressive adhesives and thicker laminates to withstand weather, UV exposure, and temperature changes. Indoor graphics focus on pedestrian safety with anti-slip surfaces optimised for controlled environments."
