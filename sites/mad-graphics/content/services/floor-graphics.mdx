## Floor Graphics

Floor graphics turn underused floor space into active communication. Retailers use them for promotional messaging, product wayfinding, and seasonal campaigns. Event organisers use them for branding, directional arrows, and sponsor zones. Hospitality and leisure venues use them for queue management and safety messaging. When laminated correctly and applied to a suitable surface, they are safe, effective, and easy to change.

Mad Graphics produces floor graphics from our Polegate studio using wide-format print technology and anti-skid overlaminates designed for pedestrian use. We work with retailers, event organisers, leisure venues, and businesses across East Sussex who need floor graphics that look professional, perform safely, and come off cleanly when the campaign ends.

Every floor graphic we produce is laminated with an anti-skid surface. This is not optional — it is a safety requirement for any graphic applied to a floor accessible to the public. Our anti-skid laminate meets R10 slip resistance requirements for pedestrian areas.

Floor graphics are available in custom sizes and shapes. For retail applications, a standard footprint might be 600mm × 400mm or A2 size. For large event installations covering significant floor areas, we tile the design across multiple panels that align accurately on site. Shaped floor graphics — footprints, arrows, circles, brand shapes — are produced by cutting the vinyl to outline after lamination.

We work with customers across Eastbourne, Hastings, Lewes, Bexhill-on-Sea, Seaford, Hailsham, Newhaven, Polegate, and the wider East Sussex area. Fast turnaround is available for time-critical event and retail campaign work.

## How It Works

### 1. Brief & Surface Check
Tell us your floor type, dimensions, the design you need, and when the graphics are required. We'll confirm suitability for your floor surface and provide a quote.

### 2. Design & Proof
We produce scaled artwork for your approval. For multi-panel layouts we plan the tile pattern at this stage to ensure accurate alignment on the floor.

### 3. Print, Laminate & Cut
Graphics are printed, anti-skid laminated, and cut to size or shape in-house. Standard orders are typically ready within two to three working days.

### 4. Application Guidance
We supply full fitting instructions with every order. For large or complex installations we can advise on application methods and, where required, arrange installation support.
