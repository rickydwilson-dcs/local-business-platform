## Graphic Design & Artwork Services

Mad Graphics has an in-house design studio that handles everything from logo creation and brand identity through to print-ready artwork preparation for our own production and for external printers. Design and print under one roof means your artwork goes from concept to finished product without the handoff friction that causes problems when design and production are separate.

Our designers work across the full range of outputs we produce: vehicle graphics, signage, large format displays, marketing print, workwear, and digital artwork for web and social use. That breadth means designs are built with production in mind — colours are matched to what our printers reproduce accurately, layouts respect the constraints of the substrate, and files are prepared to the correct specifications from the start.

We work with new businesses starting from nothing, established businesses refreshing an outdated identity, and businesses that have good branding but need it adapted and prepared for a specific application.

We serve clients across Eastbourne, Hastings, Lewes, Bexhill-on-Sea, Seaford, Hailsham, Uckfield, Crowborough, Newhaven, Polegate, Peacehaven, Battle, St Leonards-on-Sea, Heathfield, and the wider East Sussex area.

## Why Choose Mad Graphics?

Our design team produces artwork that works in the real world — not just on screen. Because we print what we design, we understand the difference between a colour that looks right in a PDF and one that prints consistently on vinyl, paper, and fabric. We know what minimum text sizes survive on a cut vinyl sign, how gradient blends behave on mesh banners, and how to prepare a file so it doesn't cause problems at the RIP stage.

That practical knowledge makes a difference. Customers who commission design here rarely encounter the production problems that come from artwork designed by someone who doesn't understand the print process.

We've been designing for East Sussex businesses for over 20 years. We're straightforward about what works, realistic about timelines, and clear about what's included in a project before we start.
