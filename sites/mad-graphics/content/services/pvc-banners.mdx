## PVC Banners

PVC banners remain one of the most effective and cost-efficient ways to promote your business, event, or message outdoors. At Mad Graphics, we have been producing high-quality printed banners from our Polegate workshop since 2004, serving businesses and organisations across Eastbourne, Hastings, Lewes, Bexhill-on-Sea, Seaford, Hailsham, and the whole of East Sussex.

Our PVC banner material is heavyweight and designed to withstand the British weather. Every banner we produce is printed in full colour using vibrant, UV-resistant inks that resist fading even in prolonged sunlight. Hemmed edges and welded eyelets are included as standard, giving you a banner that hangs straight, stays secure, and looks professional from the moment it goes up.

We produce banners for a huge range of applications. Retail shops use them to advertise seasonal sales and promotions. Construction companies hang them across hoardings and site fencing. Event organisers use them as entrance signage, stage backdrops, and wayfinding boards. Pubs and restaurants fix them to railings and building fronts to catch passing trade. Whatever your use case, we can print it.

Sizes range from compact display banners through to large building-front drops of 10 metres or more. There are no fixed dimensions — we cut to your exact requirements. Finishing options include banner poles, bungee cord kits, and rope threading for specific installation needs.

If you have print-ready artwork, send it over and we will get a proof to you quickly. If you are starting from scratch, our in-house design team can build your banner artwork from a brief, logo, or reference image. We will always provide a digital proof for sign-off before any job goes to print.

For bulk orders, repeat orders, and framework supply for multi-site businesses, we offer competitive pricing. Call us or email to discuss your requirements.

## How It Works

### 1. Brief & Artwork
Tell us your size, message, and any existing brand assets. Our team prepares or checks your artwork and sends a digital proof.

### 2. Proof Approval
You review the proof and approve it — or request changes. We only proceed to print once you are happy.

### 3. Print & Finish
Your banner is printed in full colour on heavyweight PVC, hemmed, and fitted with eyelets. Quality-checked before despatch.

### 4. Collection or Delivery
Collect from our Polegate workshop or arrange delivery to your site anywhere in East Sussex.
