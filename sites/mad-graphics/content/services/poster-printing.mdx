faqs:
  - question: "What sizes of posters can you print?"
    answer: "We print posters from A4 (210 x 297mm) up to A0 (841 x 1189mm), with all standard A-sizes available in between. We can also accommodate custom sizes within these dimensions to suit your specific display requirements."
  - question: "How much does poster printing cost?"
    answer: "Poster printing starts from £15 for A4 size, with larger formats priced accordingly. A3 posters typically cost £25-35, A2 around £45-65, A1 approximately £75-95, and A0 from £120-150. Final pricing depends on quantity, finish, and turnaround time required."
  - question: "Can you print posters the same day?"
    answer: "Yes, we offer same-day poster printing for orders placed before 2pm on weekdays. This service is available for standard sizes A4 to A0 in both gloss and matt finishes, subject to our production schedule and material availability."
  - question: "What's the difference between gloss and matt finish?"
    answer: "Gloss finish provides vibrant colours with a shiny surface that makes images pop, ideal for photography and colourful graphics. Matt finish offers a non-reflective surface that's easier to read under various lighting conditions and gives a more sophisticated, professional appearance."
  - question: "Do you provide design services for posters?"
    answer: "Yes, our in-house design team can create poster artwork from your brief, logos, and content. Design services are charged separately from printing, typically £50-150 depending on complexity. We can work with your existing branding or develop new concepts to meet your requirements."
  - question: "What file formats do you accept for printing?"
    answer: "We accept PDF, AI, EPS, and high-resolution JPEG or PNG files. For best results, supply artwork at 300dpi resolution in CMYK colour mode. We can also work with lower resolution files for simple text-based designs, though image quality may be affected."
  - question: "How long do the printed posters last?"
    answer: "Our posters are printed on UV-rated materials that resist fading and maintain colour quality for 12-24 months when displayed indoors. For outdoor use, expect 6-12 months depending on weather exposure. We use premium inks and papers to ensure maximum longevity."
  - question: "Can you print multiple copies of the same poster?"
    answer: "Absolutely. We offer competitive pricing for multiple copies, with discounts typically starting from 5+ copies. Bulk orders of 10, 25, 50, or 100+ copies receive progressively better pricing. Contact us for a quote based on your specific quantity requirements."
  - question: "Do you offer delivery or collection options?"
    answer: "We offer both collection from our Polegate workshop and local delivery throughout East Sussex. Collection is free, while delivery charges vary by distance and order value. For orders over £100, we often provide free local delivery within a 15-mile radius."
  - question: "What's your standard turnaround time for poster printing?"
    answer: "Standard turnaround is 2-3 working days from artwork approval. Same-day service is available for urgent orders placed before 2pm. If design work is required, allow an additional 1-2 days for artwork creation and approval before printing begins."
  - question: "Can you print waterproof posters for outdoor use?"
    answer: "Yes, we can print on synthetic waterproof materials suitable for outdoor display. These posters cost approximately 30-50% more than standard paper prints but offer superior weather resistance and durability for external applications like shop windows or notice boards."
  - question: "Do you have public liability insurance for your printing services?"
    answer: "Yes, we maintain full public liability insurance cover as an established business operating since 2004. Our 20+ years of specialist experience in print and graphics ensures professional service delivery with appropriate insurance protection for all our clients."
