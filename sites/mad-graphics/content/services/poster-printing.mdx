## Poster Printing

A well-printed poster placed in the right location is one of the most direct, cost-effective forms of marketing available to local businesses. Whether you need a single A1 print for a shop window or a run of A0 posters for an event, Mad Graphics produces poster prints in-house at our Polegate workshop, serving businesses and organisations across East Sussex since 2004.

We print on a range of quality poster papers — gloss, satin, and matt — at high resolution to ensure sharp text, accurate colour reproduction, and clean photographic detail at large sizes. Standard formats include A2, A1, and A0, and we are equally happy to print custom dimensions. If your display board, frame, or installation has specific measurements, we can cut to size.

Retail is one of the biggest users of poster print. Point-of-sale displays, window promotional graphics, seasonal campaign posters, new product announcements, and daily specials boards all benefit from a large, professionally printed image rather than a desktop-printed sheet. We serve retail clients across Eastbourne, Hastings, Bexhill-on-Sea, Lewes, Seaford, and the surrounding towns.

Hospitality is another key sector. Pubs, restaurants, cafés, hotels, and leisure venues use posters to promote events, daily menus, offers, and entertainment. We produce quick-turnaround poster runs for venues that need to update their display material regularly without a long lead time.

Event organisers, charities, schools, and community groups also come to us for poster printing — from fundraising event promotion to exhibition display panels and conference room posters.

If you have print-ready artwork, a fast turnaround is straightforward. If you need artwork created or adapted, our design team can help.

## How It Works

### 1. Artwork Submission
Send your print-ready file or brief our design team. We check resolution and colour profile before production.

### 2. Proof (if required)
For design jobs or colour-critical work, we send a digital proof for your approval.

### 3. Print & Finish
Printed in-house on quality poster paper at your chosen finish. Lamination applied if requested.

### 4. Ready Same Day or Next Day
Collect from our Polegate workshop or arrange delivery across East Sussex.
