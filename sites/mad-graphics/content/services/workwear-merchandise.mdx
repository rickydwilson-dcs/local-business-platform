## Workwear & Merchandise for East Sussex Businesses

Mad Graphics produces branded workwear, embroidered uniforms, hi-vis clothing, merchandise, and personalised gifts for businesses across East Sussex. From a single embroidered jacket to a full-team uniform rollout, we handle garment sourcing, artwork preparation, decoration, and quality checking in-house.

Workwear does two jobs simultaneously: it keeps staff looking professional and consistent, and it puts your brand in front of customers and the public throughout the working day. Getting both right means choosing the right decoration method, the right garment quality, and artwork that scales and reproduces well on fabric.

We work with trade businesses, hospitality operators, construction companies, healthcare providers, and retail businesses. Sole traders needing a few logoed polo shirts get the same care and quality as companies ordering 200 embroidered fleeces.

We supply and decorate across Eastbourne, Hastings, Lewes, Bexhill-on-Sea, Seaford, Hailsham, Uckfield, Crowborough, Newhaven, Polegate, Peacehaven, Battle, and the wider East Sussex area.
