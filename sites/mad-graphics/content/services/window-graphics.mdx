## Window Graphics & Frosted Vinyl

Glass frontages, office partitions, and shopfront windows are valuable display surfaces that many businesses leave blank. Window graphics convert that space into active branding, privacy screening, or promotional messaging — without permanent modification to the glazing.

Mad Graphics applies window graphics for retail, hospitality, professional, and commercial premises across East Sussex. We work with shops wanting printed displays, offices needing frosted glass effects on internal partitions, businesses that need privacy film on street-level windows, and premises that want cut vinyl contact details or trading hours on their front door.

Frosted vinyl is the most versatile and widely used window film we apply. It creates a diffused, translucent effect on glass that provides privacy from direct sightlines without significantly reducing natural light. It can be applied full-width across a window, as a mid-height band, or cut into patterns, text, and logos. The visual result is comparable to acid-etched glass at a fraction of the cost, and it is completely removable — which makes it appropriate for leased premises.

Cut vinyl lettering on windows is ideal for business names, opening hours, contact details, and service descriptions. It reads clearly from outside against most interior backgrounds and is a cost-effective way to get key information onto a shopfront without a printed sign.

Printed window graphics — full-colour promotional displays, seasonal campaigns, perforated vision film — are produced on our in-house large-format printer and applied to glass by our own fitters.

We serve commercial customers across Eastbourne, Hastings, Lewes, Bexhill-on-Sea, Seaford, Hailsham, Uckfield, Newhaven, and across East Sussex.

## How It Works

### 1. Site Assessment or Measurements
Tell us the window dimensions and the effect you want — frosted, cut vinyl, or printed. For bespoke designs or pattern work, we may visit to take measurements directly.

### 2. Design & Approval
We produce a visual showing the proposed graphics on your window and present it for sign-off. For frosted pattern work, a physical sample of the film finish is available on request.

### 3. Production
Graphics are cut or printed in-house. Frosted and cut vinyl graphics are typically ready within 2 to 3 working days. Printed window panels follow standard print lead times.

### 4. Application
Our fitters apply the graphics at your premises. Application is clean and precise. Most window graphics jobs are completed within a few hours with minimal disruption to trading.

## Why Choose Mad Graphics?

Window graphic application requires a different skill set from vehicle or flat sign work — glass is unforgiving of air bubbles, dust contamination, and misalignment. Our team has applied window graphics to commercial premises across East Sussex for over twenty years, and the standards we apply are consistent regardless of the size of the job.

We use film grades appropriate for each application — removable adhesives for lease-sensitive premises, permanent grades for long-term installations, UV-stabilised films for direct sun exposure. Matching the material to the application is part of the service.
