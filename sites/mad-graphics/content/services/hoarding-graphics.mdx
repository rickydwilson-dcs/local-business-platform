    answer: "Our hoarding graphics use UV-rated materials designed to maintain colour vibrancy for 2-3 years in typical UK weather conditions. The weather-resistant printing and lamination protect against rain, wind, and coastal conditions common in East Sussex."

    answer: "While we don't provide planning advice, our design team understands common local authority requirements for construction hoarding displays. We can incorporate mandatory information such as planning reference numbers, completion dates, and contact details into your graphics design."

    answer: "Our hoarding graphics are designed for temporary installation using appropriate fixings that secure the graphics without damaging the hoarding structure. Professional removal is included in our service, leaving hoardings clean and ready for reuse."
