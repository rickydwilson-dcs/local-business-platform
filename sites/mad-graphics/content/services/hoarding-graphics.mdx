## Hoarding Graphics & Hoarding Panels

A construction hoarding is unavoidable during a build programme. It interrupts the streetscape, blocks visibility, and signals to the public that there is disruption ahead. But a hoarding with well-designed graphics changes that dynamic — it tells the story of what is being built, projects your company's professionalism, and turns a temporary barrier into a piece of project marketing.

Mad Graphics produces large-format hoarding panels for construction companies, housebuilders, and commercial developers operating across East Sussex. We handle projects of all scales — from a single shop refurbishment with a short run of hoarding panels to a multi-unit residential development with graphics wrapping an entire site perimeter.

Our large-format production equipment prints at high resolution across continuous panel lengths, with no visible seaming for runs up to 1600 mm wide. For wider applications, we produce panels in sections designed for seamless joins on the hoarding frame. All panels are UV-laminated for outdoor durability.

Hoarding graphics typically carry the developer's or contractor's branding, the development name, key facts about the project, the anticipated completion date, and contact details for the responsible party. Architectural visualisations, CGIs, and planning-approved elevations are frequently incorporated where available. We can design all of this from a brief or produce to artwork you supply.

Turnaround for most hoarding panel jobs is three to five working days from artwork sign-off. For large-scale perimeter projects we will provide a production and delivery schedule at the quoting stage.

We have produced hoarding graphics for development and construction projects across Eastbourne, Hastings, Lewes, Bexhill-on-Sea, Uckfield, Hailsham, Newhaven, and across East Sussex.

## How It Works

### 1. Site Survey & Specification
Tell us the linear meterage of hoarding to be covered, the hoarding construction type, and panel fixing method. We specify the appropriate substrate and produce a quote.

### 2. Design & Layout
Our studio develops a hoarding graphic design to your brief, incorporating branding, project information, and supplied imagery. A scaled proof is produced for approval.

### 3. Large-Format Production
Panels are printed and laminated in-house. Production runs are scheduled to meet your site programme, with clear lead times confirmed at sign-off.

### 4. Delivery & Fixing Guidance
Panels are delivered to site or collected from our workshop. We provide fixing guidance for your site team or hoarding installer.
