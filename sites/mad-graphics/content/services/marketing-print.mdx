## Marketing Print for East Sussex Businesses

Mad Graphics produces marketing print for businesses across East Sussex — from sole traders needing business cards and letterheads to hospitality venues ordering seasonal menus, and from SMEs producing sales brochures to event organisers needing promotional flyers.

Everything is handled in-house from our Polegate workshop. That means a single point of contact from design brief through to finished print, faster turnaround than jobs sent to a remote trade printer, and direct quality control at every stage.

We work with your existing brand identity or design from scratch. All print files go through prepress checks before production to catch colour, resolution, or bleed issues before ink hits paper. Finished print is inspected and packed carefully — not just stuffed in a box.

Our customer base spans Eastbourne, Hastings, Lewes, Bexhill-on-Sea, Seaford, Hailsham, Uckfield, Crowborough, Newhaven, Polegate, Peacehaven, Battle, St Leonards-on-Sea, Heathfield, and surrounding East Sussex towns.

## Why Choose Mad Graphics?

We've been producing marketing print for East Sussex businesses for over two decades. We understand the standards local businesses need — print that represents the brand properly, arrives on time, and doesn't cause problems at delivery.

Our in-house design capability means we can catch layout and artwork issues before they reach the press, rather than printing what was sent and billing for a reprint. We're straightforward about what works and what doesn't, and we'll tell you if your artwork needs adjustment before we produce it.

Short-run digital and high-volume litho capabilities under one roof mean we can match the production method to the job — you're not paying litho rates for 200 flyers or digital rates for 50,000 leaflets.
