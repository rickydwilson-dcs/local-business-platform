faqs:
  - question: "What file formats do you provide for print design?"
    answer: "We supply print-ready files in PDF, EPS, and AI formats with embedded fonts and CMYK colours. All files include proper bleed margins and crop marks for professional printing."
  - question: "How much does print design cost?"
    answer: "Print design starts from £50 for simple layouts like business cards, ranging up to £500 for complex multi-page documents. We provide detailed quotes based on your specific requirements and complexity."
  - question: "What is CMYK colour and why is it important?"
    answer: "CMYK is the four-colour printing process used by commercial printers. We convert all designs to CMYK colour space to ensure your printed materials match your expectations and avoid colour shifts."
  - question: "Do you include revisions in your print design service?"
    answer: "Yes, we include revisions in our print design service to ensure your artwork meets your exact requirements before final file delivery. Additional major changes may incur extra charges."
  - question: "How long does print design take?"
    answer: "Simple print designs like business cards typically take 2-3 working days, whilst complex multi-page documents may require 5-7 working days. We'll confirm timescales when discussing your project."
  - question: "What information do you need to start a print design project?"
    answer: "We need your content (text and images), preferred size specifications, any existing brand guidelines or logos, and details about your intended printing method and paper stock preferences."
  - question: "Can you work with my existing brand colours?"
    answer: "Absolutely. We can match your existing brand colours using Pantone references or convert RGB colours to accurate CMYK equivalents, ensuring consistency across all your printed materials."
  - question: "Do you provide print services as well as design?"
    answer: "We specialise in creating print-ready design files. Whilst we focus on the design aspect, we can recommend trusted local printers in East Sussex who work with our files regularly."
  - question: "What are bleed and crop marks?"
    answer: "Bleed is extra artwork extending beyond the final trim size, preventing white edges if cutting is slightly off. Crop marks show printers exactly where to trim. We include both automatically in all designs."
  - question: "Can you convert my existing digital design for print?"
    answer: "Yes, we can convert RGB digital designs to print-ready CMYK format, add necessary bleed margins, adjust resolution for print quality, and ensure all fonts and images are properly embedded."
  - question: "Do you offer rush print design services?"
    answer: "We can accommodate urgent projects subject to our current workload. Rush services may incur additional charges of 25-50% depending on the timeline required and complexity of the design."
  - question: "What resolution do you work at for print design?"
    answer: "We create all print designs at 300 DPI minimum resolution to ensure crisp, professional printing quality. Images are optimised for the specific printing method and paper stock you'll be using."
