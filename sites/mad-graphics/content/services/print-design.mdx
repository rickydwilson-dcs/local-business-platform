## Graphic Design for Print

Print design is where ideas become physical. A leaflet that explains your service clearly. A roller banner that represents your business at an exhibition. A banner that draws attention to your premises. A vehicle graphic layout that puts your brand on the road. All of these depend on design that communicates clearly, reproduces accurately, and works at the scale and format it's made for.

Mad Graphics has an in-house design studio at our Polegate base. Our designers have been producing artwork for print and signage production since 2004 — which means they understand print processes, material constraints, and the practical differences between designing for a screen and designing for a banner, a sign, or a vehicle panel.

We design for a broad range of print applications. Leaflets and flyers for local distribution. Roller banners and exhibition systems for trade shows and events. Outdoor banners for events, sports sponsorships, and site hoardings. Business cards and stationery. Shopfront signs and A-board inserts. Vehicle graphics layouts. Workwear print artwork.

For jobs we produce in-house — such as banners, signs, and vehicle graphics — design is part of the production service. For print items produced elsewhere (leaflets, business cards, and so on), we can supply print-ready PDF files to the print standard required by your chosen printer.

We work with clients who have full brand guidelines and need artwork producing to specification, and with clients who have a rough idea and need help turning it into something that works on the page. Both approaches are handled by the same team.

We serve print design clients across Eastbourne, Hastings, Lewes, Bexhill-on-Sea, Seaford, Hailsham, and across East Sussex.

## How It Works

### 1. Brief & Quote
Tell us what you need designed, the format, dimensions, and whether you need print production as well as design. We'll confirm what's included and provide a quote.

### 2. Design & Proof
Our designer produces initial artwork based on your brief. A digital proof is sent for your review and feedback. Two rounds of amendments are included as standard.

### 3. Finalisation
Artwork is finalised to your approved proof and prepared for production or file supply. Print-ready files meet the specification required — bleed, colour mode, resolution, and format.

### 4. Production or File Delivery
For items we produce in-house, the job moves directly to print and production. For externally printed items, we supply files ready for your printer or manage the print production on your behalf.
