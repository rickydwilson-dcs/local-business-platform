## Safety Signs & Compliance Signage

Health and safety signage is a legal requirement for most business premises and all construction sites. It is also one of the most practically important categories of signage — the right signs in the right positions protect employees, visitors, and contractors while demonstrating that a business takes its legal obligations seriously.

Mad Graphics produces safety and compliance signage from our Polegate workshop for businesses, landlords, facilities managers, and construction companies across East Sussex. We supply standard ISO 7010 compliant signs and bespoke signs for specific premises requirements.

Standard safety sign categories include mandatory signs (blue — what must be done, such as PPE requirements), prohibition signs (red circle and bar — what must not be done), warning signs (yellow triangle — hazards present), safe condition signs (green — escape routes, first aid, emergency equipment), and fire safety signs (red — fire exits, extinguisher locations, assembly points).

We produce signs on substrates matched to the application. Aluminium composite is our standard for interior and exterior rigid signs — it is durable, flat, and takes anti-vandal lamination well. Self-adhesive vinyl on a backing sheet allows direct application to smooth surfaces such as doors, walls, and equipment. Correx is used for temporary construction site compliance signs.

Custom safety signs are available for situations where a standard ISO symbol does not cover a specific hazard or instruction unique to a premises or industrial process. We design these to the same colour coding and layout conventions as standard signs to maintain visual consistency and immediate recognition.

We serve customers across Eastbourne, Hastings, Lewes, Bexhill-on-Sea, Uckfield, Crowborough, Seaford, Hailsham, Newhaven, and across East Sussex.
