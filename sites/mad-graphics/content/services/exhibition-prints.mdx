## Exhibition & Display Prints

Exhibitions, trade shows, conferences, and corporate events represent a significant investment of time and money. The quality of your display graphics directly affects the impression you make on visitors, prospects, and partners. At Mad Graphics, we produce exhibition and display print to a professional standard that reflects well on every client we work with.

We serve exhibitors and event participants from across East Sussex — Eastbourne, Hastings, Lewes, Bexhill-on-Sea, Uckfield, and surrounding towns — who exhibit at venues and show grounds regionally and nationally. Our in-house production at Polegate means we can work to tight show deadlines without depending on external trade printers.

Our exhibition print service covers the full range of display formats. Pop-up stand graphics are one of the most common requests — curved and straight pop-up systems are widely used across trade shows, and we produce graphics in the exact panel dimensions your hardware requires. Backdrop prints for photography backgrounds, branded interview walls, and stage backgrounds are produced at any width up to the limits of our equipment, with tiling available for larger installations.

Counter display graphics, literature holder prints, hanging ceiling graphics, and window display kits are all within our regular output. For clients building a complete exhibition stand package, we can produce all graphic elements from a single brief, ensuring consistent colour and design language across every component.

Our design team works with clients who need exhibition graphics created from scratch. We understand exhibition design — the need for strong headlines that read at distance, clear contact information, photography and graphics that work at large scale, and a visual hierarchy that guides the eye across a busy stand.

## How It Works

### 1. Brief & Stand Specification
Tell us about your stand setup, the display systems you are using, and the dimensions required. We advise on substrate and format.

### 2. Design or Artwork Check
Our design team creates exhibition graphics, or we check your supplied artwork for print compatibility at the required size.

### 3. Print & Quality Check
All exhibition graphics are printed, trimmed, and quality-checked before packing. No surprises on show day.

### 4. Ready Before Your Deadline
We work to your show date. Collect from Polegate or arrange delivery to your home, office, or venue.
