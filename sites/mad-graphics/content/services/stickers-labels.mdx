## Stickers, Labels & Wall Graphics

Mad Graphics produces custom stickers, product labels, wall graphics, floor graphics, and window decals from our Polegate print studio. Whether you need branded stickers for packaging, a feature wall graphic for your office, directional floor graphics for a retail space, or a run of product labels for your small food business, we can produce it in-house.

We use digital cutting equipment alongside our large format printers to produce stickers and labels to custom shapes and sizes. All vinyl graphics are laminated to protect against UV, moisture, and surface abrasion. Our adhesive options cover removable, permanent, and specialist applications.

Small East Sussex businesses make up a large part of our label and sticker work — producers of food, drink, cosmetics, and craft products who need short-run labels that look professional without ordering the minimum quantities imposed by large label printers.

We serve customers across Eastbourne, Hastings, Lewes, Bexhill-on-Sea, Seaford, Hailsham, Uckfield, Crowborough, Newhaven, Polegate, Peacehaven, and the wider East Sussex area.

## Why Choose Mad Graphics?

In-house digital cutting means we can produce complex shapes quickly and to a high level of accuracy. We don't outsource cutting or application — the equipment is in our Polegate workshop and operated by our own team.

We've supplied labels and stickers to East Sussex businesses for over 20 years. Short runs are our strength for stickers and labels. We don't impose high minimum quantities, and our pricing is transparent.
