## The Situation

A Brighton-based trade business was exhibiting at a two-day trade event at a venue in Hove. They had ordered 500 A5 promotional flyers from an online print provider two weeks in advance. The order confirmed, production confirmed — and then nothing arrived on the expected delivery day, with the event starting at 9am the following morning.

At 4pm on the afternoon before the event, with no flyers, a tracking number that showed the order as still in production, and the online provider's support team unreachable, they called DCS Print.

## The Brief

They needed:
- 500 A5 flyers, double-sided
- Full colour
- 130gsm silk
- Print-ready artwork available immediately (they emailed it while on the phone)
- Collection from our Polegate workshop by 8am the following morning

## What We Could and Couldn't Do

We confirmed immediately that same-day production and next-morning collection was achievable for a digital print job of this size, with the following conditions:

**What we needed:** Artwork confirmed as print-ready. We ran a preflight check on the PDF they emailed within 15 minutes of receiving it. The artwork was CMYK, 300 dpi, with 3mm bleed — genuinely print-ready with no modifications required.

**What we flagged:** The artwork was approved by the client without a printed proof, which both parties accepted as a condition of the turnaround. Colour on screen may differ from colour on press.

**Pricing:** Rush jobs carry a premium — in this scenario, approximately 40% above the standard turnaround price. The client accepted this without hesitation.

## Production

The job was on press by 5:30pm. A5 digital print at 500 copies runs quickly — the press time itself was approximately 25 minutes. Cutting took a further 30 minutes. The finished flyers were in boxes by 7pm.

The client collected at 7:45am the following morning — 45 minutes before the event opened.

## The Outcome

The client had their flyers for the event. They reported that the quality matched their expectations and they distributed all 500 copies across the two days.

The original order from the online provider arrived, undamaged, four days after the event — well within the original window but useless for the intended purpose. They received a refund on that order and the situation was resolved.

## What Made It Work

**Print-ready artwork from the outset.** If the artwork had required modifications — RGB images, missing bleed, embedded fonts missing — we could not have met the 8am collection. Artwork corrections add hours to any turnaround.

**Digital print capability.** A job of this type — 500 A5 flyers — is a digital print job, not a litho job. Digital print does not require plate-making and can be on press within minutes of a file being received. Short-run digital print is the technology that makes same-day turnaround possible.

**Clear communication at the start.** The client understood from the first conversation what was possible, what the conditions were (no proof, rush premium, collection only), and what wasn't possible (litho quality, next-day delivery). Clear scope at the outset avoided surprises at either end.
