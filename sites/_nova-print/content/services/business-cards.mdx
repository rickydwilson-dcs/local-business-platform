  - question: "What is spot UV?"
    answer: "Spot UV is a coating applied selectively to specific elements of a printed design — typically a logo, text, or graphic — that creates a high-gloss raised finish against a matt background. The contrast between the matt lam base and the gloss UV element creates a striking tactile and visual effect."
  - question: "What size are standard business cards?"
    answer: "The standard UK business card size is 85mm × 55mm. We also offer square (55mm × 55mm), mini (85mm × 42mm), and custom sizes. Square cards stand out when stacked with standard cards. We advise on size relative to your design and industry context."
  - question: "What file format do you need for print-ready artwork?"
    answer: "We accept print-ready PDF files with 3mm bleed, 3mm safe zone inside the trim, and artwork set to CMYK colour mode at 300dpi or above. We check all files before going to press and will flag any issues before printing."
