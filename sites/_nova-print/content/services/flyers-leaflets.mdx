  - question: "What is the difference between a flyer and a leaflet?"
    answer: "The terms are used interchangeably in the UK, though flyers typically refers to single-sheet promotional items (A5, A6) and leaflets often implies a folded item (DL, A4 folded to A5 or three-way). We produce both — flat single sheets and folded formats — and can advise on which best suits your content and distribution method."
  - question: "What paper weight do you recommend for flyers?"
    answer: "130–150gsm silk is the standard for flyers — heavy enough not to feel flimsy but economical for large runs. For a more substantial feel, 170gsm silk is a popular upgrade. Gloss or silk finish both work well depending on your design. We always provide a stock recommendation with the quote."
  - question: "What quantities do you print from?"
    answer: "We print from 100 upwards. Short runs (100–500) are produced digitally. Longer runs (1,000+) move to litho for better cost per unit. We advise on the most cost-effective route for your quantity."
  - question: "Can you design the flyer as well as print it?"
    answer: "DCS Print focuses on print production. For artwork design, we can refer you to a design partner or advise on what your designer needs to supply. We check all files before press and will flag problems before printing."
  - question: "How quickly can you print flyers?"
    answer: "Standard turnaround is 2–4 working days from approved artwork. For large runs (10,000+) allow 5–7 days. Express same-day or next-day print is available for A5 flyers at a premium."
