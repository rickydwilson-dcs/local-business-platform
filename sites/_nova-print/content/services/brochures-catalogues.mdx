  - question: "What is the difference between saddle-stitch and perfect binding?"
    answer: "Saddle-stitching uses staples through the folded spine — suitable for brochures up to approximately 56 pages. Perfect binding glues pages into a flat spine — suitable for documents from 48 pages and above. Perfect binding gives a book-like feel with a printable spine, making it the preferred choice for premium product catalogues and capability documents."
  - question: "What page counts can you produce?"
    answer: "Saddle-stitched documents must be in multiples of 4 — common page counts are 8, 12, 16, 20, 24, and 32 pages. Perfect-bound documents start from around 48 pages. We advise on the most cost-effective page count for your content and help structure it to fit."
  - question: "What cover stock do you use for brochures?"
    answer: "Cover stock is typically 300–350gsm silk with a matt or gloss laminate. For a premium feel, soft-touch laminate on the cover is very popular. Text pages are usually 130–170gsm silk. We recommend stock specifications based on the brochure's purpose, audience, and the impression you want to make."
  - question: "Do you offer eco paper stocks?"
    answer: "Yes. FSC-certified recycled stocks and vegetable-based inks are available for clients with sustainability commitments. Eco-certified stocks are available at a modest premium and are often preferred by professional services businesses and environmentally focused brands."
  - question: "Can you print short runs of brochures economically?"
    answer: "Yes. Digital print makes short runs of 50–500 brochures economical. For runs of 1,000+, litho printing gives a better per-unit cost. We advise on the most cost-effective route based on your quantity."
