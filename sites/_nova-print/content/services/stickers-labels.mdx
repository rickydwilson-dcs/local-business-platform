  - question: "What is die-cutting?"
    answer: "Die-cutting is the process of cutting printed stickers or labels to a custom shape — rather than just rectangles or standard ovals. A custom shape (a logo outline, a product shape, a distinctive form) makes stickers more memorable and visually distinctive. Digital die-cutting allows custom shapes at low minimums without the cost of a physical cutting die."
  - question: "What is the difference between paper and vinyl stickers?"
    answer: "Paper stickers are economical and suitable for indoor, dry applications — packaging, labels, gifts, and promotions. Vinyl stickers are waterproof, UV-resistant, and suitable for outdoor use, vehicle application, bottles, and any moist environment. We advise on the correct material for your specific application."
  - question: "Can you print waterproof labels for bottles and food products?"
    answer: "Yes. We print waterproof labels on polypropylene or polyester stock with waterproof adhesive — suitable for bottles, jars, and products that will be exposed to moisture, refrigeration, or condensation. For food contact applications, we confirm the adhesive and substrate are food-safe."
  - question: "What quantities do you print from?"
    answer: "Digital label printing starts from 50 units — making it economical for small batches and product testing. For runs of 1,000+, roll-label production gives better per-unit pricing. We quote for both routes and advise on the crossover point."
  - question: "Can stickers be supplied on a roll?"
    answer: "Yes. Roll labels are available for use with label applicators in production environments. We can supply labels wound to your specification — label first in or last in, core size, and labels per roll all confirmed at the quote stage."
