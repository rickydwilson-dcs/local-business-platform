    answer: "A presentation folder is a folded, card-stock document carrier with one or two inner pockets that hold loose sheets — business cards, price lists, product sheets, proposals, or brochures. They are used in client meetings, pitches, exhibitions, and welcome packs to present documents in a branded, professional format."
  - question: "What stock do you use for folders?"
    answer: "We use 350gsm silk as standard for presentation folders — substantial enough to hold documents securely without flexing. A matt or gloss laminate is applied to protect the cover and give a premium feel. Spot UV on specific design elements is a popular premium option."
  - question: "What pocket options are available?"
    answer: "We offer single and double pocket options. Pockets can be glued (standard) or welded (more durable — recommended for heavy document loads). Business card slits are available in pockets. Die-cut folder shapes and die-cut pocket shapes are available on larger print runs."
  - question: "What is the minimum quantity for presentation folders?"
    answer: "Minimum quantities start from 100 folders. Below 250, digital print is used. At 250+ folders, litho becomes more cost-effective. We quote for both routes and advise on the crossover point for your quantity."
