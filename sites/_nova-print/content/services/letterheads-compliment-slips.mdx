  - question: "What paper weight is best for letterheads?"
    answer: "100gsm is the standard for letterheads — substantially heavier than standard 80gsm copier paper, producing a noticeably better feel. 120gsm is the premium option — it feels close to card when handled but passes through most laser printers without issues. We confirm printer compatibility before recommending 120gsm."
  - question: "Can letterheads be fed through a standard office printer after printing?"
    answer: "Yes — this is an important consideration. We print the letterhead design onto the stock using a lithographic or digital press, and you then print your letter text over it using your office printer. We confirm paper weight compatibility with your office printer before printing — not all printers handle 120gsm reliably."
  - question: "What is the standard size for a compliment slip?"
    answer: "The standard compliment slip size is DL — 210mm × 99mm, the same width as an A4 page folded into thirds. This fits a standard DL envelope without further folding and can accompany parcels, invoices, or samples."
  - question: "Do you print letterheads with variable data (names, departments)?"
    answer: "Yes. Variable data printing allows each sheet to be personalised with different names, job titles, or department information — useful for larger teams where each person needs their own headed paper. We quote for variable data on request."
  - question: "What quantities do you print from?"
    answer: "We print from 100 sheets upwards. Standard quantities are 250, 500, and 1,000. For very small runs, digital print is used. For 1,000+ sheets, litho gives better value. We advise on the most cost-effective route for your quantity."
