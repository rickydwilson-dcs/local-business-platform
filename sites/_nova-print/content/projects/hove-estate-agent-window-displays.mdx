## The Client

A Hove estate agent branch on Church Road needed a reliable supplier for their window display printing — the large printed property cards and display panels that form the primary street-level presence of any estate agent branch.

They had previously used a London-based supplier whose turnaround times were inconsistent, and who had produced two sets of panels where the colour calibration had produced noticeably inaccurate skin tones and interior colours in property photography. For an estate agent, where property photography is the primary product, colour accuracy is not negotiable.

## The Brief

The window displays use a proprietary display system with panels that clip into the window frame without requiring adhesive. Each window bay takes 12 panels — 24 panels total across the branch. The full display is refreshed quarterly as the property portfolio turns over. Between quarterly refreshes, individual panels are replaced as properties are listed or sold.

The primary requirement was colour accuracy — particularly for property photography, which must render interior colours and natural light faithfully.

## Colour Management Approach

We established a colour management workflow for this client. The estate agent's property photography is supplied as sRGB JPEG files (as delivered by their photographer). We convert to CMYK using a Fogra39 profile with a custom correction for their display system's substrate (a semi-gloss coated paper that sits in the display holders).

A colour proofing print was produced on the first run and approved by the branch manager and the photographer side-by-side against the screen original. The approved proof is retained as the colour reference for all subsequent print runs.

## Service Level

The 48-hour turnaround for new listing panels was established after an initial discussion about what "fast" meant in practice. The client's previous supplier had committed to same-day but failed to deliver; we committed to 48 hours and built a workflow that consistently achieves it — artwork received by 10am is printed and dispatched by end of following day.

The quarterly full display refresh is scheduled in advance — the client books the refresh appointment and supplies updated artwork one week in advance.

## Outcome

The contract has run for two quarterly cycles. No colour accuracy complaints have been raised, and the 48-hour turnaround has been met on all but one occasion (where an artwork file supplied by the photographer required correction). The branch manager reported that the Church Road footfall had increased following the display refresh — though she acknowledged this could not be attributed to print quality alone.
