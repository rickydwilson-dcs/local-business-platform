## The Client

An Uckfield-based artisan food producer making preserves, chutneys, and condiments had been producing product labels in-house on an inkjet printer — a solution that worked at the very beginning but was producing labels that looked domestic rather than retail-ready, and were not moisture-resistant enough for products stored in refrigerated display cabinets by the stockists who carried them.

They had six product lines with seasonal label variants, meaning label designs changed 2-4 times per year per product. This variable pattern suited short-run digital print rather than the longer print runs of lithographic label production.

## Material Specification

The primary label requirement was moisture resistance. Products sit in refrigerators and are handled by consumers who may have cold, damp hands. Standard paper labels absorb moisture and look scruffy within days in these conditions.

We specified white gloss vinyl with a gloss laminate overlay — a self-adhesive label material that is inherently moisture-resistant, with the laminate adding further protection against handling abrasion. The labels apply consistently to glass jars and remain adhered through refrigerated storage.

## Production Approach

Each of the six product lines requires a label approximately 100mm × 70mm. Seasonal variants (Christmas edition labels, summer limited edition) are additional SKUs requiring new artwork but use the same material and production approach.

Digital print is the appropriate technology for this volume and variability — no setup plates, no minimum run length, and the ability to produce mixed label designs in a single print session. We print all six product labels on the same sheet where quantities allow, reducing per-label costs.

## Trade Account

After the first three orders, we established a trade account for the client — monthly invoicing, agreed pricing for the standard label sizes and quantities, and a named contact at DCS Print for direct artwork submission. The account simplifies ordering and invoicing for a business that orders print 6-8 times per year across multiple label variants.

## Outcome

The move to professional vinyl labels improved the retail presentation of the products immediately. Two new stockists — a deli in Lewes and a farm shop near Uckfield — were approached and agreed to trial the products, with the owner noting that the improved label presentation had given her the confidence to approach retail buyers she had previously felt were out of reach.
