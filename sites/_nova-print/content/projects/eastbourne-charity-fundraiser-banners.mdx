## The Event

An Eastbourne-based charity runs an annual black-tie fundraising gala at a seafront venue. The event typically attracts 400 guests and raises a significant proportion of the charity's annual operating fund. It is one of their highest-profile public moments, and the visual presentation of the event contributes to the credibility that encourages attendance and generosity.

The charity had a limited print budget — they manage costs carefully, as their supporters rightly expect administration and event costs to be controlled. The brief was to achieve the highest possible visual impact for the given budget.

## Design Approach

The charity supplied their brand guidelines and event artwork files — they had a in-house designer who had produced the complete artwork package. DCS Print's role was pure production.

We reviewed the artwork and advised on two modifications before printing: the primary headline banner artwork had been set up at 72dpi (screen resolution) — we requested a high-resolution replacement at 150dpi minimum, which is acceptable for PVC banners viewed from 2 metres or more. The colour mode was RGB — we converted to CMYK with client approval, adjusting one element where the CMYK conversion shifted the brand red slightly.

## The Print Package

**PVC banners:** Printed on 510gsm reinforced PVC with hemmed edges and eyelets at 500mm centres. Eyelet position was confirmed against the venue's banner pole specification in advance. Outdoor-grade UV inks for the exterior banners.

**Pull-up retractable stands:** 800mm × 2000mm digitally printed vinyl in the charity's branded design, mounted in budget-tier retractable cassettes — appropriate for indoor event use rather than field/outdoor.

**A2 posters:** Digitally printed on 170gsm silk, supplied loose. The charity supplied their own frames.

**Directional signage:** Simple single-colour vinyl cut signs on 3mm foamex boards — cost-effective for temporary directional use.

## Budget Management

By using vinyl cut signage (rather than full-colour print) for directional signs, and supply-only on posters (frames supplied by the charity), we kept the total package within the agreed budget while prioritising quality on the highest-visibility items — the exterior banners and the main hall pull-ups.

All items produced and delivered to the charity's coordinator two days before the event date.

## Result

The event raised its highest total in five years. The charity's event coordinator reported that feedback on the visual presentation was positive across attendees and sponsors.
