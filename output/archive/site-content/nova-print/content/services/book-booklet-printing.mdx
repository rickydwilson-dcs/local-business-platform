  - question: "What binding options do you offer for books?"
    answer: "We offer three binding methods: saddle-stitch (stapled through the spine — suitable for 8–56 pages), perfect binding (glued flat spine — suitable for 48+ pages, book-like finish), and wire-o (spiral wire binding — lies flat when open, suitable for manuals, recipe books, and reference documents). We advise on the right method based on your page count and use case."
  - question: "What is the minimum quantity for book printing?"
    answer: "We print from 1 copy upward using digital print — there is no minimum quantity. Short runs of 1–50 copies are printed digitally. Runs of 100+ become more economical per unit. For 500+ copies, we can route to litho or book printing specialists for better pricing."
  - question: "Can you print black and white text pages and a colour cover?"
    answer: "Yes — and this is the most cost-effective approach for most books. Black and white interior pages are significantly cheaper to print than full colour. A full-colour cover on heavier stock is combined with mono interiors to give a high-quality result at reduced cost."
  - question: "Do you offer ISBNs or distribution for self-published books?"
    answer: "DCS Print focuses on local print production — we do not handle ISBN registration or commercial distribution. We produce the physical printed copies to a professional standard. For ISBN registration, we can point you to the appropriate UK publishers association."
  - question: "Can you print training manuals and technical handbooks?"
    answer: "Yes. Wire-o binding is particularly well suited to training manuals and handbooks because the document lies completely flat when open — practical for reference during hands-on work. A4 and A5 are both common manual formats. We can print and bind in any quantity from a single proof copy."
