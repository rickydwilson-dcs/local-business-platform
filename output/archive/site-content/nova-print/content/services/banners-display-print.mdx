  - question: "How long do PVC banners last outdoors?"
    answer: "Outdoor PVC banners printed with UV-resistant inks will typically retain good colour for 2–4 years depending on sun exposure. We use 440gsm or 510gsm PVC with UV-stable inks for outdoor applications. For long-term outdoor display, we recommend annual inspection and replacement where fading begins."
  - question: "What are foamex boards used for?"
    answer: "Foamex is a rigid PVC foam board — typically 3mm or 5mm thick. It is used for indoor display panels, point of sale, wayfinding, estate agent boards, and temporary signage. It is lightweight, takes a quality printed finish, and can be cut to any shape. It is not suitable for long-term outdoor use without a protective overlay."
  - question: "Can you eyelet a banner for outdoor fixing?"
    answer: "Yes. All PVC banners include reinforced edges and eyelets as standard — typically at corners and at intervals along the long edges for large banners. Bungee cord or rope is threaded through eyelets for fence or scaffold fixing."
