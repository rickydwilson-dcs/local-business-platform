## The Project

A family in Hailsham had purchased their 1970s detached home knowing that the garden needed significant work. The existing garden was functional but uninspiring — a flat expanse of lawn, a small cracked concrete patio, two old panel fences, and borders that had been left largely unmanaged.

Their brief was to create an outdoor space that worked as a family garden — usable for children now, with good bones for a more structured planting scheme as the children grew older. The key requirements were a proper patio for outdoor dining, a good lawn area, and somewhere to grow vegetables.

## Survey and Design

The soil survey found heavy clay across the full garden area — typical for this part of Hailsham. Clay soil retains water and compacts easily; the existing lawn was waterlogged through winter and hard in summer.

The design addressed this by introducing a drainage layer beneath the new patio and lawn, and raising the lawn level slightly to encourage surface drainage toward the borders. This level change also allowed a planted retaining bed that added visual interest and defined the transition from patio to lawn.

The planting design was led by the low-maintenance brief. Border plants were selected from robust, spreading varieties that would cover ground quickly and reduce the weeding burden — geranium varieties, salvias, ornamental grasses, and established shrub anchors at intervals.

## Works

**Week 1:** Strip existing lawn and borders. Remove collapsed fencing. Level assessment and peg out for new layout. Drainage layer installation under lawn area (Type 1 hardcore base, 50mm sharp sand layer).

**Week 2:** Excavation and base for sandstone patio. Retaining bed construction with treated timber edging. Sandstone laid and pointed.

**Week 3:** New turf to lawn area. Fencing — new close-board panels on concrete posts to the full rear boundary. Raised vegetable beds constructed and filled with 50% topsoil / 50% compost mix.

**Week 4:** Planting installation to the three borders. Edge definition between lawn and borders using steel edging. Final dressing and tidy.

## Result

The completed garden met the brief on all counts — a functional family outdoor space with a proper patio, good lawn, and planting that will establish and mature over the following growing season. The vegetable beds were planted by the family with summer crops in the week following completion.
