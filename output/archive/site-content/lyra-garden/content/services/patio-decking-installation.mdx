  - question: "What is the difference between porcelain and natural stone paving?"
    answer: "Porcelain paving is a manufactured ceramic tile — extremely hard-wearing, frost-proof, low maintenance, and available in a wide range of finishes including realistic stone effects. Natural stone (sandstone, limestone, slate, granite) has a unique texture and character that cannot be replicated but requires periodic sealing and more careful maintenance. We advise on the best choice for your situation and budget."
  - question: "How deep are the foundations for a patio?"
    answer: "A typical residential patio is built on a minimum 100mm compacted hardcore base, followed by a mortar bed. The exact depth depends on ground conditions, whether vehicles will access the area, and local drainage requirements. We carry out groundworks to the correct specification for your site — not a one-size-fits-all approach."
  - question: "What is composite decking and is it better than timber?"
    answer: "Composite decking is manufactured from a blend of wood fibre and recycled plastic. It looks like timber but requires no annual treatment — no oiling, painting, or pressure washing. It is slip-resistant, splinter-free, and carries a 25-year guarantee from most manufacturers. For ease of maintenance, it is usually the better long-term choice."
  - question: "Do you handle drainage as part of the installation?"
    answer: "Yes. All patio and decking installations include correct falls for drainage — water must run away from the house, not toward it. Where additional drainage is required (channels, gullies, soakaways), we specify and install this as part of the project."
  - question: "Can you install lighting into a patio or decking project?"
    answer: "Yes. We can integrate recessed ground lights, deck lights, and low-voltage LED systems into patio and decking projects. Lighting planning is best done during the design phase so conduit can be laid before the surface is installed."
