  - question: "When is the best time to trim hedges?"
    answer: "Timing depends on species. Most formal hedges (box, yew, privet) are trimmed in late spring and again in late summer. Flowering hedges should be trimmed after flowering to avoid removing buds. Conifers are best trimmed in late summer. We advise on timing specific to your hedge species and growth pattern."
  - question: "Can you reduce the height of an overgrown hedge?"
    answer: "Yes. Many overgrown hedges can be hard pruned to reduce height and width, then maintained going forward. Some species — notably Leyland cypress — cannot be cut back into old wood without dying. We assess the species and growth history before recommending a reduction approach."
  - question: "Do you remove the cuttings?"
    answer: "Yes. All hedge cuttings and clippings are removed and disposed of as part of the service. We leave the site clean and tidy on completion."
  - question: "Do you work at height on tall hedges?"
    answer: "Yes. We work from ladders and platform towers on hedges up to approximately 4 metres. For very tall conifers or boundary hedges above this height, we assess the access requirements and advise accordingly."
  - question: "How often do hedges need trimming?"
    answer: "Most formal hedges benefit from 2 cuts per year — late spring and late summer. Fast-growing species such as privet may need 3 cuts. Slower-growing species such as yew typically only need 1 cut per year. We can arrange annual or biannual contracts to handle this automatically."
