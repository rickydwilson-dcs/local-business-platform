  - question: "What preparation is needed before turf is laid?"
    answer: "Proper ground preparation is the most important factor in turf success. We excavate to 100–150mm, remove all existing vegetation and roots, incorporate a quality topsoil mix, rake to a fine tilth, and allow the surface to settle before laying. Cutting corners on preparation is the main reason new lawns fail within a year."
  - question: "What type of turf do you use?"
    answer: "We supply premium grade turf from specialist turf growers — typically a dwarf rye/fescue blend that is hardwearing, recovers well from wear, and maintains colour through dry spells better than cheaper alternatives. We avoid the watery, thin rolls sold in garden centres that struggle to establish well."
  - question: "How soon can I walk on new turf?"
    answer: "New turf should be walked on only for watering purposes for the first 2–3 weeks while roots establish into the soil. Light use is possible from around 4 weeks. The lawn should not be mown until the turf has knitted in — you should not be able to lift a corner without roots holding it. We provide a full aftercare guide on completion."
  - question: "Is turf better than seeding?"
    answer: "Turf gives an instant result and can be used in most seasons. Seeding is cheaper but takes 8–12 weeks to establish and is vulnerable to birds, drought, and heavy rain. For most residential gardens where speed and reliability are priorities, turf is the better choice."
  - question: "What time of year is best for turf laying?"
    answer: "Turf can be laid in any month when the ground is not frozen or waterlogged. The best results come from autumn and spring laying when temperatures are mild and rainfall supports establishment. Summer laying requires more intensive watering. We advise on timing relative to your project schedule."
