  - question: "What is the difference between a logo and a brand identity?"
    answer: "A logo is a single mark. A brand identity is the complete visual system — logo, typography, colour palette, iconography, usage rules, and application examples. A strong brand identity means your logo works correctly across a business card, a van livery, a website, and an exhibition stand simultaneously."
  - question: "What does your brand identity process look like?"
    answer: "We start with a brand strategy session — understanding your business, audience, competitors, and ambitions. From this we build a moodboard, then develop three logo concepts in different directions. You select a preferred direction, we refine it through two revision rounds, and deliver final files with a brand guidelines PDF."
  - question: "What file formats will I receive?"
    answer: "You receive vector master files (AI and EPS), scalable PDFs, print-ready TIFF files, and web-optimised PNG and SVG files. The brand guidelines PDF documents correct usage, clear space, colour codes (Pantone, CMYK, RGB, Hex), and typography pairings."
  - question: "How long does a full brand identity project take?"
    answer: "Typically 4–6 weeks from strategy session to final delivery. Moodboard approval takes a few days, and each concept round usually takes 5–7 working days. The timeline depends on how quickly feedback is turned around at each stage."
  - question: "Can you refresh an existing brand without a full rebrand?"
    answer: "Yes. A brand refresh retains the core equity of your existing identity while updating specific elements — perhaps modernising the typeface, refining the logo's proportions, or cleaning up colour usage. We can advise on whether a refresh or full rebrand is the right choice for your situation."
