    answer: "It depends on the size, type, and location of the sign. Many standard fascia signs fall within permitted development rights and do not require formal consent. Listed buildings and conservation areas always require consent. We advise on this as part of the initial consultation and can guide you through the application process where needed."
  - question: "Can you produce heritage-style signs for a listed building or conservation area?"
    answer: "Yes. We have experience producing signage sympathetic to heritage environments — hand-painted-effect lettering, traditional typefaces, painted or gilded finishes, and materials appropriate to listed building requirements. We work with your conservation officer's guidelines where applicable."
  - question: "What types of illuminated signs do you produce?"
    answer: "We produce LED halo-lit (reverse-lit) signs, front-lit LED tray signs, individually lit letters (forward-lit and halo-lit), and lightboxes. Illuminated signs require an electrician for final connection — we coordinate this as part of the installation."
  - question: "Can you match the signage to our existing brand colours?"
    answer: "Yes. We colour match to Pantone, CMYK, or RAL references and vinyl match using the full 3M and Avery colour ranges. If you provide a brand guidelines document, we work from the colour codes specified."
  - question: "How long does a shop sign project take?"
    answer: "A standard flat fascia sign takes 2–3 weeks from approval. Illuminated signs and 3D lettering typically take 4–5 weeks. Where planning consent is required, the timeline extends to allow for the application period."
