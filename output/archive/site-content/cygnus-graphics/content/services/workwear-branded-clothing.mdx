  - question: "What is the difference between embroidery and heat transfer printing?"
    answer: "Embroidery stitches your design directly into the fabric — it is extremely durable, has a premium feel, and is best suited to logos and text at small sizes (left chest position). Heat transfer printing applies a printed transfer to the garment — it supports full colour and photographic designs and works well for larger print areas such as full-back prints."
