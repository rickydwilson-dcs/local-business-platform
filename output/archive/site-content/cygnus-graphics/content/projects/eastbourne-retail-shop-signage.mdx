## The Project

A family-run retailer in Eastbourne town centre had taken over the business from the previous owner eighteen months earlier. In that time, they had updated the product range and improved the interior layout, but the shopfront still carried the previous owner's signage in various stages of deterioration — a worn fabric fascia sign, inconsistent window lettering in two different typefaces, and a broken A-board with the old business name.

The brief was to design and install a complete new shopfront signage package that reflected the current business identity and gave the shop a cohesive, professional presence on one of Eastbourne's main retail streets.

## Survey and Design

The survey identified several practical considerations. The fascia — a painted timber board — was structurally sound but required replacement with an aluminium tray to achieve the flat, weather-resistant surface that modern illuminated signage requires. The glazing in both ground-floor windows was suitable for full-width vinyl application.

The design brief was to use the existing logo (which the owners wanted to retain) as the primary mark, but to build a more cohesive supporting graphic language for the signage system. We developed a consistent colour application and typographic system that brought all the signage elements into alignment.

## Works

**Fascia:** New powder-coated aluminium tray sign with individual built-up halo-lit letters — each letter is a separate illuminated unit producing a back-lit glow against the face of the tray. Installed over three hours using a cherry picker on an early-morning session before the street's peak traffic.

**Window graphics:** Full-width application to both ground-floor windows — product category text and graphic elements in the brand colours using high-tack vinyl with matte laminate. The design maintained clear sightlines into the shop interior.

**Interior wayfinding:** Three directional signs to the main product departments — cut vinyl applied to timber boards matching the interior fit-out material.

**A-board:** New powder-coated steel A-board with the business name and brand graphic on a fixed header panel, and a changeable lower panel system for current promotions.

## Result

The completed shopfront presented a cohesive identity for the first time since the ownership change. The halo-lit fascia sign is visible from 100 metres along the street in daylight and after dark. The owners reported that the visual improvement had prompted positive comments from multiple regular customers in the first week after installation.
