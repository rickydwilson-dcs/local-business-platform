## The Emergency Call

A Brighton family in a Victorian terraced house in Hanover called at 7am on a Monday in January. Their combi boiler — a 13-year-old unit that had been showing signs of unreliability — had locked out completely overnight and would not reset. The property had no heating or hot water. They had two children under five.

We arranged a same-day visit.

## Diagnosis

The initial inspection found that the boiler's heat exchanger had failed — confirmed by a cracked weld that was visible on inspection and a significant pressure drop during a brief live test. The fault was not repairable; the heat exchanger replacement cost on a 13-year-old boiler was not viable. Replacement was the correct call.

The survey also identified a significant sludge build-up in the system — black iron oxide deposits visible in the radiator drain water. If a new boiler were installed without flushing, the sludge would immediately begin damaging the new heat exchanger, voiding the manufacturer warranty.

## Works Undertaken

**Day 1 (afternoon):** Power flush of the entire system. Existing boiler removed. New flue route identified — the original horizontal rear flue could not accept a modern concentric flue efficiently without modification, so a new penetration was made in the side return. Gas meter confirmed as adequate for the replacement boiler's output.

**Day 1 (evening):** New Worcester Bosch Greenstar 8000 Life installed, flued, and connected to gas and plumbing. System inhibitor dosed. Boiler commissioned and tested.

**Day 2 (morning):** Smart thermostat wired and paired. System checked at operating temperature. Radiator balance checked across all eight radiators.

## Completion

The family had heating and hot water restored within 24 hours of the initial call. The Gas Safe certificate, building regulations notification, and boiler commissioning certificate were issued on completion.

The new boiler carries a 7-year parts and labour warranty and qualifies for the Boiler Upgrade Scheme heat pump ready certificate, should the client choose to transition to heat pump technology in future.
