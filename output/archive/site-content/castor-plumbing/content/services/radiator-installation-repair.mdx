  - question: "Why is my radiator cold at the top but warm at the bottom?"
    answer: "Cold at the top is almost always caused by trapped air in the radiator. Bleeding the radiator — releasing the trapped air through the bleed valve — usually solves this within minutes. If the problem returns repeatedly after bleeding, it may indicate corrosion producing hydrogen gas or a faulty auto air vent."
  - question: "Can you fit a designer or vertical radiator to my existing system?"
    answer: "Yes. We supply and fit designer radiators, vertical column radiators, and vertical flat-panel radiators to suit any room. We check that your boiler and pump can accommodate the additional heat output required and make any necessary adjustments."
  - question: "How do you fit a heated towel rail?"
    answer: "A heated towel rail is connected into the central heating circuit — we run new pipe connections from the nearest radiator or pipework point. It can also be fitted as a dual-fuel rail with an electric element for use in summer when the central heating is off."
  - question: "Can you replace a radiator like-for-like without redecoration?"
    answer: "Yes. For a straight swap — same radiator size, same valve positions — we can replace a radiator with minimal disruption and usually without needing redecoration. If the new radiator has different dimensions or valve positions, some touching up may be required."
  - question: "Do you supply radiators or do I need to source my own?"
    answer: "We can supply radiators at trade prices or fit radiators you have sourced yourself. If you supply, please send us the manufacturer specifications in advance so we can check compatibility with your system's flow temperature."
