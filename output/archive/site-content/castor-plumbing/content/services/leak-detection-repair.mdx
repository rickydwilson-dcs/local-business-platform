    answer: "We use two principal methods: thermal imaging cameras, which detect temperature differences caused by moisture; and acoustic detection equipment, which listens for the characteristic sound of water escaping under pressure.   - question: "Can you provide an insurance report for a leak repair?"
    answer: "Yes. We produce a written report detailing: the location and probable cause of the leak, the detection method used, photographic evidence, the repair carried out, and post-repair pressure test results. This format is accepted by most home insurers."
  - question: "How much disruption is involved in accessing a leak?"
    answer: "We minimise access as much as possible — typically cutting a single access panel rather than opening an entire run. Our detection equipment means we know exactly where to look. In most cases, we make good the access point before leaving."
  - question: "What causes hidden water leaks in East Sussex homes?"
    answer: "The most common causes in this area are: corrosion on copper pipework (particularly in older Eastbourne and Lewes properties with hard chalk water), frost damage to exposed pipes, failed compression joints, and movement in older lead-to-copper connections in period properties."
  - question: "How long does a leak detection and repair typically take?"
    answer: "Detection alone typically takes 1–2 hours. A straightforward pipe repair following detection takes another 1–2 hours. Complex repairs involving significant rerouting or structural access may require a return visit. We provide a complete timeline before starting."
