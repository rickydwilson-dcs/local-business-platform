## NP Racing (British Superbike) — simplified brief (2026-08-01)

NP Racing is a private British Superbike (BSB) team run by Neil Pearson,
based in Taunton, Somerset. The business has been involved in racing and the
British Superbike paddock since 2004, with its first full season in the
premier Superbike class coming in 2020.

### The team

- **Base:** Taunton, Somerset
- **Manufacturer:** Honda
- **Championship:** British Superbike Championship (known as the ZYN British
  Superbike Championship in 2026)

### 2026 season

For the 2026 BSB season, NP Racing has been competing with Honda machinery
and has been developing young talent:

- Brayden Elliott returned to the BSB grid with NP Racing from the Knockhill
  round in June 2026.

### Reputation

NP Racing is regarded as one of the championship's respected independent
teams. While it doesn't have the budget of factory-backed outfits such as
Honda Racing UK, Ducati PBM or McAMS Yamaha, it has earned a reputation for:

- Developing riders.
- Running professionally prepared Honda Fireblades.
- Being a close-knit, family-style operation with experienced technicians.
- Consistently punching above its weight against larger teams.

### Team owner

The team is run by Neil Pearson, founder of NP Motorcycles. Under his
leadership the business has expanded from a motorcycle servicing and
workshop into a recognised name in British motorcycle racing.
