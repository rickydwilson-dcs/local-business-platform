How It Works

Four simple steps to your
new website

No jargon, no endless meetings. We keep things straightforward so you can focus on running your business.

1

Discovery call

We have a relaxed chat about your business, your customers, and what you need from your website. No hard sell, just honest advice about what will actually work for you.

2

Design & content

We create your design and write all the copy for you. You review it, suggest changes, and we refine until it is exactly right. Most clients are happy after one or two rounds.

3

Build & test

We build your site with clean code and optimise it for speed, search engines, and mobile devices. Every page is tested across browsers and screen sizes before you see it.

4

Launch & support

We handle the domain, hosting, SSL, and go-live. After launch, we keep everything updated and secure so you never have to worry about the technical side again.
