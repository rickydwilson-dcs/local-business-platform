Built for sites that can't stop

Built for sites that can’t afford to stop

Autcobel was founded in 2022 to fix a specific problem: retail and coldchain sites need electrical and data work done around live operations, not instead of them. That’s still all we do.

Why Autcobel

Sites that don’t get to close for a rewire

Supermarkets, cold storage and distribution sites don’t get to close for a rewire. Every fit-out, upgrade or repair has to work around trading hours, temperature-critical stock and live logistics — and most electrical contractors are built for sites that can shut down.

Autcobel isn’t. We plan and deliver around your operation, not the other way round.

Straight from Autcobel

Delivery specialists in cold chain and critical infrastructure

Autcobel is a specialist project delivery partner with deep roots in the UK’s cold chain, retail and commercial infrastructure sectors — bringing decades of combined experience across electrical & data systems, control and monitoring technologies, gas leak detection, plant commissioning, feasibility and design, consultancy and project management.

We blend the agility of a contractor with the foresight of a client — a perspective that helps us anticipate challenges, streamline delivery and consistently exceed expectations. Accustomed to fast-paced, high-demand environments, our engineering team is built to keep disruption to a minimum on live operational sites, whether that’s a national retail rollout, a critical refrigeration upgrade, or a complex commercial build.
