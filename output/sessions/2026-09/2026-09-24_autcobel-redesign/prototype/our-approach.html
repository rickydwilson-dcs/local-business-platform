How we work

Consultation first. Delivery second. No surprises in between.

Every site is different — what doesn’t change is how we work through it with you.

The process

Five steps, every project

1

Consultation

We start by listening: what’s live, what can’t stop, what’s already gone wrong before. No proposal goes out before we understand the site.

2

Design & planning

A project and design plan built around your operating hours, not a generic spec.

3

Collaboration, not handover

You get one point of contact for the whole project, not a different name every phase.

4

Delivery

Phased, out-of-hours or live-site work as the project needs, managed to programme.

5

Support

24/7 project support doesn’t stop at handover.

Why it matters

Most projects fail at the handoffs

Most electrical projects go wrong at the handoffs — between designer and installer, between installer and client, between “finished” and “actually working.” Keeping one team across the whole project removes most of those handoffs before they can cause a problem.
