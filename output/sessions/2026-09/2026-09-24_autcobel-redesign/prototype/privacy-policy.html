What we collect

  • Contact form submissions: name, company, email, phone, message
  • Website analytics: pages visited, general location, device type (anonymised where possible)
  • Cookies — see our Cookie Policy

Why we collect it

  • To respond to enquiries and quote for work
  • To understand how the site is used and improve it
  • Where you’ve agreed, to keep you updated about Autcobel’s services

Legal basis

We process enquiry data under legitimate interest (responding to a business enquiry you’ve initiated) and, where applicable, consent (marketing, non-essential cookies).

How long we keep it

Enquiry data is kept for as long as needed to respond and, if it becomes a client relationship, for the duration of that relationship plus any period required by law (e.g. accounting records).

Sharing your data

We don’t sell your data. We may share it with service providers who help us run the site or respond to enquiries (e.g. hosting, email, CRM), under contracts that protect your data.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR you have the right to access, correct, delete, or restrict the use of your personal data, and to withdraw consent at any time. Contact us using the details above to exercise any of these rights.

Changes to this policy

We’ll update this page if how we handle data changes, and update the date above.
