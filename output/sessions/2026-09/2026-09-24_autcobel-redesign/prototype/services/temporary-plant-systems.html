Keep the site live

Temporary Plant Systems

Permanent works shouldn’t mean the site goes dark, or the cold chain breaks, while you wait.

What’s included
  • Temporary power distribution and generation
  • Bridging supply during electrical upgrades or outages
  • Temporary containment and small power for phased works
  • Planned, scheduled changeovers between temporary and permanent systems
Flagged for Gene: confirm whether “temporary plant” should also cover temporary refrigeration/cold-storage plant, or electrical power only. Copy on this page is written to electrical/power scope only until confirmed.

Why it matters on a live site

Stock doesn’t wait for a rewire, and neither does a delivery schedule. Temporary plant keeps power — and, critically, temperature-critical areas — running while the permanent installation goes in, so the works happen without a trading or cold-chain interruption.
