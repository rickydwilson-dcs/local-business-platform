One accountable manager

Project & Design Management

One person accountable for the programme, the budget and the outcome — not three people you have to chase separately.

What’s included
  • Design development and technical drawings
  • Programme planning and sequencing around live operations
  • Budget management and cost control
  • Site coordination across trades
  • Progress reporting, so you’re not asking for updates
  • Handover documentation and close-out

Why one manager, not three contacts

A lot of project risk isn’t technical — it’s coordination. Someone has to make sure the electrician, the data installer and the site manager are working from the same programme. That’s what this service is: a single accountable manager across the whole job, so the risk of things slipping between contractors sits with us, not you.
