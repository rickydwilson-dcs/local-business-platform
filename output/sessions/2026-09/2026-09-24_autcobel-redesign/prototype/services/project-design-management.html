One accountable manager

Project & Design Management

One person accountable for the programme, the budget and the outcome — not three people you have to chase separately.

What’s included
  • Design development and technical drawings
  • Programme planning and sequencing around live operations
  • Budget management and cost control
  • Site coordination across trades
  • Progress reporting, so you’re not asking for updates
  • Handover documentation and close-out

Who this is for

Clients running a project with more than one trade on site — electrical, data, refrigeration, building works — who need one person accountable for the whole programme rather than juggling separate contacts for design, budget and delivery. Especially suited to multi-phase rollouts and refits, where a missed handoff between contractors is the real risk, not the technical work itself.

Why one manager, not three contacts

A lot of project risk isn’t technical — it’s coordination. Someone has to make sure the electrician, the data installer and the site manager are working from the same programme. That’s what this service is: a single accountable manager across the whole job, so the risk of things slipping between contractors sits with us, not you.

From enquiry to handover

What actually happens

1

Mobilisation

Programme, budget and reporting lines agreed before work starts, so everyone’s working from the same plan from day one.

2

Managed delivery

One accountable manager coordinating every trade on site against that programme, flagging and resolving issues before they become delays.

3

Close-out

Handover documentation and sign-off delivered as one package, not chased from three different contractors after the fact.

Common questions

FAQs

Can you manage a project where other contractors are already appointed?

Yes — we can step in as principal contractor or as embedded site coordination, working alongside trades you’ve already appointed rather than replacing them.

Do you manage the budget, or just the programme?

Both. Budget management and cost control sit inside this service, not as a separate line.

What does “progress reporting” actually look like?

Regular updates to whoever needs them, in a format that doesn’t need translating — so you’re not the one chasing status.
