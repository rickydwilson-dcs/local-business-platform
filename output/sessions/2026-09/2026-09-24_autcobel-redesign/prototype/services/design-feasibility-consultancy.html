Get it right before you build it

Design & Feasibility Consultancy

Upfront technical guidance before a single cable goes in, so the spec is right the first time.

What’s included
  • Feasibility studies for new builds, upgrades and retrofits
  • Technical specification support ahead of tender or design freeze
  • Compliance and performance planning
  • ESG-aligned retrofit planning and Net Zero transition guidance

Who this is for

Teams weighing up a new build, a major upgrade or an ESG-driven retrofit who need a straight answer on what’s technically feasible — and roughly what it costs — before committing budget to a full design. If you’re deciding between two or three options for a site and need the technical case for each one, this is the stage that gives you that.

Why get this in before the design is fixed

Decisions made at feasibility stage are the cheapest ones to change and the most expensive to get wrong. We look at what the site actually needs — compliance, performance, energy efficiency — before it’s locked into drawings, so the spec that goes to tender is one that survives contact with the real building.

Built around where you’re heading

Net Zero and ESG requirements are increasingly part of the brief, not an afterthought. We factor energy-efficient design and smart technology options in at this stage, so a client isn’t retrofitting sustainability onto a finished installation later.

From enquiry to handover

What actually happens

1

Site review & brief

We look at the building (or drawings, for a new build), confirm what you’re trying to achieve, and agree what “feasible” needs to mean for this project — compliance, budget, timeline, or all three.

2

Options & technical guidance

Feasibility findings and specification support you can actually act on — written to inform a real design or tender decision, not a generic report that sits in an inbox.

3

Handover to design or delivery

Findings carry straight into Project & Design Management or Turnkey Electrical & Data if you proceed, so nothing gets re-explained to a second team.

Common questions

FAQs

Is this a paid service, or part of a wider quote?

It depends on scope — get in touch with what you’re looking at and we’ll tell you upfront, not after the report’s written.

Do you only do this for new builds?

No — most of our feasibility work is on upgrades and retrofits to existing sites, where working out what’s actually achievable within the building’s constraints matters more than it does on a blank site.

What if the feasibility study says it’s not worth doing?

Then that’s what we tell you. The point of doing this stage properly is catching that before design spend, not after.
