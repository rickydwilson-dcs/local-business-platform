Control & Monitoring Systems

Control panels and remote monitoring that turn a live site into something you can actually see.

What’s included
  • Control panel design and integration
  • Remote monitoring for refrigeration, HVAC and energy systems
  • Smart automation for energy management
  • Real-time performance and fault insight

Why real-time visibility matters

A refrigeration or HVAC fault that goes unnoticed for a shift can cost a lot more than the repair. We design and integrate control and monitoring systems that surface a problem the moment it starts, not after stock or trade has already been affected.

Built into the wider installation

Control and monitoring rarely sits on its own — it’s specified alongside the electrical, refrigeration or HVAC systems it’s watching. We integrate it as part of the same project, so the monitoring is commissioned and tested against the equipment it’s actually reporting on.
