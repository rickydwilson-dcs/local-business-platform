Control & Monitoring Systems

Control panels and remote monitoring that turn a live site into something you can actually see.

What’s included
  • Control panel design and integration
  • Remote monitoring for refrigeration, HVAC and energy systems
  • Smart automation for energy management
  • Real-time performance and fault insight

Who this is for

Sites running refrigeration, HVAC or other critical equipment where a fault has to be caught in minutes, not discovered on the next walk-round — multi-site retail and cold storage operators in particular, where one unmonitored failure can mean a full stock write-off before anyone notices.

Why real-time visibility matters

A refrigeration or HVAC fault that goes unnoticed for a shift can cost a lot more than the repair. We design and integrate control and monitoring systems that surface a problem the moment it starts, not after stock or trade has already been affected.

Built into the wider installation

Control and monitoring rarely sits on its own — it’s specified alongside the electrical, refrigeration or HVAC systems it’s watching. We integrate it as part of the same project, so the monitoring is commissioned and tested against the equipment it’s actually reporting on.

From enquiry to handover

What actually happens

1

Spec against the equipment

Control and monitoring requirements defined alongside whatever it’s watching — refrigeration, HVAC, energy systems — not as an afterthought once that’s already installed.

2

Install & integrate

Panels and monitoring installed as part of the same project as the equipment they report on, so they’re commissioned together.

3

Test against real fault conditions

Commissioning includes proving the alerts actually fire, not just that the panel powers on.

Common questions

FAQs

Can you add monitoring to equipment that’s already installed?

Yes — retrofitting monitoring onto existing refrigeration, HVAC or energy systems is common; the panel and sensor design just has to work with what’s already there.

Who gets the alert when something goes wrong?

Whoever you need to — that’s agreed and configured as part of the install, not fixed to a single option.

Does this replace a building management system (BMS), or sit alongside one?

It can do either, depending on what the site already has — tell us what’s in place and we’ll scope around it.
