Gas Leak Detection Systems

Detection and alert systems for sites where a leak can’t go unnoticed.

What’s included
  • Supply and installation of gas detection and alert systems
  • Specified for high-risk and refrigerant-handling environments
  • Delivered with certified partners
  • Fully compliant with F-Gas regulations and industry best practice

Who this is for

Sites handling refrigerants or other hazardous gases — cold storage, supermarket plant rooms, industrial refrigeration — where F-Gas compliance isn’t optional and a missed leak is a safety incident, not just a maintenance issue.

Why this isn’t optional in a cold chain environment

Refrigerant leaks are a safety issue and a regulatory one. We supply and install detection and alert systems built to catch a leak early, in line with F-Gas regulations, so the risk is managed rather than discovered after the fact.

Delivered with certified partners

Detection equipment is only as good as its installation and calibration. We work with certified partners on this scope, so what goes in is specified, installed and signed off to industry best practice — not just supplied and left.

From enquiry to handover

What actually happens

1

Risk & compliance review

We confirm what needs covering under F-Gas regulations for your specific plant and site layout — not a generic detector count.

2

Supply & install with certified partners

Detection and alert equipment installed and calibrated by certified partners, specified to the risk identified.

3

Sign-off & documentation

Compliance documentation delivered so it’s ready for your own records or an external audit, not chased after the fact.

Common questions

FAQs

What does “F-Gas compliant” actually cover?

F-Gas regulations govern how refrigerant-handling systems are monitored and maintained to limit leaks of fluorinated gases. We design and install detection to meet that, working with certified partners on the specialist calibration and compliance side.

Who are the “certified partners” you mention?

Specialist detection and calibration partners we bring in for this scope. We manage the relationship, so it’s one point of contact for you, not a separate contractor to coordinate.

Is this a one-off install, or does it need ongoing servicing?

Detection systems need periodic testing and calibration to stay compliant — talk to us about what that looks like for your site when we scope the install.
