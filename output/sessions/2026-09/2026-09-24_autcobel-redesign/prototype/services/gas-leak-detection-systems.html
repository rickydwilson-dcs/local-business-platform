Gas Leak Detection Systems

Detection and alert systems for sites where a leak can’t go unnoticed.

What’s included
  • Supply and installation of gas detection and alert systems
  • Specified for high-risk and refrigerant-handling environments
  • Delivered with certified partners
  • Fully compliant with F-Gas regulations and industry best practice

Why this isn’t optional in a cold chain environment

Refrigerant leaks are a safety issue and a regulatory one. We supply and install detection and alert systems built to catch a leak early, in line with F-Gas regulations, so the risk is managed rather than discovered after the fact.

Delivered with certified partners

Detection equipment is only as good as its installation and calibration. We work with certified partners on this scope, so what goes in is specified, installed and signed off to industry best practice — not just supplied and left.
