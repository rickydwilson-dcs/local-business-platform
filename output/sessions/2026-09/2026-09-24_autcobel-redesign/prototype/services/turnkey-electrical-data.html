End-to-end delivery

Turnkey Electrical & Data

One contract, one team, from first drawing to final commissioning.

What’s included
  • Power distribution and containment
  • Lighting (including emergency and compliance-driven upgrades)
  • Data and structured cabling
  • Small power and containment for fit-outs and refits
  • Testing, inspection and certification
  • Commissioning and handover documentation

Who this is for

Teams commissioning a new distribution centre, converting a unit to cold storage, or refitting a chain of stores to a new format, who don’t want to run separate electrical and data contractors and reconcile two different snag lists. This is the service that covers the physical infrastructure end to end, under one contract.

How we deliver it

“Turnkey” means you’re not managing three different contractors to get one working system. We take a project from design through to a certified, working installation — electrical and data together, because on most of the sites we work on, they go in at the same time anyway.

Built for live sites

Fit-outs and refits on trading or operational sites get phased and sequenced around what has to stay running. Out-of-hours delivery is standard where the site needs it, not an extra line on the quote.

From enquiry to handover

What actually happens

1

Survey & scope

We walk the site (or review drawings for a new build), confirm what’s already there, and agree the scope against your programme and any trading or operational constraints.

2

Install, phased to your site

Power, lighting, data and containment go in to the sequence the site needs — out-of-hours, in stages, or as a single continuous build, whichever fits.

3

Test, certify, handover

Full testing and inspection, certification issued, and handover documentation delivered so the next contractor or your own FM team has what they need.

Common questions

FAQs

Can you work around a site that’s still trading?

Yes — this is most of what we do. Phased and out-of-hours delivery is standard, not an add-on; see Our Approach for how we plan around live operations.

Do you handle both the electrical and the data cabling, or just one?

Both, under one contract. On most of the sites we work on they’re installed together anyway, so splitting them across two contractors just adds a handover point that doesn’t need to exist.

What happens if the scope changes once you’re on site?

It gets agreed and priced before it’s actioned, not after. Your point of contact stays the same throughout, so a change doesn’t mean explaining the job to someone new.
