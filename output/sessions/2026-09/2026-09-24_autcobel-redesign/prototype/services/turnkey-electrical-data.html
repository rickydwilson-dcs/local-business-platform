End-to-end delivery

Turnkey Electrical & Data

One contract, one team, from first drawing to final commissioning.

What’s included
  • Power distribution and containment
  • Lighting (including emergency and compliance-driven upgrades)
  • Data and structured cabling
  • Small power and containment for fit-outs and refits
  • Testing, inspection and certification
  • Commissioning and handover documentation

How we deliver it

“Turnkey” means you’re not managing three different contractors to get one working system. We take a project from design through to a certified, working installation — electrical and data together, because on most of the sites we work on, they go in at the same time anyway.

Built for live sites

Fit-outs and refits on trading or operational sites get phased and sequenced around what has to stay running. Out-of-hours delivery is standard where the site needs it, not an extra line on the quote.
