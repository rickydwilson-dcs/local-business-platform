## What's included

- Feasibility studies for new builds, upgrades and retrofits
- Technical specification support ahead of tender or design freeze
- Compliance and performance planning
- ESG-aligned retrofit planning and Net Zero transition guidance

---

## Why get this in before the design is fixed

Body: Decisions made at feasibility stage are the cheapest ones to change
and the most expensive to get wrong. We look at what the site actually
needs — compliance, performance, energy efficiency — before it's locked
into drawings, so the spec that goes to tender is one that survives contact
with the real building.

---

## Built around where you're heading

Body: Net Zero and ESG requirements are increasingly part of the brief, not
an afterthought. We factor energy-efficient design and smart technology
options in at this stage, so a client isn't retrofitting sustainability
onto a finished installation later.
