## Why this isn't optional in a cold chain environment

Body: Refrigerant leaks are a safety issue and a regulatory one. We supply
and install detection and alert systems built to catch a leak early, in
line with F-Gas regulations, so the risk is managed rather than discovered
after the fact.

---

## Delivered with certified partners

Body: Detection equipment is only as good as its installation and
calibration. We work with certified partners on this scope, so what goes
in is specified, installed and signed off to industry best practice — not
just supplied and left.
