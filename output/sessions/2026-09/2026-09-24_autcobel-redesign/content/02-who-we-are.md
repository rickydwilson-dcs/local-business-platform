## The story

Section heading: **Why Autcobel**

Body (draft, needs Gene's steer — see status doc): Supermarkets, cold

upgrade or repair has to work around trading hours, temperature-critical

## What makes us different

1. **One team, one point of contact** — design, project management and
   delivery under one roof, not handed between subcontractors.
2. **Built around downtime, not around us** — out-of-hours and phased
   delivery as standard on live sites, not a premium add-on.
3. **Coldchain-literate** — we understand temperature-critical environments
   and what happens if power or data goes down in one.
4. **Nationwide, 24/7** — one contractor for every site, wherever it is in
   the UK.
