Website design in Brighton

Brighton is one of the most competitive markets in the south of England. The city is full of good small businesses and customers have plenty of choice — so if you aren’t showing up prominently online, you’re handing the work to somebody who is.

Why Brighton is harder than the towns around it

Brighton is home to a huge number of small businesses, and customers here have more choice than anywhere else I work. That cuts both ways: the market is big enough to build a business on, and crowded enough that being invisible in it costs you real work every week.

I run DCS from Polegate in East Sussex, about seventeen miles east of the city. I know the Brighton market — the density of the competition, the range of customers from student landlords to luxury homeowners, and the expectations that come with operating somewhere this size.

Brighton is the wrong place for an off-the-shelf site

Every site I build is built from scratch, and in a market this competitive that is the difference between showing up and being one of forty results nobody scrolls to.

For a Brighton business that means four things in particular:

  • Custom design, rather than a template a visitor can recognise in two seconds.
  • Copy written for what you actually do and the part of the city you actually work in.
  • Local SEO targeting Brighton neighbourhoods and the surrounding towns, not just the word “Brighton”.
  • Layouts built to turn a visit into a call or an enquiry, which is the only thing a site in this market is for.

That applies whatever the business is. The electrician quoted below works in the city; a shop, a studio or a letting agent would get the same build, written for a different customer.

My phone started ringing within two weeks of the site going live. Ricky handled everything — I didn’t have to do a thing.

Mark H. — Electrician, Brighton

Domestic and commercial are two different jobs

Brighton has more commercial work going than the towns around it — letting agents, property managers, and firms that put a contract out rather than ring the first name they find. Those buyers want to see credibility before they make contact, and they want to see it in a different place on the page than a homeowner does.

So where it’s worth doing, I’ll build the site to speak to both: separate content and separate calls to action for domestic and commercial, rather than one set of words trying to do both jobs and landing neither.

Local to Brighton, and it wouldn’t matter if I weren’t

Brighton is genuinely local to me, and I know the area, the competition and the market well enough for it to show in the work. But I build for businesses across the whole of the UK, from Cornwall to the Cairngorms, and every one of them gets the same locally-tuned approach — built around the searches their own customers are actually making.

If you run a business in Brighton and want a site that brings in consistent, quality enquiries, get in touch. There’s the pricing if you’d rather see the numbers first.

Brighton questions

What people here ask first.

Do you work with businesses in Brighton?

Yes — Brighton is one of my most active areas. I work with electricians, plumbers, builders, roofers, heating engineers and landscapers in and around the city, and the same build suits a shop, a studio or a practice just as well. The office is in Polegate, East Sussex, so Brighton is right on the doorstep.

How do I get found on Google in Brighton with this much competition?

Competing in Brighton takes a technically sound website, well-targeted local content, and a properly optimised Google Business Profile. I build all three in from the start. I’ll also position you around specific neighbourhoods and the surrounding areas, where the competition is often lower and the opportunities just as strong.

What does a professional website need in Brighton?

Brighton customers are savvy and their expectations are high. Your site needs to load fast, look right on a phone, show real reviews and accreditations, and make getting in touch frictionless. All of that is in every build as standard.

Can a website help me win larger commercial jobs here?

Yes. A strong site builds the kind of credibility that commercial clients, letting agents and property managers expect before they make contact. I can tailor the site to target domestic and commercial audiences separately, with its own content and calls to action for each.

Is DCS only East Sussex, or the rest of the UK too?

Both. The office is in Polegate, East Sussex — so Brighton is a local area — but I work with businesses across the whole of the UK. The same process and the same standard, wherever you’re based.
