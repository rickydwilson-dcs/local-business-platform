Sarah Chen is the SEO Director at Verdant Digital, where she leads a team specialising in local and regional search visibility for small and medium businesses. With over ten years of experience in the SEO industry, Sarah has seen the search landscape change dramatically — from the early days of keyword stuffing and link farms to today's AI-driven results pages and zero-click searches. Her specialism is translating these constant changes into practical, actionable strategies that business owners can actually implement.

Her work focuses on the fundamentals that remain effective regardless of algorithm updates: Google Business Profile optimisation, citation consistency, review acquisition, and creating locally-relevant content that answers the questions customers are actually searching for. Sarah is known for debunking SEO myths that persist in small business circles and for making technical concepts accessible to non-technical audiences.

At Digital Marketing Weekend, Sarah will take the Main Stage on Saturday morning to cover what has genuinely changed in local SEO during 2025–2026 — including the impact of AI overviews on local search results — and, crucially, what still works. Attendees will leave with a 30-minutes-per-week maintenance plan they can apply to their own businesses immediately.
