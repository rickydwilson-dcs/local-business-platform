James Hartley runs Future Marketing Lab, a training business that helps small business owners adopt practical AI tools to improve their marketing output without drowning in tech overwhelm. He started the business in 2023 after watching clients spend thousands on marketing agencies for tasks that could be partially automated — and after seeing those same clients paralysed by the sheer number of AI tools being launched every week. His mission is simplification: find the handful of tools that genuinely save time and improve quality, and teach people how to use them properly.

James trains clients on a deliberately short list of tools, including ChatGPT for content drafting and ideation, Canva AI for visual creation, and a selection of automation platforms for scheduling, email sequences, and social posting. He is candid about the limitations of AI tools — he spends as much time teaching clients when not to use AI as when to use it, particularly when it comes to brand voice, local credibility, and customer-facing communication where generic AI output can do more harm than good.

Sunday's Main Stage session at Digital Marketing Weekend will be one of the most practical of the weekend. James will demonstrate tools live, show before-and-after examples of AI-assisted marketing content, and give attendees a clear, prioritised list of tools they can implement immediately — categorised by budget and technical comfort level. He will also address the elephant in the room: what AI genuinely can't do for small businesses, and why the human element remains the differentiator.
