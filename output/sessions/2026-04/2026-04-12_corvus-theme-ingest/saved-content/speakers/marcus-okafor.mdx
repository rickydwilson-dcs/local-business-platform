Marcus Okafor is a paid media strategist at Spark Advertising, where he manages Google Ads and Meta advertising campaigns for small and medium businesses across a range of industries. He entered the paid advertising world after a career in direct sales, and that background shapes his approach: every campaign he builds is designed with measurable outcomes front and centre, not vanity metrics or impressive-sounding click-through rates.

Marcus specialises in helping small businesses compete in paid search and social environments that are often dominated by larger brands with significantly bigger budgets. His methods focus on tight audience targeting, disciplined negative keyword management, and conversion tracking that actually reflects business outcomes — not just website visits. He has developed a reputation for being able to make small budgets work by being ruthlessly analytical about what is and isn't delivering results.

On Sunday morning at Digital Marketing Weekend, Marcus will break down the anatomy of a small-budget paid campaign that actually works. He will cover the most common mistakes small businesses make when they run ads themselves, how to set up proper conversion tracking, and the specific targeting approaches that make the difference between a campaign that drains a budget and one that generates leads. Attendees will leave with a framework they can apply to their own Google or Meta accounts within a week.
