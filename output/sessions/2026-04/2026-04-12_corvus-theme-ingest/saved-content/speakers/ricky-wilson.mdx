Ricky Wilson is the founder of Digital Consulting Services, a digital marketing agency focused on helping local and small businesses build an online presence that actually converts. He started the business after noticing a recurring pattern: small business owners were spending money on marketing tactics that looked impressive on paper but delivered no measurable results. His mission has been to cut through the noise and replace guesswork with strategy.

Over the past decade, Ricky has worked with tradespeople, retailers, hospitality businesses, and professional service firms across the UK. His approach is deliberately practical — no abstract theory, no buzzwords, just the specific actions that move the needle for businesses operating with limited budgets and even more limited time. He has a particular focus on the intersection of local SEO, reputation management, and content marketing, and how these three disciplines can be made to work together with minimal ongoing effort.

At Digital Marketing Weekend, Ricky will open Saturday with a keynote covering the minimal marketing stack that delivers results without agency budgets. Attendees will leave with a clear picture of which channels are worth their time in 2026, how to build a basic system that works while they sleep, and what most small businesses are doing wrong — along with how to fix it in an afternoon.
