Colossus Scaffolding — New Website

January 2025

Colossus Scaffolding is an East Sussex scaffolding contractor that had been operating for several years entirely through word-of-mouth referrals. They had no web presence whatsoever — no website, no Google Business Profile, and no way for new customers to find them online. As their business grew and they started bidding on larger contracts, the lack of a professional online presence was becoming a genuine liability.

The brief was straightforward: build a professional website that establishes credibility for larger clients, gets Colossus ranking on Google for scaffolding searches across East Sussex, and creates a clear pathway for prospects to get in touch. We built the site on the DCS platform using the Vega theme customised with Colossus's navy blue and white brand palette, wrote all the service and location content, and configured the full local SEO stack from day one.

The site launched in January 2025 with 14 pages covering the core service offering, 8 location pages for the towns Colossus operates in, a project gallery, and a team page. We set up Google Workspace so the team could start using professional email addresses, and we submitted the site to Google Search Console and created their Google Business Profile as part of the launch package.

The results

  • First enquiry within 3 weeks of launch An inbound contact form submission from a new customer who found them via Google — the first digital lead in the business's history.
  • 3–5 organic leads per month by month three Steady growth in search impressions as the location pages began ranking for scaffolding + area keywords across East Sussex.
  • Professional email replacing personal Gmail The whole team switched to @colossusscaffolding.co.uk addresses. Quoting and invoicing now comes from a professional address, improving trust with new clients.
  • Google Business Profile verified and populated Profile now shows in Google Maps for Sussex scaffolding searches. 12 five-star reviews collected in the first 3 months.
  • Increased credibility for commercial contracts Being able to send prospects to a professional website before site meetings has measurably improved the conversion rate on larger commercial tenders.
  • Zero maintenance overhead The DCS managed hosting model means the Colossus team have never had to worry about updates, security, or uptime since launch.
