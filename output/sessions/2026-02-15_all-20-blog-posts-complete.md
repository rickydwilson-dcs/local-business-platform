2. **Fresh context per agent:** No token limits, no context pollution
3. **Schema-aware agents:** Automatically adapted frontmatter to match Zod schema
4. **Comprehensive outlines:** Detailed prompts = consistent high-quality output
5. **Natural keyword usage:** Agents avoided keyword stuffing, wrote naturally
6. **Local context integration:** Extensive use of specific towns and areas
7. **Trust signals:** Credentials woven naturally throughout content
8. **Cost table generation:** Agents created scannable pricing tables automatically
