Why Tradespeople Need a Website in 2025

12 April 2025·By Ricky Wilson·5 min read

Most tradespeople still rely on word of mouth. It works — until it doesn't. Referrals dry up. A regular client moves away. The phone goes quiet for a few weeks. Without a steady stream of new enquiries coming in, you're vulnerable to the natural ebb and flow of a personal recommendation network.

A website changes that. It works for you 24 hours a day, seven days a week — answering the same questions your customers would ask, presenting your work professionally, and making it easy for someone who's never heard of you to get in touch. Once it's built and ranked, you don't have to do anything. The enquiries come to you.

The numbers don't lie

Over 90% of people search online before contacting a local tradesperson. That's not a marketing statistic — it's what your potential customers are doing right now. They're typing "electrician Brighton" or "scaffolding company Eastbourne" into Google, and clicking one of the first few results. If you're not there, you simply don't exist to them.

The tradespeople who show up consistently at the top of those results have something in common: a properly built website with content that matches what customers are searching for. It's not magic. It's structure, relevance, and a bit of patience.

Why Google visibility matters for your trade

Unlike Facebook or Instagram, Google captures intent. Someone searching for "emergency plumber Lewes" right now has a problem and needs it solved today. That's exactly the kind of customer who turns into a job, not just a like. A website that ranks for those terms is a consistent source of high-quality leads — people who are already looking for exactly what you offer, in the exact area you work.

The competitor angle

Here's a useful exercise: Google your trade and your town right now. Who comes up? If it's not you, it's a competitor picking up the calls that should be going to your phone. Most of those competitors don't have particularly impressive websites — they just have one. Getting on the first page of Google in a local market doesn't require a huge budget; it requires a well-structured site and consistent local SEO.

Generating enquiries while you sleep

A contact form on your website works the same way a phone call does — except it works at midnight on a Sunday. Customers fill it in when they think of you, whenever that is. The next morning, you have a lead in your inbox. For busy tradespeople who can't always answer the phone on the job, this is one of the most practical benefits of having a proper website.

Combine that with a Google My Business listing, a few location pages covering the towns you work in, and some genuine customer reviews, and you have a reliable local lead generation system that runs itself. That's what we build for every client.
