Questions

Common pricing questions

Do I own my website?

If you pay upfront, yes — you own the site outright. The monthly hosting fee just covers ongoing service. If you choose Pay Monthly, it's a service model: we build and maintain the site on your behalf, but if you stop paying the service ends and the site comes down. Think of it like leasing rather than buying.

What happens if I want to cancel?

Upfront clients can cancel the monthly hosting at any time with 30 days' notice. Pay Monthly clients have a 12-month minimum, then roll monthly with 30 days' notice. If you cancel, we'll help you export your content and hand everything over. No lock-in tactics.

Can I upgrade my plan later?

Yes, absolutely. You can upgrade from Starter to Professional or Growth at any time. You'll pay the difference in setup cost (for upfront) or move onto the higher monthly rate. Your existing content and design carry over — we just add the extra pages and features.

Are there any hidden fees?

No. The prices you see are what you pay. Domain registration is included on every tier. Google Workspace email is billed directly by Google (~£5.50/user/month) — we set it up, but it goes through your Google account, not us. Everything else is included.

What's included in a content update?

A content update covers things like changing a price, updating your service description, adding a new testimonial, or tweaking your contact details. Major additions — like a whole new service section or a redesign — are quoted separately depending on scope.

What's the difference between upfront and pay monthly?

Same site, same quality, same support. The difference is ownership and cashflow. Upfront means you pay more now but less over time — and you own the asset. Pay Monthly means no big initial outlay, but you're paying for an ongoing service. By year 2, the upfront option is significantly cheaper in total.
