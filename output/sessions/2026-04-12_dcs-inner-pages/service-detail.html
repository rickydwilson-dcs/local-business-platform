Web Design

Website Design for Tradespeople

A professional website that gets you found on Google and turns visitors into paying customers — built specifically for tradespeople.

About this service

Running a trade business means your time is spent on the tools — not managing websites. That's why we handle everything for you: design, build, content, SEO, hosting, and ongoing support. You get a professional web presence without needing to become a web developer.

Every website we build is mobile-first, fast-loading, and built to rank in Google for local search terms like "electrician in Brighton" or "scaffolding Sussex". We use structured data markup (Schema.org) so your business appears correctly in Google search results and Google Maps.

We don't use page builders or templates bought off a shelf. Each site is custom-designed to match your brand and built on a modern technology stack that handles hundreds of pages without slowing down.

What you get

  • Mobile-first, fast-loading Over 70% of trade enquiries come from mobile. Your site will score 90+ on Google PageSpeed.
  • Full local SEO built in Schema markup, meta tags, sitemap, and content optimised for your target area from day one.
  • No ongoing maintenance headaches We manage hosting, security updates, and uptime monitoring. You focus on the work.
  • Custom domain + professional email yourname@yourcompany.co.uk included. No more quoting from a Gmail address.
  • Contact form with email notifications Every enquiry lands straight in your inbox, including the caller's name, phone, and message.
  • Unlimited revisions during build We tweak until you're happy. No extra charges for design changes while we're building.

Frequently asked questions

  • How long does it take to build my website?

    Most projects go from kick-off to launch in 2–4 weeks. We start with a discovery call, then design, build, and review in stages. You'll have full visibility throughout and can request changes at any point before launch.

  • Do I need to write the content myself?

    No. We write all the content for you based on a short questionnaire about your services and target area. Our content is written by a human (not AI-generated) and optimised for local search terms relevant to your trade.

  • Will I be able to update the website myself?

    We handle all updates for you as part of the monthly management fee. If you'd prefer to manage content yourself, we can set up a simple content management interface — just let us know at the start of your project.

  • What if I already have a logo or brand colours?

    Great — we'll incorporate your existing branding. If you don't have a logo yet, we can design one as an optional add-on (£250). We'll always match your trade's identity, not use a generic template.
