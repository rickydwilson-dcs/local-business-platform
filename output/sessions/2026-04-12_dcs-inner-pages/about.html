Our story

Digital Consulting Services started because we kept seeing the same problem. Brilliant tradespeople — scaffolders, electricians, plumbers, painters — with no online presence. Relying entirely on word of mouth. Turning away work because they couldn't handle the growth, or not getting enough work because nobody could find them.

We built a system specifically for this. A professional website with strong local SEO, written to attract the right enquiries, fully managed so you can focus on the job. No technical knowledge required. No need to write your own content. We handle everything.

We've now built and manage websites for over 30 trade businesses across Sussex and the South East. Every one of them is getting more online enquiries than before. That's the only metric that matters.
