About Digital Consulting Services

Digital Consulting Services was founded in East Sussex with a simple belief: every tradesperson who's good at their job deserves to be found by customers looking for exactly what they do. We started building websites for local tradespeople after seeing how many skilled, reliable contractors were losing work to less capable competitors who simply had a better web presence.

What makes us different from other web agencies is that we specialise exclusively in trade businesses. We know how local SEO works for plumbers, electricians, scaffolders, painters, and gardeners — the search terms customers use, the questions they ask, and what it takes to convert a visitor into a paying job. We don't build ecommerce sites or brand campaigns. We build websites that fill diaries.

We're a small, expert team. Every client works directly with us from kick-off to launch and beyond. We're available by phone and email, we respond quickly, and we tell you straight what's working and what isn't. No account managers, no outsourcing, no jargon.
