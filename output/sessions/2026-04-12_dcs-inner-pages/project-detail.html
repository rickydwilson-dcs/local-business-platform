Colossus Scaffolding — New Website

January 2025

The challenge

Colossus Scaffolding had been trading in East Sussex for several years, building a strong reputation among builders, property developers, and homeowners across the region. But their online presence was essentially non-existent — no website, no Google listing, and no way for new customers to find them without a personal referral.

Paul, the owner, knew he was missing out on enquiries. Competitors with basic websites were appearing on Google for the searches his ideal customers were making — "scaffolding company Sussex", "scaffold hire East Sussex", and dozens of variations. He wanted to fix that, and he wanted it done without months of back-and-forth or a five-figure agency bill.

What we built

We built Colossus a full platform site using the Professional tier: 40 pages of service and location content covering scaffolding for residential projects, commercial builds, and specialist access work across East Sussex and into West Sussex and Kent. The design uses a bold navy and white palette that reflects the strength and precision of the trade. Every page is mobile-first and loads in under two seconds.

The site includes Schema markup for a local business, consistent NAP (name, address, phone) across all pages, a contact form with email and SMS notifications, and a blog section to support ongoing SEO. We set up Paul's Google Workspace email, registered the domain, and handled the Vercel deployment. He went from nothing to a fully operational online presence in under three weeks.

The results

  • First organic enquiry within 3 weeks of launch
  • 3–5 qualified leads per month from Google within the first quarter
  • Professional business email replacing personal Gmail account
  • Appearing on page one of Google for 12+ target keywords within 90 days
  • Google My Business listing live and verified, driving map pack traffic
  • All ongoing changes handled by DCS — Paul focuses on the job, not the website
