Banners

Printed banners for shops, sites, events and exhibitions. We design them in-house and print on UV-rated materials, so you can choose the type that suits where it will hang.

Illustrative mock-up. Real project photos to come.

Banners differ mainly in the material. PVC is a solid sheet, the usual choice for a message that has to be read from the road. Mesh is perforated, so it suits fences, scaffolding and open ground where wind is a worry. Fabric has a softer finish for indoor displays and backdrops. Roller banners are freestanding stands that pull up from a base and pack away. Pick by where it goes and how often it moves.

Which banner should I choose?

For a message on a wall or railing, PVC. For a fence, scaffold or exposed spot, mesh. For indoors, fabric or a roller banner. If you are unsure, tell us where it is going and we will suggest one.

Can you design the banner?

Yes. Our in-house team can work from your logo and brand colours, or start from a blank page.

Do banners come with eyelets?

Most hanging banners are finished with eyelets so you can tie or cable-tie them up. Tell us if you want them placed a particular way.

What do you need for a quote?

The size, the wording or artwork, and where it will be used. A photo of the spot helps.
