Brand identity

Your logo is one piece. Brand identity sets the colours, typefaces and layout rules around it, so your van, shopfront and paperwork look like they belong to one business.

Get a free quote01323 589 700

Illustrative mock-up. Real project photos to come.

Most businesses do not look inconsistent on purpose. A logo gets made, then the van is lettered by one firm, the cards printed by another and the website built by a third. Brand identity pulls those together. We choose a colour palette and typefaces, set out how the logo should and should not be used, and put it on a guide you can hand to anyone who works with your brand. Every sign and print job after that starts from the same rules.

When you need it

  • Your van, sign and cards all look different
  • You are rebranding an existing business
  • Several people produce materials for you
  • You are opening a second site or adding vehicles
  • You have a logo but no colours or fonts chosen
  • A supplier keeps asking which colours to use

What you get

  • Colour palette. The exact colours to use on every job, set out clearly.
  • Typefaces. Fonts picked for headings and body text, with notes on use.
  • Logo usage rules. Spacing, minimum sizes and what not to do to it.
  • A brand guide. One document you can send to any printer, sign maker or web designer.

How it works

  1. Review what you haveWe look at your logo, vehicles, signs and print as they stand.
  2. Set the directionWe talk through how you want the business to come across.
  3. Draft the identityColours, type and logo rules are put together for you to look at.
  4. Test it on real itemsWe apply it to a sign, a vehicle and a page of print.
  5. Hand over the guideYou receive the brand guide and the supporting files.

Questions

Can't see yours? Ring 01323 589 700.

Do I need a new logo for this?

No. We can build the identity around the logo you already have, or design a new one if the old one is holding the business back.

What is the difference between a logo and a brand identity?

A logo is the mark. A brand identity includes the colours, fonts and rules for using the mark, so everything you produce matches.

Is it worth it for a small business?

If you buy signs, vehicle graphics and print from more than one place, yes. A guide stops each job from drifting away from the last.

Can you apply the identity to my signs and vans?

Yes. We design and make the graphics, so the identity carries straight through.

Will you update my existing materials?

We can rework current artwork to match the new identity. Tell us what you have and we will look at it.

Can I send the guide to other suppliers?

Yes. That is what it is for. It keeps anyone producing your materials working from the same colours and rules.
