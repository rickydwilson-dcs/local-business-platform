Banners

Fabric banners

Printed fabric banners for indoor displays, event backdrops and exhibition stands. A soft, non-glare finish, designed in-house and printed in full colour to suit the space you have.

Get a free quote01323 589 700

Illustrative mock-up. Real project photos to come.

Fabric banners are printed on textile rather than vinyl. The surface is matt, so it avoids the glare that lights and cameras pick up on a shiny sheet, and it hangs and packs more softly. They suit reception areas, shop interiors, conferences and stage backdrops. We print to size and finish the edges for hanging from a pole, rail or frame. For an outdoor message, PVC or mesh is usually the better choice.

When you need it

  • Backdrops for events and conferences
  • Exhibition stands and trade shows
  • Retail interiors and shopping centres
  • Reception areas that need branding
  • Product launches and photo walls
  • Hospitality venues and function rooms

What you get

  • Matt textile finish. Less glare under lights and in photos.
  • Full-colour print. Logos, patterns and photos reproduced clearly.
  • Finished for hanging. Edges prepared for a pole, rail or frame.
  • In-house design. Artwork made to fit the space.

Questions

Can't see yours? Ring 01323 589 700.

Is fabric better than PVC?

Not always. Fabric looks softer and avoids glare, which suits indoors and photos. PVC is the more usual choice for outdoor messages.

Can I use a fabric banner outside?

We would normally suggest PVC or mesh for outdoors. Tell us the conditions and we will advise.

Can I reuse it?

Yes. It can be taken down and stored for the next event, folded or rolled.

Can you print a full backdrop?

We print custom sizes. Tell us the width and height you need and we will confirm.

How do I hang it?

Usually from a pole, rail or frame. We can finish the top and bottom edges to suit.

Do you design the artwork?

Yes, in-house, from your brand files or from scratch.
