Large format, window and wall

Poster printing

Posters for shop windows, events, offices and notice boards. Printed to the size you need, from A4 and A3 up to large formats, with design done in-house.

Illustrative mock-up. Real project photos to come.

A poster does one job: stop someone walking past and tell them one thing. We print posters on paper stock in standard sizes such as A0, A1, A2, A3 and A4, or cut to a custom size. We can also print on foam board or Correx for something stiffer. Keep the message short and the image big, and we will lay it out so it reads from across the room.

When you need it

  • Announcing a sale, opening or special offer
  • Advertising an event or fundraiser
  • Filling a shop window display
  • Keeping a menu or price list on show
  • Putting up staff notices or safety information
  • Decorating a waiting room or office

What you get

  • Standard sizes. A0, A1, A2, A3 and A4, or a custom size.
  • Paper or board. Printed on paper, or mounted on foam board or Correx.
  • In-house design. Artwork made here, or print-ready files checked.
  • A proof first. You see it before it is printed.

How it works

  1. Choose size and stockTell us the size and whether it needs to be rigid.
  2. Send your filesOr give us the wording and images and we will design it.
  3. Check the proofWe check resolution and colours, and you approve the layout.
  4. PrintPrinted at full size on the chosen stock.
  5. Collect or deliverRolled, flat or boarded, ready to put up.

Questions

Can't see yours? Ring 01323 589 700.

What sizes do you print?

The usual A-sizes from A4 up to A0, plus custom sizes. Ask if you need something larger.

Can you design my poster?

Yes. Send the wording, logo and any images and we will lay it out.

Can I use my own artwork?

Yes. We check it is big enough to print well and tell you if the image is too small.

Can posters go outside?

Paper posters are best kept indoors. For outdoor use, ask us about banners or Correx boards.

Can you mount posters on board?

Yes. Foam board or Correx gives a stiffer finish for easels and stands.

Do you print one-offs?

Yes. A single poster is fine, and we can do repeats of the same design.
