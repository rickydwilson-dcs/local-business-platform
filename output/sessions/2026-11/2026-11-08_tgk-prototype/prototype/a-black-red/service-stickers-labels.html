Stickers and labels

Stickers and labels for your products, packaging, vehicles and premises. Printed to the shape and size you need, and designed in-house if you have no artwork.

Illustrative mock-up. Real project photos to come.

Stickers and labels are both printed vinyl or paper with an adhesive back, but they do different jobs. Stickers are for being seen: logos, slogans, promotions, giveaways, branding on equipment. Labels are for information: product names, ingredients, addresses, barcodes, warnings. Both can be cut to a custom shape, supplied as singles or on a sheet, and printed in as many or as few as you need. Choose by what the piece has to do, then talk to us about size and material.

When you need it

  • You want your logo on tools, cases or equipment
  • Products need a label before they go on sale
  • Packaging needs a branded seal or closing sticker
  • A promotion needs something people can take away
  • Equipment or stock needs clear identification
  • Existing labels have worn out or the branding changed

What you get

  • In-house design. We can set up your artwork or create it from scratch.
  • Custom shapes. Round, square, rectangular or cut to the outline of your logo.
  • Suitable materials. We help you choose the stock that suits where it will be used.
  • Short or long runs. Order a small batch to test or a larger run to keep in stock.

What is the difference between a sticker and a label?

A sticker is mainly for display and branding. A label carries information about what it is stuck to. The materials and adhesives can be the same.

Can I get a custom shape?

Yes. We can cut to a circle, rectangle, rounded corners or the outline of your logo.

Do you design them as well as print them?

Yes. Send what you have and we will make it print-ready, or we can design from scratch.

Can I order a small quantity?

Yes. Tell us how many you need and we will say what is possible.
