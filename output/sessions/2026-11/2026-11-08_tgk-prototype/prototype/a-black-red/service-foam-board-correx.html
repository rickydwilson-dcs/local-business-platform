Signs and signage

Foam board and Correx

Lightweight printed boards for notices, events and short-term signs. Foam board suits indoor use, and Correx suits places that need something tougher and plastic, such as events.

Foam board is a light, smooth board that takes print well. It suits indoor displays, presentations and point-of-sale. Correx is a fluted plastic sheet, a lot like corrugated card but made of plastic. It is light, stiff and suits short-term signs that go outside, such as event signs and estate agent boards. Both are simple to make and easy to replace. Neither is a long-term outdoor choice.

When you need it

  • Event signs that only need to last a day or two
  • Indoor displays and exhibition boards
  • Point-of-sale cards in a shop
  • Temporary notices and site signs
  • Presentation and mounted prints
  • Short-run to-let and open day signs

What you get

  • Foam board. Smooth and light, good for indoor print.
  • Correx. Fluted plastic sheet for short-term outdoor signs.
  • Cut to size. Straight cuts, or to shape for simple outlines.
  • In-house design. Artwork made here, to your brand.

What is the difference between foam board and Correx?

Foam board is smooth and suits indoor display. Correx is plastic and fluted, so it copes better outside for short periods.

Can I use Correx outdoors?

For short-term signs, yes. It is not meant as a permanent outdoor sign. Ask us about rigid board for that.

Will foam board survive rain?

No, keep it indoors. Water gets into the surface.

Can you print on both sides?

Yes, on both materials.

Can you cut it to a shape?

Simple shapes, yes. Send the outline you want.

Can I get a lot of the same sign?

Yes. Correx suits repeat runs for events and campaigns.
