Artwork and prepress

Already have a design, a screenshot or a file from someone else? We repair it, redraw it and set it up so it prints or cuts cleanly, without you needing to know the technical bits.

Get a free quote01323 589 700

Illustrative mock-up. Real project photos to come.

Files arrive in all states: a logo saved from a website, a Word document, a photo of an old sign, a PDF with the wrong colours. Prepress is the work between that file and the machine. We redraw blurry logos as vector, fix sizes and cut lines, check colours, allow for bleed and trim, and convert files to what the job needs. It also covers the unglamorous jobs: removing a background, matching a colour or rebuilding a font that has gone missing.

When you need it

  • Your logo is a small, pixelated image
  • A file will not open or has missing fonts
  • You have a photo or scan of an old design
  • Artwork needs adjusting to a new size
  • A design has to be cut from vinyl
  • Colours look wrong when printed

What you get

  • Redrawn artwork. Low-quality logos rebuilt as clean vector files.
  • Correct set-up. Size, bleed and cut lines made right for the job.
  • A file check. We look for problems before anything is produced.
  • Usable formats. Files supplied in the types your job or supplier needs.

How it works

  1. Send the fileEmail whatever you have, however rough.
  2. We assess itWe check size, quality, colours and fonts.
  3. Tell you what is neededWe say what can be saved and what must be redrawn.
  4. Repair and set upThe artwork is rebuilt, resized and prepared for output.
  5. Check and supplyYou see the result and receive the finished file.

Questions

Can't see yours? Ring 01323 589 700.

Can you fix a blurry logo?

Usually, yes. We can redraw it as vector artwork so it is sharp at any size. Very rough images may need rebuilding more than tidying.

What file types can you work from?

Most common ones, including PDF, image files and design formats. Send what you have and we will tell you what we can use.

Do I need prepress if I'm printing with you?

We do the set-up as part of the job, so you do not have to. This service is for when you need the files fixed or supplied on their own.

Can you make files for another printer?

Yes. Tell us their requirements and we will set the artwork up to match.

What is bleed?

Extra artwork extending past the cut edge, so that no white edge shows if the trim is slightly off.

What if I only have a photo of my old sign?

That is enough for us to redraw it. We will match the lettering and shapes as closely as the photo allows.
