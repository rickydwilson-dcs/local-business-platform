Folders

Presentation folders with your logo and colours on the outside and a pocket inside. For quotes, proposals, information packs and anything you hand over in paper form.

Get a free quote01323 589 700

Illustrative mock-up. Real project photos to come.

A folder holds loose papers and makes them look considered. Printed presentation folders usually fold from a single sheet, with a pocket on one side, and often a slit to hold a business card. We design the outside and, if you like, the inside pocket, in your brand colours. They are used by builders handing over quotes, by estate agents giving out details and by schools and clubs issuing information packs.

When you need it

  • Quotes and proposals need to look like a proper pack
  • You hand out information packs to new customers
  • A school or club issues the same pack to many people
  • A venue gives guests printed information on arrival
  • You want your branding on something people keep
  • Your paperwork arrives in plain envelopes

What you get

  • Pocket folder. A fold with an inner pocket and room for A4 papers.
  • Card slot. A slit to hold a business card, if you want one.
  • Branded outside. Your logo and colours on the cover.
  • Design included. Layout from your artwork or from scratch.

How it works

  1. Describe the contentsWhat goes inside and how many sheets.
  2. Choose the stylePocket on one side or both, with or without a card slot.
  3. Send your brandingLogo, colours and any text for the cover.
  4. Approve the proofCheck the cover, the pocket and the cut.
  5. Printed and cutPrinted, cut and folded to fit.

Questions

Can't see yours? Ring 01323 589 700.

What size are the folders?

Usually A4, to hold standard paperwork. We can discuss other sizes.

Can they hold a business card?

Yes. We can add a slit in the pocket for a card.

Can I put text inside?

Yes. We can print the inside as well as the outside.

Can you design the folder for me?

Yes. We design it to match your cards and letterhead.

How many do I need?

Think about how many packs you hand over. Tell us the number and we will say what is practical.

Can I reorder with the same design?

Yes. We keep the artwork and reprint on request.
