Stickers and labels

Labels

Printed labels for products, packaging, parcels and equipment. They carry the name, details or warning that has to be read, set out clearly on a surface that will take them.

Get a free quote01323 589 700

Illustrative mock-up. Real project photos to come.

A label does a plain job: it says what something is. It might be a product name on a jar, a handling instruction on a box, an address on a parcel or an asset tag on a piece of kit. We print labels on adhesive stock cut to size, as a roll or as sheets, and we design them with you so that the text can be read at a glance. For anything with legal wording, you supply the text and we set it.

When you need it

  • Products need a label before they can be sold
  • Parcels and boxes need handling or address labels
  • Equipment needs asset or serial identification
  • Stock needs a clear name and date line
  • A product needs a label in a new size or wording
  • Handwritten labels no longer look right

What you get

  • Clear layout. Text set at a readable size with room around it.
  • Your wording. You supply the details, we set them out.
  • Sizes to fit. Cut to the size and shape of your container or box.
  • Repeat orders. We keep your artwork so reordering is simple.

How it works

  1. Tell us what it labelsJar, bottle, box, parcel or equipment, with its size.
  2. Send the wordingNames, details, codes and any logos.
  3. Choose the finishPaper or vinyl stock, depending on how it will be handled.
  4. Approve the proofCheck every word, number and the size.
  5. Printed and cutSupplied on a roll or as sheets, ready to apply.

Questions

Can't see yours? Ring 01323 589 700.

Can you print labels for food products?

We can print the label you design or supply. You are responsible for the legal wording and we set it out as you give it to us.

Can you do barcodes?

If you give us the code, we can include it on the label. Tell us what scanner will read it so we size it properly.

Do you do rolls or sheets?

Both. Rolls suit applying many labels in a row. Sheets suit small quantities and hand application.

Can I reorder the same label?

Yes. We keep your approved artwork on file, so a repeat order is quick to set up.

What size should my label be?

Measure the flat area where it goes and tell us. We will suggest a size that leaves a margin.

Will they stay stuck on cold or damp surfaces?

It depends on the stock and the surface. Tell us the conditions and we will suggest a suitable material and adhesive.
