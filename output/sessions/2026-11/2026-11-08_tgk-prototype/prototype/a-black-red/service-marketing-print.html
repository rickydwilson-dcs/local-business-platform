Print and stationery

The everyday print a business needs: cards, leaflets, brochures, letterheads, folders and menus. Designed in-house and printed to standard sizes, or to your own size.

Most printed work falls into two groups. Items you hand over: business cards, flyers, leaflets, brochures. Items that sit in your business: letterheads, folders, menus. Business cards are the smallest and the most used. Flyers and leaflets are for one-off promotions. Brochures run to several pages when you have more to say. Letterheads and folders make documents look consistent. Menus are for places that serve food and drink. We print on standard sizes such as A4, A5 and DL.

When you need it

  • You are starting up and need cards and letterheads
  • A promotion needs flyers through doors or on counters

Questions

Which print should I start with?

Most businesses start with business cards and a letterhead. Add flyers or a brochure when you have something to promote.

Can you match my existing branding?

Yes. Send your logo, colours and a sample and we will match them.

Can you design the print as well?

Yes. Our in-house team can design from your brief or work from artwork you supply.

What is the difference between a flyer and a brochure?

A flyer is one sheet for one message. A brochure has several pages and suits a fuller explanation of your business.
