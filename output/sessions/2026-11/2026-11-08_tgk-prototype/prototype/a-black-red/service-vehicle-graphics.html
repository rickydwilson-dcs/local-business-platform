Vehicle graphics

Lettering, logos and partial graphics for vans, cars and whole fleets. Designed in-house by The Graphic Kings and cut from UV-rated vinyl, so your vehicles do some of your advertising for you.

No full wraps. Lettering and partial graphics only.

Illustrative mock-up. Real project photos to come.

Vehicle graphics here means vinyl cut to shape and fitted to the panels, doors and windows of your vehicles. Van graphics suit tradespeople and delivery firms. Car graphics suit sales reps and company cars. Fleet graphics keep several vehicles looking like one business. Magnetic signs come off when the vehicle is not working for you. We do lettering, partial graphics and livery. We do not do full vehicle wraps.

When you need it

  • A new van that needs your name and number on it
  • Company cars that should look like they belong together
  • Adding vehicles to an existing fleet
  • Old lettering has peeled, faded or gone out of date
  • A vehicle you only use for work some of the time
  • A rebrand that has to reach every vehicle

What you get

  • In-house design. Artwork made here, from your brand files or from scratch.
  • UV-rated vinyl. Material chosen for outdoor use on a vehicle.
  • A proof first. Nothing is made until you have approved the artwork.
  • One point of contact. Design, production and fitting arranged through us.

How it works

  1. Tell us the vehicleSend the make, model and a photo of each side.
  2. Agree what goes onName, number, logo and any line about what you do.
  3. Check the proofWe show you the artwork on the vehicle shape.
  4. Approve and we make itProduction starts only after your sign-off.
  5. FittingWe fit it and check it before it goes back on the road.

Questions

Can't see yours? Ring 01323 589 700.

Which vehicle page should I read?

Van graphics for work vans, car graphics for cars and estates, fleet graphics for three or more vehicles that should match, and magnetic signs if the vehicle is not always on business duty.

Do you do full vehicle wraps?

No. We do lettering, logos, partial graphics and livery. We do not cover a whole vehicle in printed film.

Can you design the graphics for me?

Yes. Our in-house team works from your logo and brand colours, or helps you build a simple look from scratch.

What do you need for a quote?

The vehicle type, what you want on it and any existing artwork. Photos of the vehicle help.
