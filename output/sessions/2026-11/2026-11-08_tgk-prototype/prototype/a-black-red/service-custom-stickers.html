Stickers and labels

Custom stickers

Stickers cut to the shape of your logo or any outline you like. Use them to brand equipment, seal packaging or hand out as a promotion.

Get a free quote01323 589 700

Illustrative mock-up. Real project photos to come.

A custom sticker is printed vinyl or paper with an adhesive back, cut to the shape you choose. Circles and rectangles are the easy options. A die-cut sticker follows the outline of your logo, which looks sharper on a laptop lid or a toolbox. We can supply stickers one at a time, on a sheet or on a roll, and we can set up your artwork if it is not ready to print.

When you need it

  • You want branded stickers to hand out with orders
  • Tool cases and equipment need your name on them
  • Packaging needs a closing sticker with your logo
  • A launch or promotion needs something eye-catching
  • A club or event needs matching stickers for everyone
  • Your logo needs to go on a flat surface quickly

What you get

  • Any shape. Round, square, or cut to the outline of your artwork.
  • Your artwork. Print-ready files used as supplied, or set up by us.
  • Material options. Vinyl or paper, depending on where the sticker will be used.
  • A proof. You see the shape and colours before we print.

How it works

  1. Send your logoAny format will do, even a photo of what you have now.
  2. Choose size and shapeSay where the sticker goes so we pick a size that works.
  3. Choose the materialVinyl for surfaces that get handled, paper for dry indoor use.
  4. Approve the proofCheck the cut line and colours.
  5. Printed and cutWe print, cut to shape and supply as singles, sheets or rolls.

Questions

Can't see yours? Ring 01323 589 700.

Can you cut stickers to the shape of my logo?

Yes. That is called die-cutting, and it works best on logos with a clear outline.

What can I stick them on?

Most clean, dry, smooth surfaces. Rough or dusty surfaces hold stickers badly. Tell us the surface and we will advise.

Can they go outside?

Some vinyl stickers are made for outdoor use. Tell us where yours will be and we will recommend the right material.

Do I need print-ready artwork?

No. We can set up your logo or design the sticker from scratch.

Can I get them on a roll or sheet?

Yes. Say how you plan to use them and we will supply them in the most practical form.

Can I have different designs in one order?

Yes. We can group several designs on the same run. Tell us what you have in mind.
