Design

Print design

Cards, flyers, brochures and letterheads, laid out properly. We arrange your words, images and logo on the page so the finished print is clear to read and looks like you.

Get a free quote01323 589 700

Illustrative mock-up. Real project photos to come.

A print job fails on the page long before it reaches the press. Text set too small, a logo stretched to fit, a phone number lost in the corner. We lay out print so the important things are easy to find: who you are, what you do and how to get in touch. We set it to the right size and format for the item, from an A5 flyer to a folded leaflet, then print it here if you want us to.

When you need it

  • You need business cards and have no design
  • A flyer has to go out and the layout is not done
  • Your brochure is full of text and needs structure
  • Your letterheads and cards do not match
  • You have words and photos but no layout
  • A menu or price list needs setting out

What you get

  • A layout that reads. Clear hierarchy, so the key details stand out.
  • The right size. Set up for the item, whether business card, A5 or DL.
  • Your brand applied. Logo, colours and fonts used consistently.
  • Print-ready files. Supplied to print with us or elsewhere.

How it works

  1. BriefTell us what the item is, who reads it and what it must say.
  2. Send the contentWords, photos and your logo, in whatever state they are in.
  3. First layoutThe designer sets out the page for you to look at.
  4. Your changesMark up what to move, fix or reword and we amend it.
  5. Final artworkThe approved layout is set up and supplied for printing.

Questions

Can't see yours? Ring 01323 589 700.

Can you write the text?

We lay out the words you give us. If you only have rough notes, we can help tidy them into something that fits the page.

Can you use my own photos?

Yes. We check they are good enough for print and tell you if one is not.

What sizes can you design for?

Standard sizes such as business cards, A5, A4 and DL, as well as folded and multi-page items. Ask if you have something unusual.

Will it match my signs and vehicle?

Yes. We use the same logo, colours and fonts, so print and signage look like one business.

Can I get it printed elsewhere?

Yes. We can supply the artwork as a file for another printer, though we would like to print it for you.

What if I just need small changes to an old design?

That counts as artwork work. Send us the file and tell us what to change.
