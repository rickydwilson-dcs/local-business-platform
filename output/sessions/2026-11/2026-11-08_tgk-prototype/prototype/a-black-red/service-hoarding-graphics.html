Signs and signage

Hoarding graphics

Large printed panels for the fence around a building site or shop-fit. They hide the work, carry your brand and tell people what is coming.

Get a free quote01323 589 700

Illustrative mock-up. Real project photos to come.

A hoarding is the temporary wall around a site. Printed graphics turn it from a plain fence into a billboard for the project. We print panels to fit the sections of your hoarding, usually on PVC or board. Typical content is the developer's name, an artist's impression of the finished building, contact details and any notices. Tell us the panel size and the surface they are going on.

When you need it

  • Covering the fence round a building project
  • Announcing a new shop behind a shop-fit
  • Showing the finished look of a development
  • Advertising a coming soon opening
  • Putting contractor and developer names on show
  • Hiding a site and tidying up the street

What you get

  • Panels to fit. Sized to your hoarding sections.
  • Print on PVC or board. Chosen to suit how it is fixed.
  • In-house design. Made from your plans, photos and logo.
  • Eyelets or fixings. Finished to match how it goes up.

How it works

  1. Measure the hoardingPanel sizes and the surface you are fixing to.
  2. Send your contentLogos, images, wording and any notices.
  3. Approve the artworkWe lay it out and you sign off.
  4. We print and finishPanels printed and prepared for fixing.
  5. Collect or arrange fittingChecked, then ready for the site.

Questions

Can't see yours? Ring 01323 589 700.

What do you print hoardings on?

Usually PVC or board, depending on how they will be fixed. Tell us about the hoarding and we will suggest one.

Can you use an artist's impression?

Yes. Send the image at the largest size you have and we will check it will print well.

Do I need consent for a hoarding?

Some sites do. Check with your council or the site owner.

Can you fit them?

Ask us about fitting when you get in touch.

Can you make a set of panels?

Yes. Panels can be printed to run together across the fence.

Can the graphics be changed later?

Yes. Single panels can be reprinted if details change.
