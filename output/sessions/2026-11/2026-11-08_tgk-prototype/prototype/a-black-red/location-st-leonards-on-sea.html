Working in St Leonards-on-Sea

St Leonards-on-Sea runs along the seafront next to Hastings, with Kings Road, London Road and the streets around Warrior Square full of independent shops, galleries, cafes and restaurants. Those businesses tend to care how things look, so we spend time on the design. Shop signs, window graphics, stickers for products, menus and print for events are the usual jobs. Local trades come to us for van lettering as well.
