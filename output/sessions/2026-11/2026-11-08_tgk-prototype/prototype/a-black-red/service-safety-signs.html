Signs and signage

Safety signs

Notices for the workplace, printed to your wording and layout. Fire, first aid, no entry, PPE and site rules, on rigid board or self-adhesive vinyl.

Safety signs are short instructions or warnings that people need to follow without stopping to think. We print yours on rigid board for fixing to walls and posts, or on self-adhesive vinyl for doors and glass. You tell us what each sign has to say and what symbol it uses. We lay it out clearly and make a matching set. Check your own risk assessment first so you order the signs your site actually needs.

When you need it

  • Marking fire exits and assembly points
  • Showing first aid and equipment locations
  • Warning of restricted or private areas
  • Reminding staff about PPE
  • Labelling machinery and storage areas
  • Replacing faded or missing notices

What you get

  • Matching sets. Signs for a site produced together, in one style.
  • Board or vinyl. Rigid for walls and posts, adhesive for doors and glass.
  • Your wording. Printed as supplied, set out clearly.
  • In-house design. Layout and colours worked out here.

How it works

  1. List the signsWhat each one says and where it goes.
  2. Check your requirementsConfirm the signs you need against your own risk assessment.
  3. Approve the artworkWording, symbols and colours signed off.
  4. We printSigns made as one set.
  5. Ready for fixingChecked and ready to collect.

Questions

Can't see yours? Ring 01323 589 700.

Are your signs compliant with safety regulations?

We print to the wording and symbols you provide. Choosing the right signs for your premises is down to you and your own assessment, so please check what you need.

Can you supply standard symbols?

Yes, we can use common safety symbols. Tell us which ones and we will include them.

Can you make signs for a whole site?

Yes. Send a list and where each goes and we will make them as a set.

Can I get them as stickers?

Yes. Self-adhesive vinyl suits doors, glass and smooth surfaces.

Can you replace signs to match existing ones?

Often, yes. Send a photo of the old sign.

Can you add our company name?

Yes, small and out of the way so the message stays clear.
