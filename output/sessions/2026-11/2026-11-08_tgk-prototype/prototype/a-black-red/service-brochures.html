Brochures

Folded or multi-page brochures that explain your services, show your work and tell people how to get in touch. Designed in-house and printed to standard sizes.

A brochure is for when a flyer is not enough. It can be a single sheet folded into panels, or a stapled booklet with several pages. Tradespeople use them to show a range of jobs, shops use them to show a product line, and clubs use them to explain what they offer. We lay out the text and images, set it in your brand colours and print to A4, A5 or DL. A good brochure has a clear order, short sections and your contact details on the back.

When you need it

  • You offer several services and need to explain them
  • You want to show photographs of past work
  • A product range needs a catalogue-style handout
  • You are pitching to a business or a committee
  • A venue needs to explain what it offers
  • A flyer cannot hold everything you need to say

What you get

  • Fold or booklet. Folded panels or a stapled multi-page booklet.
  • Layout design. Text and images arranged so they read in order.
  • Your photographs. We prepare your images for print.
  • Print-ready proof. Check every page before it prints.

How it works

  1. Plan the contentsList the sections, in the order you want them read.
  2. Gather materialText, photographs, logo and contact details.
  3. Choose the formatFolded sheet or stapled booklet, A4, A5 or DL.
  4. Review the proofGo through every page and panel.
  5. Print and finishPrinted, folded or stapled and trimmed.

Questions

Can't see yours? Ring 01323 589 700.

How many pages can a brochure have?

Folded brochures have panels, usually four to eight. Booklets have more pages in multiples of four. Tell us how much content you have.

Can you use my photographs?

Yes. We prepare them for print. If they are too small to print well, we will tell you.

Can you write the text?

We can tidy and shorten your text. If you need full copywriting, tell us what you have.

What sizes do you print?

A4, A5 and DL are the most common. We can discuss other sizes.

Is a booklet better than a folded brochure?

A folded brochure is simpler and easier to carry. A booklet suits more pages, such as a portfolio or catalogue.

Can I update it later?

Yes. We keep the layout so you can change a page and reprint.
