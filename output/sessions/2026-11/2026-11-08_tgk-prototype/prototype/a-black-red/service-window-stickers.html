Large format, window and wall

Window stickers

Smaller vinyl stickers for glass doors, windows and display cases, for when a full window dressing is more than you need. Opening hours, logos, payment notices and short messages, cut or printed to size.

Not every window needs a full dressing. A sticker on the door telling people when you open, or the cards you take, can do the job on its own. We make window stickers as printed shapes or as cut lettering, in the sizes you choose. They can be fitted to the inside of the glass so the front stays clean, or to the outside if you need to read them from the street.

When you need it

  • Showing opening hours on a shop door
  • Telling customers which payments you take
  • Marking an entrance, push or pull
  • Adding a logo to an office door
  • Advertising a single offer in the window
  • Labelling display cabinets or counters

What you get

  • Cut or printed. Plain lettering or full-colour designs.
  • Any shape. Rectangles, circles or cut around your logo.
  • Choice of side. Inside or outside the glass.
  • In-house design. Layout made here and checked with you.

How it works

  1. Say what it needs to showWording, logo and where it goes on the glass.
  2. Pick size and stylePrinted, cut or both, and how big.
  3. Check the proofWe draw it up and you approve it.
  4. Cut or printMade from vinyl and trimmed to shape.
  5. ApplyWe give clear instructions, or arrange fitting.

Questions

Can't see yours? Ring 01323 589 700.

Can I order just one?

Yes. A single sticker is fine, and so are matched sets for several doors.

Will it leave a mark when removed?

Vinyl for glass is designed to come off. Tell us the glass type and we will confirm.

Inside or outside?

Inside keeps it away from cleaning and wear. Outside suits glass you cannot read through.

Can you cut around my logo?

Yes. We can cut to the outline of the logo or shape.

Can I change the wording later?

The wording is fixed once made. If your hours change, we can make a new set.

Is there a minimum size?

Small text needs to stay readable. We will tell you if a design is too small to work.
