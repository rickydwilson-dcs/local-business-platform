Menus

Menus for cafes, pubs, restaurants and takeaways. Laid out so customers can find what they want, in a format that suits your venue and your tables.

Get a free quote01323 589 700

Illustrative mock-up. Real project photos to come.

A menu has to be read in poor light and in a hurry. We lay yours out with clear headings, readable type and enough space between items. Formats include single A4 sheets, folded A4 or A5, and DL takeaway menus. We match your venue's colours and logo, and set out prices exactly as you give them to us. When your menu changes, we update the file and reprint, so you do not have to start again.

When you need it

  • You are opening a cafe, pub or restaurant
  • Prices or dishes have changed
  • A seasonal or special menu is needed
  • A takeaway menu needs to go out to homes
  • The old menu looks tired or stained
  • Tables need a simple drinks or specials card

What you get

  • Clear layout. Headings, sections and spacing that make it easy to order.
  • Format options. Single sheet, folded A4 or A5, or DL takeaway.
  • Easy updates. Change a dish or price and reprint.
  • Matches your venue. Your logo, colours and tone.

How it works

  1. Send the menu textDishes, descriptions, prices and any notes.
  2. Choose the formatSingle sheet, folded or takeaway DL.
  3. See a proofWe lay it out in your style.
  4. Check every lineRead dishes, prices and allergy notices.
  5. Printed and trimmedReady to use on tables or in takeaway bags.

Questions

Can't see yours? Ring 01323 589 700.

Can you design a menu from scratch?

Yes. Send us the dishes and prices and tell us about the venue.

Can you update an existing menu?

Yes. Send the old file or a copy of it and your changes.

Do you print takeaway menus?

Yes. DL is a common size that fits in a bag or a letterbox.

Do you handle allergen information?

You give us the wording and we set it on the menu. You are responsible for its accuracy.

Can I have different menus for lunch and dinner?

Yes. We can match the layout across several menus.

Can I have a wipe-clean menu?

Ask us about suitable materials. We will say what is available.
