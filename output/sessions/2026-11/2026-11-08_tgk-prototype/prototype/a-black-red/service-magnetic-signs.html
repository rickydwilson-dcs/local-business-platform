Magnetic signs

Signs that stick on with a magnet and come off when you need them to. Useful for cars and vans that are not always on business duty, or for a short job.

A magnetic sign is a printed face bonded to flexible magnetic sheet. It holds to steel body panels, so it goes on a door or side panel and lifts off again with no adhesive. Put your name, number and a line on what you do on it, and fit it when you are working. It needs a flat steel panel to grip. It will not hold on aluminium or plastic panels.

When you need it

  • The car is private at weekends and business on weekdays
  • You need signs for a short contract or event
  • A hire or lease vehicle you cannot mark permanently
  • You share a vehicle between jobs or people
  • Trying a design before committing to vinyl
  • A second vehicle you only use occasionally

What you get

  • Printed face. Your name, logo and number printed to the size you choose.
  • Flexible magnetic sheet. Cut to size with rounded corners, so they are less likely to lift.
  • Sold in pairs. Order for both sides of the vehicle, or just one.
  • In-house design. We lay it out to suit the door or panel you are fitting it to.

How it works

  1. Measure the panelSend photos and the flat area of each door or side panel.
  2. Confirm it is steelA magnet test on the panel tells you if it will hold.
  3. Check the artworkWe show you the sign sized for that panel.
  4. Print and cutMade to size after you approve it.
  5. Collect or arrange deliveryWe tell you how to fit and look after it.

Questions

Can't see yours? Ring 01323 589 700.

Will a magnetic sign stay on at speed?

It should on a flat steel panel, fitted clean and flat. Remove it and wipe the panel and the back of the sign regularly, and take it off before an automatic car wash.

Will it mark my paintwork?

Dirt trapped behind a magnet can mark paint over time. Keep both surfaces clean and dry, and lift the sign off now and then.

Which panels can it go on?

Flat steel only. Aluminium, plastic and composite panels will not hold a magnet, and a heavily curved panel will not sit flat.

Do you do full wraps?

No. We do lettering, partial graphics, livery and magnetic signs only.

Can I use one set on two vehicles?

Yes, if the panel sizes suit both. Measure each vehicle first.

Is vinyl better than a magnet?

Vinyl stays put and suits a vehicle that is always on business. A magnet suits a vehicle that is not.
