Large format, window and wall

Wall graphics

Logos, murals and large images for feature walls in offices, shops, gyms and reception areas. Designed in-house and printed to fit the wall you have.

Get a free quote01323 589 700

Illustrative mock-up. Real project photos to come.

A plain wall behind a reception desk is wasted space. Wall graphics can be a cut logo, a large printed image or a repeating pattern. We print on vinyl made for walls and cut it to the size of the area. The finish of the paint matters, so tell us what is on the wall. Smooth, sealed paint works best. We will talk you through anything rougher before you commit.

When you need it

  • Putting a logo behind a reception desk
  • Dressing a feature wall in a shop
  • Adding a mural to a gym or studio
  • Setting out values or a timeline in the office
  • Branding a meeting room
  • Covering a plain wall in a waiting area

What you get

  • Cut or printed. A single-colour logo or a full-colour image.
  • Made to fit. Sized to your wall, door gaps and sockets.
  • In-house design. Layouts planned from your brand and photos.
  • Surface advice. We tell you what suits your paint and wall.

How it works

  1. Send wall photosInclude the height and width and any obstacles.
  2. Check the surfacePaint type and texture decide which vinyl suits.
  3. Approve the designWe mock it onto a photo of your wall.
  4. Print and cutMade in panels where the wall is large.
  5. Apply to the wallPanels lined up and fitted, or arranged with you.

Questions

Can't see yours? Ring 01323 589 700.

Will it damage the paint?

It depends on the paint. Tell us what is on the wall and we will say which vinyl suits.

Can it go on textured walls?

Smooth walls give the best result. Rough or textured surfaces may need a different approach, so ask first.

Can you do very large walls?

Yes. Large areas are made in panels and lined up on the wall.

Do you fit them?

Yes, we can arrange fitting. Send the address and any access details.

Can I use my own image?

Yes. It needs to be large enough to print at wall size. We will check it for you.

Can it be removed?

Wall vinyl can usually be removed, but results depend on the paint. We will explain this when you ask.
