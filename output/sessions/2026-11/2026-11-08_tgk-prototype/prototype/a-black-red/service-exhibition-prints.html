Exhibition prints

Printed graphics for trade shows, fairs and pop-up stands, made for anyone who has to look good in a crowded hall. Panels, backdrops and display prints made to fit your stand and carry your brand clearly.

Get a free quote01323 589 700

Illustrative mock-up. Real project photos to come.

An exhibition stand has seconds to get noticed. The print has to be large, clean and easy to read from the aisle. We print stand graphics for panels and backdrops, and for the framework you already own or hire. Give us the stand dimensions and we will size the artwork to fit. We can also print on foam board for lighter display panels, and pair prints with roller banners.

When you need it

  • Taking a stand at a trade show
  • Running a pop-up at a local fair
  • Replacing worn graphics on an existing stand
  • Adding a backdrop behind a counter
  • Launching a product at an event
  • Dressing a reception or showroom display

What you get

  • Fitted to your stand. Sized to the panels or framework you have.
  • Clear artwork. Layouts built to read from a distance.
  • Light panel options. Foam board for easy carrying and set-up.
  • In-house design. Artwork made here, matched to your brand.

How it works

  1. Send the stand detailsDimensions, panel layout or a photo of the stand.
  2. Share your brandLogo, colours, images and the key message.
  3. Review the layoutWe show the artwork on the stand plan for approval.
  4. Print the panelsPrinted to the exact sizes and checked panel by panel.
  5. Packed for the eventLabelled and ready for you to set up.

Questions

Can't see yours? Ring 01323 589 700.

Do you supply the stand frame?

We focus on the printed graphics. If you need a frame or system, ask and we will say what we can arrange.

Can you print to fit my existing stand?

Yes. Send us the panel sizes and we will print to match.

Can you design the stand artwork?

Yes. We lay it out from your brand guidelines or build the look with you.

What can the prints be mounted on?

Foam board is a common choice for light panels. Tell us your set-up and we will advise.

Can I add roller banners?

Yes. They suit a stand well and we make them as well.

How do I send artwork?

Email it to us. We will check it before printing and tell you if anything needs fixing.
