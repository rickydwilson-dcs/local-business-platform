Graphic design

Artwork for everything we print, and for anything you need printing elsewhere. Start from a rough sketch, an old logo or a blank page and finish with files that are ready to use.

Design is where most of our jobs begin. A sign, a van or a set of leaflets is only as good as the artwork behind it, so we do that part in-house. The four services below cover different stages. Logo design makes a mark from scratch. Brand identity builds the colours, type and rules around it. Print design lays out the cards, flyers and brochures. Artwork and prepress fixes and prepares files you already have.

When you need it

  • Starting a business and you have no logo yet
  • Your current logo looks dated or is a poor scan
  • A job needs artwork and you only have an idea
  • Your materials all look different from each other
  • You have a design that needs fixing before it prints
  • You are about to order signs, vans or print

What you get

  • In-house designers. The people who design it sit next to the people who print it.
  • Artwork made to fit. Sized and set up for the sign, vehicle or print it is for.
  • Your changes included. You see the work and tell us what to change before it is final.
  • Files you own to use. Final artwork supplied so you can use it again later.

How it works

  1. Tell us what you needShare the business, the audience and where the design will be used.
  2. We draw up ideasThe designer sketches and builds the first direction.
  3. You review itSay what works and what doesn't, and we adjust.
  4. We refineFine detail, colour and layout are tightened up.
  5. Final files suppliedYou get the finished artwork in the formats you need.

Questions

Can't see yours? Ring 01323 589 700.

Which design service do I need?

If you have no logo, start with logo design. If you have a logo but nothing consistent around it, look at brand identity. For a specific item such as a flyer, choose print design. If you already have artwork, choose artwork and prepress.

Do I have to print with you?

No. Design is a service on its own, and we can supply files for use elsewhere. Most customers do print with us because the artwork is made for the job.

Can you work from my sketch?

Yes. A pencil drawing, a photo of an old sign or a list of words is enough to start from.

Who owns the finished artwork?

Please confirm this with us when you brief the job, so the terms are clear before work starts.
