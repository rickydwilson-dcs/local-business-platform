Design

Logo design

A logo that is simple enough to read on a van door and still looks right on a business card. Designed in-house for new businesses and for ones that have outgrown their first mark.

Get a free quote01323 589 700

Illustrative mock-up. Real project photos to come.

A good logo has to work small and large, in colour and in one colour, cut from vinyl and printed on paper. We design with all of that in mind from the first sketch. We talk to you about the business, who you want to reach and what you like and dislike, then draw it up. The finished logo comes as vector artwork, so it can be scaled to a badge or a shopfront without going soft.

When you need it

  • You are starting out and have no logo
  • Your logo is a low-quality image that blurs when enlarged
  • The old logo no longer suits the business
  • You are merging or renaming the business
  • Your logo does not work in one colour
  • You want something that looks right on a vehicle

What you get

  • An original mark. Drawn for your business, not picked from a template.
  • Vector artwork. Scales to any size without losing sharpness.
  • Colour and one-colour versions. So it still works for vinyl, stamps and single-colour print.
  • Files for each use. Supplied in the formats printers and sign makers ask for.

How it works

  1. The briefWe ask about the business, your customers and any marks you like.
  2. ConceptsThe designer draws up ideas and shows you the directions.
  3. Your feedbackPick what works and tell us what to change.
  4. RevisionsWe rework the chosen idea until it is right.
  5. Final filesThe finished logo is supplied in colour, one-colour and vector formats.

Questions

Can't see yours? Ring 01323 589 700.

What do you need to get started?

A short description of the business, who it is for and any logos you like or dislike. A sketch or an existing logo helps if you have one.

Can you redraw my old logo?

Yes. If you have a blurry or pixelated version, we can rebuild it as clean vector artwork, or take the chance to improve it.

Will it work on a vehicle or sign?

That is how we design it. We check the mark at small and large sizes and in one colour, because that is where weak logos fall apart.

Can I choose the colours?

Yes. Bring your preferences and we will advise on what reads well on a vehicle, a sign and paper.

What files will I get?

Vector artwork plus versions for print and screen. Ask us about any particular format when you brief the job.

Can you carry the logo onto signs and print?

Yes. Once the logo is done we can use it across vehicle graphics, signs, stationery and anything else we make.
