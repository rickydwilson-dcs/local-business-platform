Letterheads

A4 letterheads with your logo and contact details at the top or foot of the page. Quotes, invoices and letters that look like they come from one business.

Illustrative mock-up. Real project photos to come.

A letterhead is the paper your business writes on. A4 is the standard size, with your logo and contact details at the top or foot and a clear space for the letter. You can print on them from an office printer, write on them by hand, or send them as a template for quotes and invoices. We design a clean layout that sits beside your business cards and folders, and print them as single sheets or in a pad.

When you need it

  • Quotes and invoices need to look professional
  • You send letters to customers or suppliers
  • The company name or address has changed
  • A set of print needs a matching letterhead
  • You want a template for emailing documents as PDFs
  • Your current paper looks home-made

What you get

  • A4 layout. A clear header or footer with space for the body.
  • Digital template. A PDF version you can use in Word or by email.
  • Matched stationery. A letterhead that sits with your cards and folders.
  • Design included. A clean layout from your logo and details.

How it works

  1. Send logo and detailsBusiness name, address, phone, email, web address.
  2. Choose the placementTop, foot or side, depending on how you use the page.
  3. See a proofWe show you the layout with a sample letter.
  4. Approve itCheck addresses, numbers and spacing.
  5. Printed or supplied digitallyPrinted sheets, plus a PDF template if you want one.

Questions

What size are your letterheads?

A4, the standard letter size. Other sizes on request.

Can I get a digital version?

Yes. We can supply a PDF template you can use on screen and print yourself.

Can you add a company registration number?

Yes. Give us the exact wording for the footer and we will set it.

Can they match my business cards?

Yes. We design them as a set so the logo and colours match.

Can I print on them from an office printer?

Many people do. Tell us which printer you use so we choose a suitable stock.

Can I reorder?

Yes. We keep your approved artwork and reprint when you need more.
