Car graphics

Door lettering, logos and window graphics for company cars, sales cars and taxis. Subtle enough to look smart, clear enough that people can read who you are and how to reach you.

Get a free quote01323 589 700

No full wraps. Lettering and partial graphics only.

Illustrative mock-up. Real project photos to come.

A car has less flat space than a van, so car graphics are about choosing the right spots. Doors, the rear window, the tailgate and the bonnet edge take most of it. We cut vinyl lettering and logos to suit the shape, and can use perforated window film so the view out of the rear glass stays usable. We do lettering and partial graphics. We do not do full wraps.

When you need it

  • A company car for a sales rep or surveyor
  • Taxis and private hire cars that need a name and number
  • Driving instructors, carers and home visitors
  • A personal car now used part-time for work
  • Franchise cars that must carry set branding
  • Replacing door lettering after a respray or repair

What you get

  • Door lettering. Name, logo and contact details sized for the door panel.
  • Window graphics. Rear and side glass lettering, including perforated film where it suits.
  • In-house design. We lay it out on your car shape and adjust until it sits right.
  • UV-rated vinyl. Material chosen for use on a vehicle outdoors.

How it works

  1. Send the car detailsMake, model and a photo of the doors, rear and bonnet.
  2. Choose the positionsWe suggest where lettering reads best on that body shape.
  3. Review the layoutYou see the artwork placed on the car outline.
  4. Sign offCutting begins once you have approved the proof.
  5. Fit and finishApplied to clean panels, then checked for lifting and alignment.

Questions

Can't see yours? Ring 01323 589 700.

Will car graphics damage the paint?

Vinyl fitted to sound paintwork should come off again, but we cannot promise the result on a respray or damaged paint. Tell us if the car has been repainted.

Can people still see out of the rear window?

With perforated window film, yes, though the view is reduced. We can talk through what suits your car.

Do you do full wraps?

No. We do lettering, logos and partial graphics only.

Can I take it off when I sell the car?

Vinyl lettering can be removed. Ask us about removal when you book.

Can you do a magnetic version instead?

Yes. Magnetic door signs suit cars you use for work only some of the time.

Can you match my van?

Yes. Send photos of the van and we will scale the design to suit the car.
