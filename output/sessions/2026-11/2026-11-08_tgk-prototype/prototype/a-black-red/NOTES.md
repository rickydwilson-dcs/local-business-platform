# Direction A: Black and red (round 2)

## Concept

- Press-shop job sheet: black ground, off-white "paper" block, dashed ticket rules. Images carry the page; words are a headline and a line per section.
- Hero: copy left, van mock-up bleeding off the right edge behind a red edge rule. Services: 7 image cards plus an eighth "No full wraps" tile (also carries the Clothing Kings cross-link) in one 4x2 grid that fills exactly one viewport.
- Red is rationed: CTA fills, card numerals, rules, the one full red quote band. Never body text.
- Mock-ups are labelled "Illustrative mock-ups. Real project photos to come." at the hero and services head.

## Tokens

--tgk-black #160E0B, --tgk-surface #241915, --tgk-line #5A4B44, --tgk-red #DD201B, --tgk-paper #F4EFE9, --tgk-white #FFFFFF, --tgk-muted #B5A9A2

## Fonts

Archivo (variable width + weight): condensed 70% heavy display, 100% body. No tabular figures or mono.

## Contrast (WCAG)

- paper/black 16.67; white/black 19.06; muted/black 8.32; muted/surface 7.48; black/paper 16.67; white/red 4.89 (buttons, quote band)
- BELOW 4.5: red on black 3.90 (large numerals 24px+ bold, rules, borders only); red on paper 4.28 (not used as text); black on red 3.90 (not a text pairing)
- line #5A4B44 on black 2.29: decorative dividers only
