Signs and signage

A-boards

A pavement sign you carry out in the morning and bring in at night. We make A-boards for shops, cafes, pubs and salons, with printed or changeable faces.

Get a free quote01323 589 700

Illustrative mock-up. Real project photos to come.

An A-board is a simple way to stop people walking past. It stands on the pavement, folds flat and goes inside at closing time. We supply the frame and the printed panels. Some businesses want a fixed message like opening hours and a logo. Others want a face they can change, such as a menu or a daily offer. Tell us which and we will set it up that way.

When you need it

  • Catching foot traffic outside a shop
  • Showing today's menu or special
  • Pointing to a first-floor or back entrance
  • Promoting an event or an opening
  • Marking a salon, gym or studio
  • Replacing a damaged board face

What you get

  • Frame and panels. A folding frame with faces that suit your message.
  • Fixed or changeable. Printed faces, or a surface you can update yourself.
  • In-house design. Artwork made here, to your brand.
  • Spare faces. Extra printed panels so you can swap the message.

How it works

  1. Choose a stylePrinted faces, or a board you write on.
  2. Send your wordingLogo, text and any offers to show.
  3. Approve the artworkWe lay it out and you sign it off.
  4. We print and assembleFaces made and fitted to the frame.
  5. Collect itChecked, then ready for pick up.

Questions

Can't see yours? Ring 01323 589 700.

What size are your A-boards?

Tell us where it will stand and how much room you have. We can suggest a size from there.

Can I change the message myself?

Yes, if you choose a changeable face. A printed face stays as it is, so order spares if you plan to swap it.

Will it blow over?

Any pavement sign can catch the wind. Ask us about the frame options and how to weigh it down.

Do I need permission to put one on the pavement?

Possibly. Rules on pavement signs are set locally, so check with your council before you put one out.

Can you print on both sides?

Yes. Both faces can carry the same or different artwork.

Can you replace the face on my existing frame?

Often, yes. Bring the frame or send its measurements.
