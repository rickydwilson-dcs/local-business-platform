Banners

Mesh banners

Perforated banners for fences, scaffolding and open ground. The holes let wind through, so they suit spots where a solid sheet would be pulled about.

Get a free quote01323 589 700

Illustrative mock-up. Real project photos to come.

Mesh banners are printed on a perforated vinyl. The image still reads clearly from a distance, but air passes through the holes, which takes strain off the fixings. That makes mesh the usual choice for scaffold sheeting, site hoarding, temporary site fencing and exposed fields. We print to size, finish the edges and add eyelets or reinforced fixings for tying on. Print is slightly less sharp up close than solid PVC.

When you need it

  • Branding a scaffold during building work
  • Site fencing that needs a company name
  • Developers advertising a new scheme
  • Outdoor events in open or exposed spots
  • Sports grounds and perimeter fences
  • Coverings where a solid sheet would catch the wind

What you get

  • Perforated print. Image stays readable while air passes through.
  • UV-rated material. Chosen for banners left outside in daylight.
  • Finished for fixing. Edges and eyelets suited to fences and scaffold.
  • In-house design. Layout and artwork done here.

Questions

Why choose mesh over PVC?

Mesh lets wind pass through, so it puts less strain on fixings. It suits fences, scaffolding and open ground.

Does mesh look as sharp as PVC?

Fine detail is a little softer because of the perforations, so we keep the design bold. Large lettering and logos work best.

Can I fit it to scaffolding?

Yes, mesh banners are commonly tied to scaffold and fencing. We finish them with eyelets for that.

Can you design it for me?

Yes, in-house. We can work from your logo and brand colours.

Can I use one on site hoarding?

Yes. Give us the panel sizes and we will print to fit.

What size can you do?

Custom sizes. Tell us the dimensions and we will confirm whether it can be one piece.
