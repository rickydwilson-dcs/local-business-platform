Large format print

Big prints for glass, walls, floors and display stands, for shops, offices and events. Posters, exhibition graphics and fitted vinyl, designed in-house and printed on UV-rated materials.

Large format means anything too big for an office printer. The products in this group differ by where they go. Posters and exhibition prints are printed sheets for display boards and stands. Window graphics and window stickers go on glass. Wall graphics go on painted walls. Floor graphics go on the ground and need a different material. Tell us the surface and the job, and we will point you at the right one.

When you need it

  • Dressing a shop window for a new opening
  • Printing posters for a promotion or event
  • Getting graphics ready for an exhibition stand
  • Putting your brand on a blank office wall

What you get

  • In-house design. Artwork made here, to your brand or from scratch.
  • UV-rated materials. Chosen to suit the surface and where it will be used.
  • Made to size. Cut to your window, wall, board or stand.
  • A proof first. Nothing goes to production until you approve it.

  1. Tell us the surfaceGlass, wall, floor or display board, and roughly how big.

Questions

Can't see yours? Ring 01323 589 700.

How do I choose between the options?

Start with the surface. Glass takes window graphics or stickers, walls take wall graphics, floors take floor graphics, and boards or stands take posters or exhibition prints.

Can you measure up?

Send photos and dimensions first. If the job needs it, we can talk through a site visit.

Can you design the artwork?

Yes. Our in-house team works from your brand guidelines or helps you build the look.

Do you print on a specific size?

We make to the size you need, within what the material and printer allow. Tell us the dimensions and we will confirm.
