Roller banners

Freestanding pull-up banners for events, shops and exhibitions. The print pulls up from a compact base and the stand packs away when you are done.

Get a free quote01323 589 700

Illustrative mock-up. Real project photos to come.

A roller banner is a printed sheet that winds into a cartridge base. You pull it up, hook it to a pole and it stands on its own, usually beside a stand, a till or a door. When the day is over it winds back in and goes into its bag. They are made for moving between venues, so keep the design simple: a headline, a logo and one way to get in touch. They are intended for indoor use.

When you need it

  • Exhibitions and trade shows
  • Beside a till or in a shop window
  • Conferences, talks and training days
  • Reception areas and waiting rooms
  • Pop-up shops and temporary spaces
  • Open days and networking events

What you get

  • Full-colour print. Printed to fit the stand, with clear lettering.
  • A stand to suit. A pull-up base and pole for the print.
  • Reusable display. Packs down to carry or store between events.
  • In-house design. Layout made for a tall, narrow space.

How it works

  1. Choose the standPick the size and base that suits your use.
  2. Plan the layoutA short headline, your logo and contact details.
  3. Approve the proofCheck the artwork at the stand's real proportions.
  4. Print and fitPrinted and fitted into the cartridge base.
  5. Test and hand overPulled up and checked before you take it.

Questions

Can't see yours? Ring 01323 589 700.

What size are roller banners?

They are made in set stand sizes. Tell us how much space you have and we will recommend one.

Can I change the graphic later?

Often, yes. Many stands let you swap the print for a new one without buying a new base. Ask us about yours.

Can I use one outdoors?

They are designed for indoor use. For outside, PVC or mesh is usually a better choice.

Can you design it for me?

Yes. We design for a tall, narrow format and keep it readable from a distance.

Does it come with a bag?

Ask us when you request a quote and we will confirm what is included.

What files should I send?

Your logo, ideally as vector artwork, plus any images and the wording you want.
