Banners

PVC banners

Full-colour PVC banners for fences, railings, shopfronts and events. Designed in-house, printed on UV-rated material and finished with eyelets, ready to hang where people will see them.

Get a free quote01323 589 700

Illustrative mock-up. Real project photos to come.

PVC is the standard banner material: a flexible, solid vinyl sheet that takes bold colour and large lettering well. We print to the size you need, hem the edges and add eyelets so it can be tied or cable-tied to a fence, wall or frame. It suits a single clear message, such as an opening, an offer or a sponsor. In very windy spots, ask about mesh instead.

When you need it

  • A sale or special offer on the shopfront
  • A grand opening or new-branch announcement
  • Property for sale or to let
  • Car boot sales and outdoor markets
  • Club and event sponsor boards
  • Seasonal promotions on a fence or wall

What you get

  • Full-colour print. Logos, photos and large lettering reproduced clearly.
  • UV-rated material. Chosen for banners that hang in daylight.
  • Finished edges. Hemmed with eyelets, ready to hang.
  • A proof first. You approve the layout before we print.

How it works

  1. Send the sizeGive us the dimensions and where it will hang.
  2. Supply wording or artworkSend your logo or ask us to design it.
  3. Approve the proofCheck spelling, colours and layout.
  4. Print and finishPrinted, hemmed and fitted with eyelets.
  5. Collect or arrange deliveryChecked before it leaves us.

Questions

Can't see yours? Ring 01323 589 700.

What sizes can you print?

Custom sizes, within the width of our printer. Tell us what you need and we will say if it can be done in one piece.

Can I put a PVC banner outside?

Yes, PVC banners are made for outdoor use, and we use UV-rated material. Exposed or windy spots are better suited to mesh.

Can you add a photo or logo?

Yes. Send the files, preferably as vector artwork or high-resolution images.

Can the eyelets go where I want?

Usually, yes. We can place them at the corners, along the edges, or at set intervals.

Do you do double-sided banners?

Ask us. Tell us what you need and we will confirm what we can do.

Can you reprint an old banner?

Yes. Send a photo of the old one or the original file and we will rebuild it.
