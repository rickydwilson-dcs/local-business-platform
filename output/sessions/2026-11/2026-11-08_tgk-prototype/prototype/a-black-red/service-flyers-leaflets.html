Flyers and leaflets

One-page flyers and folded leaflets for promotions, openings and announcements. Printed in A4, A5 or DL, designed in-house, and ready to hand out or post.

A flyer says one thing on one sheet: an offer, an opening, a service. A leaflet folds, so it has room for a little more detail, like opening hours, services and a map. A5 is the usual size for handing out and posting through doors. DL, the long narrow shape that fits an envelope, suits leaflets that sit in a rack. A4 works for noticeboards and counter displays. We design them and print them double-sided if you wish.

When you need it

  • You are opening and want people to know
  • A seasonal offer needs getting out there
  • You are handing out information at an event
  • A service needs explaining to customers
  • You want something to put through doors in your area
  • A noticeboard or shop counter needs a poster-style sheet

What you get

  • Sizes to suit. A4, A5 or DL, flat or folded.
  • Design included. A layout with a clear heading and one next step.
  • One side or two. Single sided for simple offers, double sided for detail.
  • Proof before print. You check every word.

How it works

  1. Say what it is forOne promotion, one message, one audience.
  2. Choose a sizeA5 to hand out, DL for racks, A4 for notice boards.
  3. Send wording and imagesOr we help you write and find them.
  4. Approve the layoutWe send a proof for you to check.

Questions

Can't see yours? Ring 01323 589 700.

What is the difference between a flyer and a leaflet?

A flyer is a single sheet. A leaflet is folded and has more panels for information.

Which size should I pick?

A5 is the easiest to hand out or post. DL fits racks and envelopes. A4 suits noticeboards.

Can you write the text?

We can tidy and shorten what you give us. For fuller copywriting, tell us what you need.

Can I print on both sides?

Yes. Many flyers carry the offer on the front and details or a map on the back.

Can you help with delivery to doors?

We print the flyers. Ask us whether we can help with distribution.

Can I update the design for next time?

Yes. We keep your artwork so you can change the offer and reprint.
