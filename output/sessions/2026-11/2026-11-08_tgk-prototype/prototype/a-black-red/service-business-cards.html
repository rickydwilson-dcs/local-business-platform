Business cards

Business cards in the standard 85x55mm size, with your name, role and contact details laid out so people can find them quickly and keep them.

Get a free quote01323 589 700

Illustrative mock-up. Real project photos to come.

A business card is still the quickest way to leave your number with someone who has just met you. The standard size is 85x55mm, which fits a wallet and a card holder. Most cards carry a name, a role, a phone number, an email and a web address, and a logo on one or both sides. We design the layout, print it and trim it. If you have an old card you like, we can rebuild it rather than start again.

When you need it

  • You have just started trading and need something to hand out
  • Your details have changed and the old cards are wrong
  • A new member of staff needs their own cards
  • You go to trade shows or networking events
  • You leave cards in shops or on noticeboards
  • The old design looks dated

What you get

  • Standard 85x55mm size. Fits wallets and card holders.
  • Two sides if you want. Details on the front, a logo, map or offer on the back.
  • Design included. A clean layout from your logo and details.
  • Matching stationery. Cards that sit well beside your letterhead and folders.

How it works

  1. Send your detailsName, role, phone, email, address and web address.
  2. Send your logoOr tell us you do not have one and ask for design help.
  3. See a proofWe show you the layout with your details in place.
  4. Check and approveRead every number and letter before it prints.
  5. Printed and trimmedCut to size and ready to collect.

Questions

Can't see yours? Ring 01323 589 700.

What size are your business cards?

The standard 85x55mm. We can print a different size on request.

Can you use my existing design?

Yes, if the file is good enough. If not, we can rebuild it.

Do I need a logo?

No. We can design a simple one or set your name in clean type. See our logo design service.

Can I have a different name on each card?

Yes. Give us the list and we set up each version.

Can the back be printed too?

Yes. Many people use the back for a map, a list of services or a social handle.

Can I reorder later?

Yes. We keep your approved artwork on file, so a repeat run is quick.
