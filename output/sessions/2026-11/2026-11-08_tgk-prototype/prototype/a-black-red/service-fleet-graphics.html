Vehicle graphics

Fleet graphics

One look across every vehicle you run. We design a livery once, then apply it to each van, car or truck, so a fleet of three or thirty looks like one business.

Get a free quote01323 589 700

No full wraps. Lettering and partial graphics only.

Illustrative mock-up. Real project photos to come.

Fleet work starts with a master design: logo position, colours, type size and contact details, set out on a template for each vehicle type you run. After that, every new vehicle gets the same treatment, fitted in the same places. Livery is the word for that standard look. We handle lettering, partial graphics and livery, and we can update the whole fleet when you rebrand. We do not do full wraps.

When you need it

  • You run three or more vehicles that should match
  • A new contract needs branded vehicles on the road
  • You are adding vehicles to an existing livery
  • A rebrand means every vehicle has to change
  • Mixed vehicle types, from small vans to larger trucks
  • Hire or lease returns that need graphics taken off

What you get

  • A master livery. One agreed design, scaled for each vehicle type.
  • Repeat orders. We keep your artwork on file, so adding a vehicle is simple.
  • Consistent fitting. Same positions and spacing on every vehicle.
  • In-house design. Livery drawn and adjusted here, not sent out.

How it works

  1. List the vehiclesTell us the makes, models and how many.
  2. Build the liveryWe draw one master design and fit it to each vehicle template.
  3. Approve the setYou sign off the livery for every vehicle type in one go.
  4. Roll it outWe make and fit the vehicles in the order that suits your diary.
  5. Keep it on fileArtwork is stored so later vehicles match the first.

Questions

Can't see yours? Ring 01323 589 700.

What is livery?

The standard look you put on every vehicle: colours, logo, lettering and where each goes. It is what makes separate vehicles read as one fleet.

How many vehicles count as a fleet?

There is no fixed number. If you want more than one vehicle to match, fleet graphics is the right page.

Can you update old fleet graphics?

Yes. We can remove worn lettering and apply new, or add to what is there. Photos of the vehicles help.

Can you match livery someone else did?

Yes. Send photos or the artwork and we will match new vehicles to the existing ones.

Can you do vehicles in stages?

Yes. We keep the artwork on file so you can add vehicles as you buy them.

Do you do full wraps?

No. We do lettering, partial graphics and livery. We do not do full vehicle wraps.
