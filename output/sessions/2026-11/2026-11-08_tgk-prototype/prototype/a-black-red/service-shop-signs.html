Signs and signage

Shop signs

The sign over your door is how new customers find you. We design and make fascia, projecting and flat-panel shop signs from rigid, UV-rated materials.

Get a free quote01323 589 700

Illustrative mock-up. Real project photos to come.

A shop sign has to be read from across the road, so we keep the artwork bold and the wording short. Fascia signs run along the front of the building. Projecting signs stick out over the pavement so they can be seen from along the street. We make signs on rigid board with printed or cut vinyl faces. Send us a photo of the front of the building and the space you have.

When you need it

  • Opening a new shop or unit
  • Replacing a faded or damaged fascia
  • Rebranding after a change of name
  • Adding a projecting sign for passing trade
  • Matching signs across more than one branch
  • Updating a sign after a change of owner

What you get

  • Fascia and projecting. Flat or hanging signs sized to your frontage.
  • In-house design. Artwork made here, to your brand or from scratch.
  • UV-rated materials. Chosen to cope with sun on a south-facing front.
  • A proof first. You see the layout before it is made.

How it works

  1. Send a photoThe shopfront and the space the sign will fill.
  2. Measure upWe confirm sizes and what the sign is fixed to.
  3. Approve the layoutWording, colours and logo agreed on a proof.
  4. We make itBoard prepared and graphics applied here.
  5. Ready to fitChecked and ready for fitting.

Questions

Can't see yours? Ring 01323 589 700.

Can you work from a photo of the shop?

Yes, as a starting point. We will confirm measurements before anything is made.

Do you need planning permission?

Some shop signs do. Check with your local council before ordering, especially in conservation areas or for illuminated signs.

Do you make illuminated signs?

Ask us. Tell us what you have in mind and we will say what we can do.

Can you match our logo exactly?

Yes. Send the logo file and any brand colours and we will match them.

Can you replace just the face of an old sign?

Often, yes. Send a photo of the existing sign and its frame.

Do you do window lettering too?

Yes. It works well alongside a fascia sign.
