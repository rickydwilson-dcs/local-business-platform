03How it goes

The process

01 02 03 04

Step one — no prep needed

A conversation.

A phone call, or a few messages if you'd rather. Tell me what you do and who you do it for. That's the whole brief. No forms, no document to fill in on your evening off.

Step two — I do the thinking

I write it and design it.

Copy, structure, images, colours, the lot. You don't have to know what your homepage should say — working that out is the job you're paying for.

Step three — your turn

You review it.

You see it before anyone else does. Unlimited revisions during the build, until it's right. "Can the green be less green" is a perfectly good note.

Step four — and it stays that way

It goes live, and I keep it running.

Hosting, updates, security, uptime monitoring and support from then on. If something needs changing next March, you message me and it changes.

So — what do you actually do, and who rings you? Domestic rewires, fuse boards, EV chargers. Mostly Eastbourne and Hailsham. Perfect. That's everything I need to start.
