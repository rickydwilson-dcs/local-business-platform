01The arrangement

You barely lift a finger.
That is not a slogan, it is the split.

Running the business is the job. The website is not. So the work is arranged so that almost all of it sits on my side of the line, and the little that has to be yours is a conversation rather than a homework assignment.

Your side — three things

What you do

  1. 01Have one conversation, by phone or by message. There is nothing to prepare.
  2. 02Answer a handful of questions about how the business actually works.
  3. 03Say yes, or say change it. As many times as it takes, at no extra cost.

No brief. No copy deck. No image library. No CMS login, no dashboard, no technical knowledge required at any point — before launch or after it.

05The process

Four steps. The first one
is a conversation.

01

A conversation

A call, or a few messages. No brief needed, nothing to fill in, nothing to prepare beforehand. I ask, you talk, I take it from there.

02

We write it and design it

Copy, structure, imagery, colour — all handled. You see a finished thing, not a wireframe and a request for content.

03

You review

Unlimited revisions during the build, until you are happy with it. Not three rounds, not a change-request form. Until you are happy.

04

It goes live, and we keep it running

Hosting, updates, monitoring and support from that day on. The relationship carries on; it is a managed service, not a hand-off.

My name is Ricky Wilson. I started Digital Consulting Services in 2019 and I have designed and built every site that carries its name since.

Nothing is outsourced and nothing is offshored. There is no account manager between us and no ticket queue to join. When you ring the number at the top of this page, I answer it — and when your site needs something at half past eight on a Tuesday, I am the one who does it.
