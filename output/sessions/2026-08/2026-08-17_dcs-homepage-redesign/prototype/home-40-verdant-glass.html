How it goes

Four steps. Four things you never do.

The four steps — you are in one of them

  1. Step 01

    A conversation

    A call or a few messages. No brief needed, nothing to fill in.

  2. Step 02

    We write it and design it

    Copy, structure, imagery, colour — all handled.

  3. Step 03

    You review

    Unlimited revisions during the build until you're happy.

  4. Step 04

    It goes live, and we keep it running

    Hosting, updates, monitoring, support. This is the part that does not end.

And four things you never have to do

  1. 01

    Write a brief

    Most studios want you to arrive with one. There is nothing to fill in here — it comes out of a conversation.

  2. 02

    Supply the words

    Copy is part of the job. You will not be sent a blank document and asked what you'd like it to say.

  3. 03

    Learn a system

    No CMS, no login, no dashboard. If something needs changing, you send a message and it changes.

  4. 04

    Chase anyone

    Hosting, security, updates and monitoring are handled. One person is accountable and you have his number.
