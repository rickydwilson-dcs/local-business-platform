Work section · three options

The card grid is the odd one out.

Every other section of the page is a full-bleed colour wall carrying display type. The client work is the one place built from bordered white boxes with a heading tab on top, which is why it reads as a different design. All three options below drop the box and speak the page's own language. Same four clients, same videos, same palette, same family. Each is shown live — resize the window or open on a phone to see both breakpoints.

Option A

Pill reel

The mechanic you pointed at. Pills name the whole set so you know there are four without scrolling; selecting one hard-cuts the entire ground to that client's colour, so the section becomes another colour wall rather than an island. The active pill is also the timer.

Argues: the set stays one object, and the section earns its place in the colour rhythm — ink, magenta, navy, aqua as you move through the four. Trade: a passive scroller who never taps sees one client, not four.

Option B

Full-bleed stack

No new interaction to learn. Each client becomes one more sticky full-viewport panel, identical in behaviour to the six service cards further down the page — except the video is the ground rather than a thumbnail inside a box. The most congruent of the three by definition: it is the page's existing move, applied to different content.

Argues: one motion vocabulary for the whole page. Trade: four more full screens of length, and the work no longer reads as a set — you meet the clients one at a time with no sense of how many there are.

Option C

Index & reveal

The agency index. All four names sit as display type in one list, so the set is legible at a glance and costs a fraction of a screen; the video belongs to whichever row is active. On desktop the well is pinned beside the list and swaps; on a phone the list is the whole interface and the well sits under it. This revives the .row treatment already sitting unused in the r9 stylesheet.

Argues: the shortest of the three, and the only one where all four names are readable without any interaction. Trade: the video is smaller than in A or B, so the work itself is less of the picture.
