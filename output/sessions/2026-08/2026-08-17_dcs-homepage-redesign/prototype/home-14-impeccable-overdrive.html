Four steps, and three of them are mine.

Start to live is usually two to four weeks, depending on how big it is and how quickly you come back to me.

1

A conversation

A call, or a few messages if you're on site. No brief needed, no forms to fill in. I ask the questions, you just answer them the way you'd explain it to a customer.

2

I write it and design it

Copy, structure, images, colours: all handled here. You don't have to work out what to say about your own business. That's the bit I'm good at.

3

You review it

I show you the real thing, not a mock-up. Unlimited revisions during the build until you're happy with it. Tell me it's wrong and I'll change it.

4

It goes live, and stays live

Hosting, updates, monitoring and support from then on. If something breaks at 8pm on a Sunday it's my problem, not yours.
