How it goes

Four steps, and three of them are mine.

1

A conversation

A call, or a few messages if you'd rather. There's no brief to write and no form to fill in — I ask the questions, you just answer them, and I take it from there.

2

I write it and design it

Copy, structure, images, colours, the lot. You'll see it when there is something worth looking at, not a fortnight of half-finished pages.

3

You review it

Unlimited revisions while the site is being built. We keep going until you are happy with it. That isn't a sales line, it's how the price is worked out.

4

It goes live, and stays looked after

Hosting, updates, monitoring and support from then on. Nothing to log into, nothing to learn, and someone to ring if it needs changing.
