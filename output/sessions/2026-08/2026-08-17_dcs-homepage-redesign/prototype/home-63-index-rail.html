[04]// how it goesfour steps

It starts with a conversation, not a contract.

01

A conversation

A call, or a few messages if that suits you better. Nothing to prepare, nothing to fill in. I ask what you sell, who buys it and what people always ring up to ask.

02

I write it and design it

Copy, structure, imagery, colour, the lot. You do not need to know what to say — that is the part I am actually being paid for.

03

You review

Unlimited revisions while it is being built. Say "the blue is wrong" and the blue changes. There is no change-request form.

04

It goes live, and I keep it running

Hosting, security updates, monitoring, backups and support from then on. Everything documented, nothing locked in — it is your site and your domain.
