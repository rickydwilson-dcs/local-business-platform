How it works Four steps · about four weeks, typically

It starts with a conversation, not a brief.

  • Step 1

    A conversation

    A call, or a few messages if that suits you better. Nothing to prepare, nothing to fill in, no brief to write. I ask the questions; you already know the answers.

  • Step 2

    I write it and design it

    Copy, structure, imagery and colour, all handled. You do not need to know what to say about your own business — that is the part I am being paid for.

  • Step 3

    You review

    You see it properly, on your own phone. Unlimited revisions while it is being built — we keep going until it is right, not until the hours run out.

  • Step 4

    It goes live, and stays looked after

    Hosting, updates, monitoring and support from then on. No dashboard to learn, no login to lose — you ring me, and it gets done.
