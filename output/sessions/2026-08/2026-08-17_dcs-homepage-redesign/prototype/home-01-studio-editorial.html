01 The premise

You will barely lift a finger.

Most agencies want a brief, your copy, your photographs and a clear idea of what you want before they will start. That is fine if you have a marketing department. You have a van, a full diary and a phone that rings while you are up a ladder.

So the work runs the other way round here. I ask the right questions, write the words, make the design decisions, build the thing, put it live and keep it running. You get a professional website without having to learn anything, manage anything, or ever log in to it.

  1. i

    One person, start to finish

    You are hiring me, not a company. Nothing is outsourced and nothing is offshored. The person you speak to on day one is the person who builds it.

  2. ii

    Agency-quality output

    Proper design, writing that sounds like you rather than a brochure, and a site that loads fast and stays secure.

  3. iii

    A conversation, not a brief

    A call or a handful of messages. Everything I need comes out of that — there is no document for you to write.

  4. iv

    The words are handled

    I write the copy. You never have to sit down and work out what to say about your own trade.

  5. v

    Nothing technical, ever

    No system to learn, no login, no dashboard. Something needs changing? Send me a message and it is changed.

  6. vi

    One price, all in

    A setup fee and a low monthly. Hosting, SSL, security updates, uptime monitoring and support all sit inside it.

  7. vii

    It does not end at launch

    This is a managed service, not a hand-off. I am still here in year three, and the site is still being looked after.

03 Services

Six things I do, and do properly.

Most clients take the first three together and never think about it again. The rest are there when the business needs them.

  1. 01

    Website Design

    Bespoke websites built for local tradespeople. No templates, no DIY, nothing you have to assemble yourself.

  2. 02

    Local SEO

    Service pages, location pages, Schema markup and Google ranking — built in from the start, not sold back to you later.

  3. 03

    Ongoing Management

    I look after your site so you can focus on your trade. Updates, backups, monitoring and changes on request.

  4. 04

  5. 05

  6. 06

04 How it runs

Four steps. Three of them are mine.

  1. 01

    A conversation

    A call, or a few messages if that suits you better. No brief needed, no forms to fill in, nothing to prepare.

  2. 02

    I write it and design it

    Copy, structure, images, colours, the lot. You do not have to decide what a homepage should say.

  3. 03

    You review

    Unlimited revisions during the build. We keep going until it is genuinely right, not until a quota runs out.

  4. 04

    It goes live, and stays live

    Hosting, updates, monitoring and support from launch onwards. Nothing to renew, nothing to remember.
