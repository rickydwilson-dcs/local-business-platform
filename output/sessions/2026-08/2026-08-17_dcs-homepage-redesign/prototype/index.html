HOW TO REVIEW

Judge the direction, not the details

These are prototypes, not the finished site. Wording, section order and spacing all get fixed in round 2. What matters now is whether the feeling is right — would a plumber trust it, and does it look like it came from somewhere expensive?

WHAT'S REAL

The content is accurate

Prices, tiers, client names, outcomes and testimonials all come straight from the live business. There are no stock photos anywhere — every visual is drawn in code, which is why they load instantly and will translate cleanly into the React build.

WHAT'S NEXT

Shortlist, then round 2

Pick two or three. Round 2 takes those further — real motion work, the inner pages, and a proper mobile pass — and only then does the winner get rebuilt as the actual site.
