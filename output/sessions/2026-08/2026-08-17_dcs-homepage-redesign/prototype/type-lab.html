Typography — body and headings

Two labs on one page. Part A changes the prose and holds the headings at Schibsted. Part B changes the headings and holds the prose at Inconsolata 600, so you can see what each display face does against your front-runner. Poppins is dropped from both.

On the serif question: you were right. A serif fails as body copy on a page like this and comes into its own at display size, where the thin strokes read as refinement instead of weakness. Three are in Part B — Instrument Serif, Bodoni Moda, Fraunces — alongside five non-serifs, so it is not a serif-only question.

The heading metric to watch is “widest line”. The hero is three lines with a hard-set break, and the longest of them is PROFESSIONAL. Anything much over +5% will not fit the hero at its current size and would force the display type down a step.
