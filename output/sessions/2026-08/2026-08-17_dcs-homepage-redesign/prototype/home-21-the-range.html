● The studio

There is one
person here.

Which is the point. It is also the reason the range above exists — twenty-odd businesses, and I have sat with every single one of them.

My name is Ricky Wilson. I started Digital Consulting Services in Polegate in 2019.

I don’t have account managers, a sales team, or a queue of freelancers you’ll never meet. You talk to me, and the person you talk to is the person who does the work. When something breaks at half nine on a Tuesday, you don’t raise a ticket — you text me.

What I’ve learned across a fabric shop, a yoga studio, a garage and a scaffolding firm is that the differences are mostly on the surface. Underneath, every owner wants the same three things: to be found, to look credible, and never to have to think about the website again. That’s what I sell, and it’s the same product whatever line you’re in.
