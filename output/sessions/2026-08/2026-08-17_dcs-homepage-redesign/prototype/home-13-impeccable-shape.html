04How it worksp.12

Four steps. You are only needed for two of them.

1

A conversation

A call or a few messages. No brief needed, no forms to fill in. I ask what you do, where you work and who you want ringing you.

2

I write it and design it

Copy, structure, images, colours — all handled. You don't need to know what to say, because working that out is my job.

3

You review

Unlimited revisions during the build until you're happy. Say it plainly. "That photo's wrong", "we don't do that anymore", and it changes.

4

It goes live, and I keep it running

Hosting, updates, monitoring, support. There is no login for you to learn and no backend for you to touch, ever.
