03How it works Four steps · almost none of them yours
1

We talk

A call, or a few messages. No brief to write, nothing to fill in. It starts with a conversation, not a contract.

2

You decide

We write the copy, choose the structure, the imagery and the colour, and put it in front of you. You say yes, or you say change it.

3

I build it

Unlimited revisions while it's being built, until you're happy. Everything documented, nothing locked in.

4

You own it

It goes live, and the hosting, security, monitoring and support carry on from there. That's what the monthly is for.
