10Notes to the figures

Which numbers are measured, and which aren’t.

Anyone can draw a rising line. Here’s exactly what’s behind each one on this page, so you can decide how much weight to give it.

  1. 1Colossus Scaffolding enquiry chart. Measured: the first Google enquiry arrived within three weeks of launch, on a 30-page site. Illustrative: the weekly enquiry counts, drawn to show the shape a working site produces. Not their traffic data.
  2. 2Effort split by step. Illustrative. It describes how the job is divided between us, not a measured statistic. The point it makes is real: the only step that needs much of your time is the first conversation.
  3. 3Client trend panels. Illustrative shapes, not per-client measurements. The line printed against each client name in the register is the measured claim, and it’s the only claim being made.
  4. 4Google position slope. Illustrative. Measured: every site is built with service pages, location pages and Schema markup, and submitted to Google Search Console. Nobody can guarantee a Google position, and I won’t pretend otherwise.
