Accent colour — indigo vs navy

The Offer and Selected Work modules, six times. Exactly one value changes between them: --plum, the tertiary. Magenta and cyan are untouched, so what you are judging is only how the third colour behaves next to them. No JS, so nothing is hidden behind a scroll reveal, and the section images are suppressed to keep the comparisons close together.

The number that decides it is “vs ink ground”. Selected Work is a checkerboard of coloured tiles on near-black. As the navy gets darker its contrast with magenta actually improves — 2.18 with the old indigo, 3.44 at the darkest — so that is not the constraint. What breaks is the tile against the panel behind it: 1.69 with the old indigo, 1.33 with the navy now chosen, and by option 5 it is 1.07, where 1.00 would be invisible. Scroll to option 5's grid and the Mad Graphics and Bexhill tiles have effectively gone.

Judge it on the Work grid, not the Offer cards — the Offer sits on paper, where every one of these works.
