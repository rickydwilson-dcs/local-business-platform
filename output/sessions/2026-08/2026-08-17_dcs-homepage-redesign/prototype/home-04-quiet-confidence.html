04How it worksFour steps

Four steps, and you're only really needed for two.

From first message to live site is usually three to five weeks, depending on how quickly you come back to me. There's no lengthy onboarding and no forms to fill in.

  1. Step 01

    A conversation

    A call, or a few messages if you'd rather. I ask about your trade, your area, and the jobs you actually want more of. No brief needed.

  2. Step 02

    I write it and design it

    Copy, structure, images, colours — all handled from that conversation. You don't have to supply anything you don't have.

  3. Step 03

    You review it

    You look at the real thing, not a slideshow. Unlimited revisions during the build until you're genuinely happy with it.

  4. Step 04

    It goes live, and stays live

    Hosting, updates, monitoring and support from then on. When you want something changed, you message me.
