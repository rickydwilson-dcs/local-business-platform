01 — The promise

You don’t need a brief. You don’t need to know what to say.

Most studios expect you to arrive with copy, images and a clear idea of what you want. If you had all that, you’d probably have a website already. So we start from a conversation instead.

  1. 01

    You’re hiring a person

    Not a company, not an account manager, not an offshore team. The person you speak to is the person who designs and builds it.

  2. 02

    The content is handled

    The words get written for you, drawn out of a conversation about how the business actually works.

  3. 03

    Nothing technical to learn

    No CMS, no login, no dashboard. You want something changed, you say so and it’s changed.

  4. 04

    It stays looked after

    Hosting, security, updates, monitoring and support are part of the monthly, not an extra invoice.

04 — How it goes

Four steps, and three of them are mine.

01

A conversation

A call, or a few messages if that’s easier. No brief to write, nothing to fill in. I ask about the business, who buys from you and what usually goes wrong. That is the brief.

02

I write it and design it

Copy, structure, imagery, colour, the lot. You see a real page, not a wireframe and not a mood board.

03

You review

Unlimited revisions during the build, until you’re happy. Say “I don’t like the blue” and that is a perfectly good piece of feedback.

04

It goes live, and stays looked after

Hosting, SSL, domain management, security updates, uptime monitoring and support. You have my number.
