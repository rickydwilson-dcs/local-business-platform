02 / The deal

You barely
lift a finger.
I do the rest.

Most agencies expect you to turn up with a brief, your copy, your images and a clear idea of what you want. If you had all that, you wouldn't need an agency. So I don't ask for it.

We have a conversation. I ask the right questions, write the words, make the design calls, build it, host it, and keep it running. You go back to work.

  1. 01

    You're hiring a person, not a company

    Ricky does the work. Nothing outsourced, nothing offshored, no account manager in the middle.

  2. 02

    Content handled

    I write the copy. You don't need to know what to say about your own business — that's the job.

  3. 03

    No technical knowledge required

    No CMS, no login, no dashboard to learn. Need a change? Send a message and it's done.

  4. 04

    All-in pricing

    Setup plus a low monthly covers hosting, security, updates and support. No surprise invoices.

  5. 05

    It's a relationship, not a hand-off

    The site doesn't get dumped on you at launch. It's looked after for as long as you want it looked after.

04 / How it goes

Four steps.
Three of them are mine.

  1. 01

    A conversation

    A call, or a few messages if you're on site. No brief needed, no forms to fill in. I'll ask the questions.

  2. 02

    I write and design it

    Copy, structure, images, colours — all handled. You get something to react to, not a blank page to fill.

  3. 03

    You review

    Unlimited revisions during the build. It isn't finished until you'd happily put it on the side of your van.

  4. 04

    Live, and looked after

    It goes live and stays that way. Hosting, updates, security, monitoring and support, month after month.
