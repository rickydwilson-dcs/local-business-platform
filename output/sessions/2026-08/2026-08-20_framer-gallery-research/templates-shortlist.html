You flagged this one. An agent had reported its demo dead — that was wrong, it only tried the guessed URL. It loads fine, and it is the strongest fit in the sweep for what you asked for.

Boldness

bolddesign.framer.website
Free No count-ups Poster

Real identity is "Boldness — Brand & Web Design by Remy Cole". Near-black ground, chartreuse / indigo / bone accents, heavy condensed poster grotesk. It is the only shortlist-grade template in the entire sweep that is clean on your count-up rule by construction — a DOM scan for numeric leaf nodes returns nothing but 01 02 03 04, the process numerals. No stat band, no percentages, no counter anywhere.

  • The animated imagery — image cards at four or five sizes drifting at different scroll rates over giant ghosted display type, and interleaved in z so some pass in front of the headline and some behind. That z-interleave is what creates the poster depth; a flat parallax grid doesn't read the same way.
  • Process steps — each step is a two-tile stack: a tall tile carrying only the numeral at ~200px, and a second tile beneath with the name and copy. The four assemble diagonally as you scroll, each offset down-right of the last, until they sit in a row.
  • Service slabs — service name set enormous inside a rounded chartreuse block, with project imagery abutting its edges.
  • Statement type — full-viewport lines like "NOBODY REMEMBERS POLITE DESIGN" in bone on black, with image cards passing through the letterforms.

For a local business audience a named person often builds more trust than a "we", but it is a decision, not a detail.

What to do instead

Kanso · $99

A horizontal strip — 15+ Years / 140+ Projects / 97% — separated by slashes, drifting continuously. Motion, but every figure is authored DOM text, so it is always correct.

Fuel · free

The corpus itself

Health warning

These demos are in poor condition, and it is one design decision repeated — opacity: 0 until observed — taken without asking what happens when the observer never fires.

Blank on arrival

Mondragon ($59) renders entirely blank on the page a buyer lands on — 4,035 characters in the DOM, nothing painted. Bungee ($49) the same over 11,214px. Pearl, Elian, Flexio, Fathom, SwissFolio all show a coloured void where the hero should be.

Preloaders that never finish

Fabrica ($129), Mōno ($99) and Asake sit on splash screens for 16+ seconds with no content ever appearing. Mira ($129) uses a percentage count-up as the preloader. Creatiq's blur-reveal never resolves and scrolling stops working.

Motion ahead of the visitor

Torque® ($129) puts a full-screen "welcome, scroll to explore" gate between you and the homepage. Raw Studio fires an interstitial modal before you've read anything. Nora Vale remaps vertical scroll to a horizontal filmstrip. Rolen's hero simply doesn't scroll.

Motion that damages legibility

Agenciy, Platform® and Nyro use a per-character scroll-linked colour fill that never completes — at rest the tail of every headline stays ghosted (Virtual Reality Encounte). Collins' rotated card stack is only legible mid-transition.

The test to apply to anything we build: screenshot it with JavaScript disabled. That is what a visitor on a bad train connection gets. Content renders at full opacity by default; motion animates transform from a visible state, gated behind prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference, with the un-animated state being the complete one.

Scope

What this didn't cover

  • ~60 Featured templates were never touched. The Featured shelf holds 99+ agency templates and is Trending-ordered, so those are the ones Framer itself promotes. My partition sent agents down the alphabetical tail instead. One strong candidate (Mattis®) came from there by chance. This is the obvious next pass — the slugs are listed in findings-tpl-batch-e.md.
  • Mobile was not assessed at all this round. Desktop only. Agents were barred from resizing because it corrupts concurrent screenshots and doesn't work anyway — the screenshot tool pins a fixed viewport override.
  • Demo URLs are unreliable. Framer serves across three hostnames (.framer.website, .framer.media, .framer.ai) and about a third of slugs don't match their demo. Several resolve to squatted or unrelated sites. Verified URL tables for two batches are in the findings files.
  • One template was wrongly reported dead — Bold Design, the one you asked about. Treat any "Site Not Found" in these files as unverified unless the agent checked the embedded previewUrl.
  • 85 of 159 demos opened. Roughly 20 slugs resolved but went unopened on budget, and a handful (optique, fox-x, james, hrzns) could not be resolved at all.
  • No hover-state or mobile-menu pass, and no interior pages — homepages only.
