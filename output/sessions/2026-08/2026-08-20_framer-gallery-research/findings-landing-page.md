Coverage: ~110 tiles skimmed, 9 opened live, 6 shortlisted.

## Shortlist

### 1. Rareform — https://rareformhealth.co

Best pricing card in the segment: a dark gradient "head" block carrying name/price/CTA, a hard seam,
then a cream feature body. Tiers stack via "Everything in X, plus:". Also a step-accordion process
section and a floating stat chip anchored to a real photo.

### 2. Milo — https://milopet.com

Most transferable to a local service business: split pill+circle nav CTA, a persistent "we'll call
you" pill, a **contact-_channels_ row list instead of a form**, and a tinted-row "what's included"
panel.

### 3. Billow — https://www.billow.so

Closest to the default-studio reference. The **audience tab-switcher** (Freelancers / Studios /
Agencies …) is the component DCS most obviously needs. FAQ accordion with a tinted square plus-button,
and honest "Soon / On Roadmap" chips.

### 4. Numenu — https://numenu.xyz

The flat counter-proposal: segmented tab nav, heading → colour-panel-with-real-screenshot → caption
cards, a checkbox comparison table, square-cornered two-tone pricing. **No radius, no shadow, still
reads expensive.**

### 5. Trowel Craft — https://trowelcraft.com.au

An actual Melbourne plaster-repair firm. Contact form with a **photo-upload dropzone** ("send us a
picture of the job"), chamfered-corner panel, split nav with centred hexagon badge, deliberately
staggered service cards, pinned swipe carousel.

### 6. Lumenda — https://lumenda.de

**Duotone photo inside a numbered process card** — the highest-leverage fix for clients whose
photography you don't control. Stat _rows_ rather than columns. Case study is a construction firm.

## Flags raised

- The whole segment leans on scroll-reveal gating that leaves pages blank if it fails.
- Lumenda's stat count-ups visibly double-render mid-animation — the same false-figure trap already
  recorded in project memory as `feedback_animated_counters_show_false_figures`.
