The Challenge

The team at Colossus were excellent at their trade. The problem was perception. Colossus Scaffolding had a solid reputation built entirely through word of mouth — no website at all, not even a basic holding page. When a commercial client — a developer, a construction firm, a property manager — searches for a scaffolding contractor, they're making a judgement call based on what they find online. No website signals the opposite of established and insured, regardless of how good the actual work is.

Beyond winning commercial contracts, there was the long-term opportunity of organic search traffic. Dozens of people in East Sussex search for scaffolding services every month, and Colossus weren't appearing for any of them — free enquiries going to competitors every single day.

The Solution

We built Colossus a 30-page website on the DCS platform, using the Vega theme in navy blue — professional, clean, and well-suited to a trade contractor audience. The structure was planned to serve two goals at once: look credible to potential clients, and rank in Google for the searches that matter.

On the service side: dedicated pages for residential, commercial, new build, hire and event scaffolding, each explaining the process before a client has even made contact. On the location side: pages for every main town and area across East Sussex where Colossus works — Eastbourne, Hastings, Bexhill, Lewes, Seaford, Hailsham and more — each targeting the local search terms for that area. That's the architecture that makes local SEO work: search engines can see clearly who you are, what you do, and exactly where you do it.

We also set up Google Business Profile, added proper Schema.org markup for a local contractor, and submitted the site to Google Search Console on launch day.

The Results

Within three weeks, Colossus received their first enquiry via the website contact form — a residential customer in Eastbourne who had found them through Google. Not a referral, not a repeat customer. A cold lead, from the internet, for the first time.

Within a few months the site was appearing in Google Maps results for scaffolding searches across East Sussex. Colossus now has the professional presence needed to pursue larger commercial work — when a developer or property manager looks them up, they find a well-presented site that clearly explains capability, service area and how to get in touch.
