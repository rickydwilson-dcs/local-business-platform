File

How

  • Drag on the image to draw a redaction box.
  • Click a dashed suggestion to accept it.
  • Click × on a green box to remove it.
  • Boxes are what gets filled. Cover the whole plate, plus a couple of pixels.

Keys

  • → / Space next   ← prev
  • n no plate here
  • a accept all suggestions
  • u undo last box
  • e export
