Lot 01

“BRG 214”, the ex-Winterbourne car, off the road from 1979 and rebuilt to its 1954 delivery specification.

1954 Jaguar XK120 Fixed Head Coupé

Coachwork by Jaguar Cars Ltd, Browns Lane · Restored at Berwick, March 2023 to June 2024

The car arrived on a trailer with its bonnet in the boot and forty-five years of standing water in both sills. What follows is the whole of it: every intervention with a date against it, everything we kept, everything we could not, and who did which part.

Note on the engine. The unit fitted is not the one recorded on the Heritage Certificate. It was substituted in 1971 by a previous owner and we have not replaced it. We say so here rather than in a footnote.

Originality, itemised

Retained

  • Original chassis frame, straight and unrepaired — checked on a jig, Bexhill
  • Original bulkhead and scuttle
  • Original doors, bonnet and boot lid, all matched to the shell
  • Original data plate and body number tag
  • The 1954 paint mixing card, found taped inside the spare-wheel well

Replaced, and why

  • Both sills — new sections wheeled here from flat sheet — the originals had no sound metal left to weld to
  • Both floors — corroded through at the seat mounts
  • Offside rear wing, lower third — stress-hardened by a 1980s repair

Renewed

  • All brightwork re-plated to the original pattern — per factory drawings held by the marque club
  • Screen and door rubbers, new old stock where obtainable

The workshop

Berwick, East Sussex

DPM Autobody is a restoration and paint shop at Berwick, in East Sussex. It has one booth, one wheeling machine and a small number of people who have worked together a long time.

Everything a car needs is made and finished here, with two exceptions: engines are built elsewhere and trim is cut and stitched elsewhere. Both are subcontracted to people we name on the lot they belong to, because a claim you cannot check is not worth making.

What we are actually known for is paint. Some of the cars that leave this shop wear another restorer's name on the badge; that arrangement is theirs to disclose rather than ours, and it is why the paint sections above are the longest part of every file.

Cars are taken on one or two at a time. There is no waiting list to join, only a conversation to have.

  • Panel fabrication and repair sections
