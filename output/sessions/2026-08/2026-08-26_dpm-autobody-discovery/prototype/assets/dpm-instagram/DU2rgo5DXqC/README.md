### The tension worth noticing

Seven of fourteen are engine, underside or suspension — precisely the "more paint, less mechanics"
axis says to cut. But these are **concours documentation**, and a concours car is judged underneath.
So the underside shots are not filler: they are the proof that the restoration is real rather than
cosmetic. The resolution is placement, not deletion — paint and finished car lead, the mechanical and
underside evidence sits inside the lot record where it does its work.
