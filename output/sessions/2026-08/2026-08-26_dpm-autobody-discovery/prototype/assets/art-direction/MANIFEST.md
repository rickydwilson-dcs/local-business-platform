## Why these exist

The asset audit found DPM has **no photograph of a finished car in daylight away from the workshop**,
and everything reachable is capped at 1080px. These plates stand in for the commission, and double as
the visual half of the photography brief — they are easier to hand a photographer than a paragraph.

## Art direction these encode — Ricky's three axes, 2026-08-27

- **More hands, less faces.** Every craft plate is shot at the hands. No face appears anywhere.
- **More paint, less mechanics.** Not one engine, gearbox or suspension component in the set. Every
  process image is a paint-process image.
- **More finished items, less process.** Four of fourteen are finished cars in real light — against a
  real library that is ~80% shells and primer.
