02 Lot 02  ·  Finished and delivered

Bentley S3
Continental

A Continental gives you the longest unbroken panels we ever paint, and near-black gives you nowhere to put a mistake. There is a chrome waistrail that runs the whole length of the car, and either it is straight along every inch of the door and the wing or it is not.

Look at the shut line as the light crosses it. The gap is even top to bottom, and the reflection carries across it without stepping. All of that is settled in bare metal, long before there is any colour to look at.

A second S3 is on the jig now.

03 Lot 03  ·  In the booth

Jaguar
Aston Martin Sea Green

The owner wanted a colour from another marque entirely. Aston Martin Sea Green goes down looking almost grey under the booth lamps, and only turns green when it is dry and the light starts moving across it.

Mixed here, sprayed here, judged here, under the same lamps every time. The strip light overhead runs as one unbroken highlight from the front wing to the door, and that unbroken run is the only proof there is that a panel underneath is straight.

This car is still in our hands, so its record stops at the paint. We would rather show you the surface as it is than wait until there is a finished photograph to show you.

04 The workshop

What we do,
and what goes out

Bare-metal restoration and concours paint on classic and performance cars, in one building in Berwick, East Sussex. Panel work, lead loading, filler, primer, colour, flatting and polishing — all of it by the same hands, on the same floor.

A restoration here starts with the car photographed exactly as it arrived, and ends with a plaque riveted into the engine bay carrying its chassis number. Between those two things sits a file that the owner keeps: every panel, every gap, every decision, photographed as it happened.

Two things leave the building. We would rather list them than let “full in-house restoration” quietly cover them.

  • Panel work, fabrication, weldingIn house
  • Lead loading and metal finishingIn house
  • Primer, block work, guide coatsIn house
  • Colour mixing and sprayingIn house
  • Flatting, cutting and polishingIn house
  • Photographic record of the buildIn house
  • Engine buildingOut of house
  • Trim and upholsteryOut of house
