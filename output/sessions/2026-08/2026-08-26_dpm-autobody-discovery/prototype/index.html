DPM Autobody · Berwick, East Sussex · Prototypes, August 2026

Two versions, and a type study

The design is Direction D, “The Register”. It now exists twice, built from one source: a client version with nothing on it but the site, and a working version that adds the notes — what is real, what is a first draft, and what still needs David’s sign-off.

Both are generated by node build.mjs from src/. Edit the source, run the build, and the two stay in step. Never edit client/ or annotated/ by hand.

    The type study

    Does this site want a serif headline?

    Eleven headline faces and six text faces against real copy at the exact sizes the prototype uses, on both near-black and paper. The section that decides it is the one where DPM’s own mark and the headline share a frame — the mark is a wide geometric sans, and not every serif survives sitting under it. Picking a pairing prints the two CSS lines to change.

    The single-file version, with AI art direction standing in for Lots 02 and 03. Superseded on 29 August when the real Bentley and Jaguar replaced it. Kept so the two can be compared; project-p1800.html is its project page.

    Superseded · do not edit

About the photography. Every photograph on the client and working versions is DPM Autobody’s own, of DPM Autobody’s own work. The P1800 frames come from their Instagram; the Bentley plates are three crops of the 4032×3024 photograph on their own site; the Jaguar plates are frames from their own film, lens-corrected. The AI art-direction plates that stood in for the second and third cars survive only in directions A, B, C and the superseded D.

Assets are not in git. output/.gitignore denies binaries across sessions/** by design. Rebuild them with assets/dpm-work/make-plates.zsh; the DPM mark rebuilds with tools/trace-logo.mjs. A fresh clone gets working HTML and 404ing images until those two run.

Nothing here has been published. No R2 upload, no Vercel deploy. David has not seen any of it.

Research, positioning and the full argument are in the session folder — start with synthesis.md.
