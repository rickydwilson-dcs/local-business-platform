## What this corpus actually is — read this before using any of it

**Every one of these is a repair or modification customer. Not one is a restoration client.**

| Reviewer         | Date    | The job, as described          | Register     |
| ---------------- | ------- | ------------------------------ | ------------ |

| Craig Mayhew     | 2016-10 | Unstated, repeat trade         | **Quality**  |
| Daryl Taylor     | 2016-10 | Vauxhall VXR, short notice     | Service      |

Two things follow, and both matter to the site:

1. **The newest of these is six years old (September 2020).** The whole visible review corpus
   predates the concours restoration work the site is being built around. It is evidence about the
   bodyshop DPM used to be.
2. **The recurring praise is speed, service and value for money** — "quick turn around", "short
   notice", "went out of his way". That is the register of a good local bodyshop and it is the
   opposite of what a concours restoration buyer is looking for, which is patience and obsession.
   Quoting the service-led ones on this site would actively work against the positioning.

**The two worth using** are the ones that talk about _standard_ rather than speed:

- **Liam Hunt** — "to a standard better than out of the factory". This is the strongest line in the
  whole corpus, because it is a customer independently saying what DPM say about themselves:
  _"All lines and gaps refined far beyond the standard they left the factory. When we restore cars
  we aren't just returning them to a factory standard we are improving all the areas that are
  substandard in our eyes."_ (DPM's own caption, 2026-07-08.)
- **Craig Mayhew** — "I'm very fussy and they are spot on." Fussiness is exactly the right register.

Sam Ciftci's _"the paint finish is fantastic looks like it was meant to be that way out of factory"_
is a good line about paint specifically, and paint is the differentiator — but it is about an
Integra bumper moulding, and trimming the quote to hide that would be shading the truth. Left off
the page; noted here in case David wants it.
