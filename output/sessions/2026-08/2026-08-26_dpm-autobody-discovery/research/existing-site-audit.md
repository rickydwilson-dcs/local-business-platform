## Assessment

**Keep:** the "Artists of Automotive Restoration" line; the documented-restoration promise; the
workshop-in-the-countryside story; named craftsmen.

**Fix:**

1. Positioning — concours restoration first, everything else subordinate or dropped.
2. No real URL structure — everything is anchors, so there is nothing for search to rank and nothing
   to link to. Needs real routes per service and per project.
3. **"Our Work" is the whole business and it is under-served.** For a concours shop the portfolio _is_
   the sales argument. This is where the Instagram library and the new video should land.
4. Insurance/accident repair dilutes the high-end message. Options: keep it as a quiet secondary
   page, or drop it. **Ask David** — it may be real revenue he does not want to lose.
