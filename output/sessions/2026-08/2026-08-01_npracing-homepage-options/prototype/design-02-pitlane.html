01 — The team

Run by Neil Pearson, built by the paddock.

NP Racing is a private British Superbike team based in Taunton, Somerset, led by Neil Pearson — founder of NP Motorcycles. Since 2004 the business has grown from a motorcycle servicing workshop into a recognised independent name in the BSB paddock, with its first full Superbike-class season arriving in 2020.

Without the budget of factory-backed outfits like Honda Racing UK, Ducati PBM or McAMS Yamaha, the team has earned its reputation the hard way.
