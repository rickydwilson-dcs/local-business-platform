The team

Run by Neil Pearson. Built by the paddock.

NP Racing is a private British Superbike team based in Taunton, Somerset, led by Neil Pearson — founder of NP Motorcycles. Since 2004 the business has grown from a motorcycle servicing workshop into a recognised independent name in the BSB paddock.

Without the budget of factory-backed outfits like Honda Racing UK, Ducati PBM or McAMS Yamaha, NP Racing has earned its reputation the hard way — developing riders, running professionally prepared machinery, and staying close-knit.
