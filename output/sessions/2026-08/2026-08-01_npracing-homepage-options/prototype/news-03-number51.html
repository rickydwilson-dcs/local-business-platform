9 JUN BritishSuperbike.com

Elliott set for Superbike return at Knockhill with NP Racing

Brayden Elliott rejoined the ZYN British Superbike grid with NP Racing from round three at Knockhill (19–21 June 2026), after a season in the Pirelli National Superstock class and a test with the team at Donington Park.

"I'm very excited to be back on a Superbike. Competing in the premier category is always the goal and one that I've been working towards."— Brayden Elliott
"We are excited to be returning to the BSB grid at Knockhill with Brayden. He has already settled in to the team following our recent test."— Neil Pearson, NP Racing

26 FEB BritishSuperbike.com

NP Racing sign Superbike rookie Thomson for 2026 campaign

Connor Thomson, 23, from Surrey, signed with NP Racing for his full-time Superbike debut in the 2026 ZYN British Superbike Championship, having impressed on a wildcard for the team at the 2024 Brands Hatch finale.

"Getting to be on the Superbike grid for my debut season in BSB is a moment I've worked towards for so many years."— Connor Thomson
"When Connor wildcarded for the team in 2024, he showed grit and determination... I am pleased to welcome him back."— Neil Pearson, NP Racing
