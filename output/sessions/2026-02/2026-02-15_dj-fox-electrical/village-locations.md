## Content Structure

Each location page follows the consistent template with:

### Frontmatter (YAML)

- `title` - SEO title with "Electrician in [Village]" format
- `seoTitle` - Alternative title for meta tags
- `description` - 150-160 character meta description emphasizing proximity and response time
- `keywords` - 4 location-specific keyword phrases
- `coordinates` - [latitude, longitude] for map integration
- `region` - "East Sussex"
- `isCounty` - false (villages, not county-level)
- `hero` - Hero section with title, description, image placeholder, CTA
- `breadcrumbs` - Navigation breadcrumbs (Home > Locations > [Village])
- `faqs` - 5 service-specific FAQ pairs addressing common concerns

### Content Body

1. **Introduction section** - Positions D J Fox as local expert, emphasizes proximity and response times
2. **Services list** - 8 electrical services offered (consistent across all locations):
   - 24/7 Emergency Electrical Callout
   - EICR Testing & Certification
   - Consumer Unit Upgrades
   - Full House Rewiring
   - Electrical Fault Finding
   - Socket & Lighting Installation
   - EV Charger Installation
   - PAT Testing

3. **Why Choose Us section** - 6 key differentiators:
   - Very/Extremely close proximity (emphasized based on distance)
   - NICEIC Approved
   - 15+ Years Professional Experience
   - 24/7 Emergency Service
   - Transparent Pricing
   - All Work Certified

4. **Emergency Electrician section** - Specific emergency services and commitment to 24/7 response

5. **Landlord EICR section** - Regulatory requirements and compliance messaging

6. **Contact section** - Clear CTA and service area confirmation
