## Content Writing Guidelines

### Voice & Tone

- **Professional but approachable** - expert but not condescending
- **Local and personable** - "we serve Eastbourne and surrounding East Sussex areas"
- **Safety-focused** - emphasize qualifications, compliance, safety
- **Transparent** - honest about costs, timeframes, what's involved

### Trust Signals to Include

- NICEIC/NAPIT registration numbers
- Years serving East Sussex area
- Number of jobs completed
- Certifications (Part P, 18th Edition, specific manufacturer approvals)
- Insurance coverage (public liability, professional indemnity)
- Customer review snippets (with permission)
- Before/after photos from local jobs

### Call-to-Actions (Vary by Post)

- "Get your free, no-obligation quote today"
- "Book your EICR inspection online"
- "Call us 24/7 for emergency electrical work"
- "Download our electrical safety checklist"
- "See our customer reviews"
- "Check our service coverage areas"

### Legal/Compliance Notes

- Always mention Part P compliance for notifiable work
- Include certification/testing offer for all installations
- State qualifications (NICEIC, 18th Edition, etc.)
- Disclaimers on costs ("prices are indicative, final quote depends on specific requirements")
- Safety warnings where appropriate ("never attempt DIY electrical work")
